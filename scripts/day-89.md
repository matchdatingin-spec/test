# Day 89 — IELTS Academic Writing Task 1 Malayalam: Describe Any Graph or Chart

**Target length:** 17–19 minutes
**Level:** B2 *(exam preparation)*
**One-line promise:** A reliable four-paragraph structure for every chart type — and the rule that Task 1 forbids your opinion entirely.

**⚠️ SCOPE — state this on camera in the first 30 seconds:** This lesson covers **IELTS Academic** Task 1 only. ✅ **General Training** Task 1 is a completely different task — you write a **letter**, not a chart description. ⛔ Do not let a General Training candidate follow this formula. ⭐

**📌 Accuracy note:** The four-paragraph structure below is a **teaching scaffold**, not an official requirement. ✅ IELTS assesses Task Achievement, Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource and Grammatical Range and Accuracy — it does **not** mandate a paragraph count. Present it as a dependable default, not a rule. ⭐

*(Encouraging — this task is winnable.)*

Good news to start with.

*(Explain.)* Task 1 is worth **one third** of your Writing score, and it is by far the **easier** half to improve. ✅

*(Point at camera.)*

*(Explain why.)* Because Task 2 asks you to **think** — you need ideas, a position, arguments. ✅

*(Emphasise.)* Task 1 asks you to **describe**. ✅ No opinion. No outside knowledge. Just accurate, selective reporting of what's in front of you. ⭐

- ❌ Giving their **opinion** *(forbidden here!)* ⚠️
- ❌ Listing **every** number instead of selecting the important ones ✅
- ❌ Missing the **overview** paragraph — which alone caps you at Band 5 ⚠️

## 🧠 2. THE CORE LOGIC (1:00 – 6:00)

*(Explain the task requirements precisely.)*

### 📊 What Task 1 actually asks

*(Set the frame.)*

*(Explain.)* You get a **visual** — a line graph, bar chart, pie chart, table, map, or process diagram. ✅ And this instruction: ✅

> *"Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant."*
>
> *Write at least 150 words.* *(20 minutes)*

*(Emphasise the three key words.)* ⭐

| Key word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Summarise** | not every detail — the **big picture** ✅ |
| **Selecting** | **choose** what matters; ignore the rest ⭐ |
| **Comparisons** | compare figures, don't just list them ✅ |

*(Firmly.)* ⭐ Notice what's **absent**: any request for your **opinion**, **reasons**, or **solutions**. ⚠️

### ⚠️ The absolute rule: NO OPINION

*(The most important rule of Task 1.)*

> ### 👉 **Never** write what you think, why it happened, or what should be done. ✅

*(Show the errors.)*
| ⚠️ Avoid in Task 1 | ✅ Do this instead |
|---|---|
| *This is because of urbanisation* ⚠️ *(a reason the chart never showed)* | *The figure rose sharply* ✅ |
| *I think this trend is worrying* ⚠️ *(opinion)* | *The trend continued upward* ✅ |
| *The government should act* ⚠️ *(solution)* | *Figures remained stable* ✅ |
| *This is a positive development* ⚠️ | *This was the highest of the three* ✅ |

*(Emphasise.)* ⚠️ Adding reasons or opinions is **off-task** ✅ — it wastes your 150 words on content that earns **zero** marks. ⚠️

*(Firmly.)* ⭐ **Describe. Compare. Nothing else.** ✅

### 🔑 THE FORMULA — four paragraphs

*(The core deliverable.)*

> ### **1. Introduction** — paraphrase the question *(1 sentence)* ✅
> ### **2. Overview** — the 2 biggest features, no numbers ⭐
> ### **3. Body 1** — detail group A, with figures ✅
> ### **4. Body 2** — detail group B, with figures ✅

*(Emphasise paragraph 2!)* ⭐

> ### 👉 **The OVERVIEW is the single most important paragraph.** ✅

*(Explain.)* Examiners look for it specifically. ✅ Without a clear overview, you **cannot** score above Band 5 on Task Achievement — regardless of how accurate your details are. ⚠️

*(What goes in it.)* ✅ The **most striking things** a person would notice in five seconds — pick the two clearest: ✅
- ✅ the overall **trend** *(rose, fell, fluctuated)* ✅
- ✅ the **highest** and **lowest** ✅
- ✅ any **dramatic** change or **crossover** ⭐

*(⚠️ Keep detailed numbers out of the overview.)* ✅ Save the figures for the body — the overview is for the **big picture**. ✅ *(A single figure is allowed if it IS the headline, but don't clutter it.)*

### ⏰ The 20-minute plan

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| **0–3 min** | Read, identify the **overview** features 🔍 ⭐ |
| **3–17 min** | Write ✍️ |
| **17–20 min** | Check figures and grammar 🔍 |

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ **Three minutes finding your overview** ✅ is the best-spent time in Task 1. ⭐

*(Word count.)* **150 is the official minimum · 170–190 is our practical target** ⭐ ✅ Don't write 250 — you'll steal time from Task 2, which is worth **twice** as much. ⚠️

*(Slogan.)*
> **Select, don't list. Overview always. No opinion ever.** 📊 ⭐

## 🔍 3. BUILDING THE ANSWER (6:00 – 15:00)

---

### 📋 The task

> **The line graph below shows the number of international tourists visiting three Indian states between 2010 and 2020.**
>
> *Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.*

**The data** *(shown on screen as a line graph)*:

| Year | Kerala | Goa | Rajasthan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2010 | 0.6 m | 0.5 m | 1.3 m |
| 2015 | 1.0 m | 0.5 m | 1.5 m |
| 2019 | 1.2 m | 0.9 m | 1.6 m |
| 2020 | 0.1 m | 0.1 m | 0.2 m |

---

### 🅐 Step 1 — Find the overview *(3 minutes)*

*(Do this on screen.)*

*(Ask.)* What would someone notice in **five seconds**? ✅

1. ⭐ **All three collapsed in 2020** *(the dramatic change)* ✅
2. ⭐ **Rajasthan was highest throughout**, but **Kerala grew fastest** ✅

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ **Those two facts are your overview.** ✅ Everything else is body detail. ✅

---

### 🅑 Step 2 — Introduction *(1 sentence)*

> ### **Formula: paraphrase the question, changing the words** ✅

**Model:**
> ✅ *"The line graph illustrates how many overseas visitors travelled to three states in India — Kerala, Goa and Rajasthan — over an eleven-year period from 2010 to 2020."* ⭐

*(The paraphrase moves.)*
| Question word | ✅ Paraphrase |
|---|---|
| *shows* | **illustrates / depicts / presents** ✅ |
| *the number of* | **how many** ✅ |
| *international tourists* | **overseas visitors** ⭐ |
| *visiting* | **travelled to** ✅ |
| *between 2010 and 2020* | **over an eleven-year period from 2010 to 2020** ⭐ |

*(⚠️ Never copy the question.)* ✅ Copied wording demonstrates none of **your** English, so it earns you nothing — paraphrase it instead. ⚠️

---

### 🅒 Step 3 — The overview *(the crucial paragraph)*

> ### **Formula: "Overall, ..." + the 2 big features, kept free of detailed figures** ✅

**Model:**
> ✅ *"Overall, visitor numbers in all three states increased steadily until 2019, before falling dramatically in 2020. Rajasthan attracted the most tourists throughout the period, although Kerala saw the sharpest growth of the three before the final year."* ⭐

*(Emphasise.)*
| Feature | Where |
|---|---|
| overall trend + the 2020 collapse ✅ | sentence 1 ⭐ |
| highest state + fastest grower ✅ | sentence 2 ⭐ |
| specific numbers | **none** ✅ correct! |

*(Firmly.)* ⭐ **Start it with "Overall,"** ✅ — it signals to the examiner that this is your overview. ✅ Do it every time. ⭐

---

### 🅓 Step 4 — Body paragraph 1

*(Group logically — here, the growth years.)*

**Model:**
> ✅ *"Between 2010 and 2019, Rajasthan remained the leading destination, with arrivals rising from 1.3 million to 1.6 million. Kerala, however, experienced the most substantial increase over the same years, doubling from 0.6 million to 1.2 million and narrowing the gap with Rajasthan considerably. Goa's figures, by contrast, were flat at 0.5 million until 2015, after which they climbed to 0.9 million by 2019."* ⭐

*(Note the techniques.)*
- ✅ **Comparisons**, not a list: *"however," "by contrast," "narrowing the gap"* ⭐
- ✅ **Varied verbs**: *rising, doubling, climbed, remained* ✅
- ✅ **Figures included** ✅
- ✅ **No reasons given** ✅ correct! ⭐

---

### 🅔 Step 5 — Body paragraph 2

*(The second grouping — here, 2020.)*

**Model:**
> ✅ *"In 2020, all three states recorded a steep decline. Rajasthan fell to just 0.2 million, while both Kerala and Goa dropped to approximately 0.1 million. The reduction was proportionally greatest in Kerala, which lost over 90 per cent of its 2019 arrivals."* ⭐

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ Notice — the essay says **what** happened, never **why**. ✅ No mention of the pandemic. ✅

*(Firmly.)* ⚠️ Writing *"this was due to Covid-19"* would be adding **outside knowledge** — which is off-task in Task 1. ✅

---

### 📄 The full answer *(168 words)*

> ✅ *The line graph illustrates how many overseas visitors travelled to three states in India — Kerala, Goa and Rajasthan — over an eleven-year period from 2010 to 2020.*
>
> *Overall, visitor numbers in all three states increased steadily until 2019, before falling dramatically in 2020. Rajasthan attracted the most tourists throughout the period, although Kerala saw the sharpest growth of the three before the final year.*
>
> *Between 2010 and 2019, Rajasthan remained the leading destination, with arrivals rising from 1.3 million to 1.6 million. Kerala, however, experienced the most substantial increase over the same years, doubling from 0.6 million to 1.2 million and narrowing the gap with Rajasthan considerably. Goa's figures, by contrast, were flat at 0.5 million until 2015, after which they climbed to 0.9 million by 2019.*
>
> *In 2020, all three states recorded a steep decline. Rajasthan fell to just 0.2 million, while both Kerala and Goa dropped to approximately 0.1 million. The reduction was proportionally greatest in Kerala, which lost over 90 per cent of its 2019 arrivals.* ⭐

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ **168 words. Four paragraphs. Zero opinions.** ✅

### 📚 The language reference

*(Screenshot-worthy tables.)*

#### 📈 Describing increase

| Verbs | Nouns |
|---|---|
| rose, increased, grew, climbed, surged ⭐ | a rise, an increase, growth, a surge ✅ |

#### 📉 Describing decrease

| Verbs | Nouns |
|---|---|
| fell, declined, dropped, decreased, plummeted ⭐ | a fall, a decline, a drop, a reduction ✅ |

#### 🎚️ Describing the **size** of change

| Big | Small | Sudden |
|---|---|---|
| sharply, dramatically, considerably, substantially ⭐ | slightly, marginally, gradually ✅ | suddenly, abruptly ✅ |

#### ➡️ Describing no change

- ✅ *remained stable / steady / unchanged* ✅
- ✅ *levelled off / plateaued* ⭐
- ✅ *stayed flat* ✅

#### 🔄 Describing fluctuation

- ✅ *fluctuated* ✅ · *varied* ✅ · *rose and fell* ✅

#### ⚖️ Making comparisons

- ✅ *while / whereas* ⭐
- ✅ *by contrast / in comparison* ✅
- ✅ *twice as high as / half of* ⭐
- ✅ *the highest / lowest / most significant* ✅
- ✅ *slightly more than / almost double* ✅

#### 🔢 Approximating figures

- ✅ *approximately · around · just over · just under · nearly · roughly* ⭐

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ **Approximating is good practice** ✅ — *"just over 1.2 million"* reads more naturally than a string of exact decimals. ✅

### 📊 The other chart types — quick notes

*(Practical coverage.)*

| Type | Key approach |
|---|---|
| **Line graph** 📈 | describe **trends over time** ✅ |
| **Bar chart** 📊 | **compare** categories; note highest/lowest ✅ |
| **Pie chart** 🥧 | describe **proportions**; use *accounted for, made up* ⭐ |
| **Table** 📋 | **select** — never describe every cell ⚠️ |
| **Process diagram** 🔧 | **passive voice** + sequencing words ⭐ 🔗 *(Day 59!)* |
| **Map** 🗺️ | describe **changes** between the two versions ✅ |

*(For a process diagram.)* ⭐ 🔗 *(Day 59's passive is the natural choice here — the doer is usually unknown or irrelevant. If the diagram names who does it, active voice is fine.)*
> ✅ *"First, the raw material **is collected** and **transported** to the factory. It **is then washed** before **being crushed** into smaller pieces."* ⭐

*(For a pie chart.)*
- ✅ *"Housing **accounted for** the largest share, at 35 per cent."* ⭐
- ✅ *"Transport **made up** just under a fifth of total spending."* ✅

## ⚠️ 4. THE MALAYALI TRAP (15:00 – 18:00)

### Trap 1: Giving reasons and opinions ⚠️

*(The costliest Task 1 error.)*

*(Explain.)* Why do candidates do this? ✅ Because it feels **incomplete** to describe a fall without explaining it. ✅ It feels like half an answer. ✅

*(Firmly.)* But Task 1 is a **reporting** task. ✅ Adding reasons is like a news reporter adding their political opinion to a weather report. ⚠️

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| *Tourism fell in 2020 due to the pandemic* ⚠️ | **Tourism fell sharply in 2020** ✅ |
| *Kerala grew because of its backwaters* ⚠️ | **Kerala showed the sharpest growth** ✅ |
| *This is a worrying trend* ⚠️ | **The figure declined steadily** ✅ |
| *The government should promote tourism* ⚠️ | *(delete entirely)* ✅ |

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ **If a sentence contains "because," "should," or "I think" — delete it.** ✅

### Trap 2: No overview paragraph ⚠️

*(A band-capping omission.)*

*(Explain.)* Candidates often jump straight from the introduction into the numbers. ✅

*(Firmly.)* ⚠️ Without an overview, Task Achievement is capped around **Band 5** ✅ — even with perfect grammar. ⚠️

*(The fix — an unmissable habit.)*

> ### 👉 Write **"Overall,"** as the first word of your second paragraph. Every single time. ✅

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ That one word makes the paragraph impossible for the examiner to miss. ✅

### Trap 3: Listing every number 📋

*(A Coherence and Task Achievement problem.)*

*(Explain.)* Many candidates try to include **every** data point: ✅

> ⚠️ *"In 2010 Kerala was 0.6, in 2011 it was 0.7, in 2012 it was 0.8, in 2013..."* ⚠️

*(Firmly.)* The task says **"selecting"** ✅ — so listing everything is failing the instruction, not exceeding it. ⚠️

*(The fix.)*

> ### 👉 Describe the **start**, the **end**, and any **turning point**. Skip the middle. ✅

- ✅ *"Kerala doubled from 0.6 to 1.2 million between 2010 and 2019."* ⭐ *(two numbers, whole trend)*

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ **Two well-chosen numbers beat ten listed ones.** ✅

### Trap 4: Wrong tense ⚠️

*(A simple, common error.)*

*(Explain.)* Match the tense to the **time** in the chart: ✅

| Chart shows | Tense |
|---|---|
| Past years *(2010–2020)* | **past simple** ✅ |
| Present/current data | **present simple** ✅ |
| Future projections | **will / is expected to** ✅ |
| A process diagram | **present simple passive** ⭐ |

*(Emphasise.)* ⚠️ Most graphs show the **past** ✅ → use **past simple** throughout. ✅ Mixing tenses is a very visible error. ⚠️

### Trap 5: Language to fix in Indian Task 1 answers ⚠️ *(some are errors, some are just wordy)*

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| *The graph shows about the tourists* 🔗 | **shows the number of tourists** ✅ |
| *increased with 20%* | **increased by 20%** ⭐ |
| *In 2010 to 2020* | **from 2010 to 2020** ✅ / **between 2010 and 2020** ✅ 🔗 *(Day 25!)* |
| *The number of tourist* 🔗 | **tourists** ✅ |
| *Number of tourists were* 🔗 *(Day 55)* | **The number of tourists was** ✅ |
| *It was highest in Rajasthan* | **Rajasthan recorded the highest figure** ⭐ |
| *Kerala is increased* | **Kerala increased** ✅ |
| *rise up* / *decrease down* | **rise** ✅ / **decrease** ✅ 🔗 *(Day 28!)* |
| *20 percentage* | **20 per cent** ✅ |
| *In the year 2010* | **In 2010** ✅ |

*(Highlight the "increased by" one!)* ⭐ **increase BY** an amount ✅ · **increase TO** a level ✅
- ✅ *rose **by** 0.6 million* ✅ *(the change)*
- ✅ *rose **to** 1.2 million* ✅ *(the endpoint)*
- ✅ *rose **from** 0.6 **to** 1.2 million* ⭐ *(both)*

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ Getting *by / to / from* right is a quick, visible accuracy gain. ✅

## 🔑 PRESENTER ANSWER KEY — *do not read on camera*

*(For the pinned comment, the video description, and replying in the comments. Several items have **more than one correct answer** — accept the alternatives listed.)*

**Q1 — the overview only (2 sentences, no numbers)**
**What a good answer looks like:** begins *"Overall,"* · names the biggest movement *(rise to 2019, sharp fall in 2020)* · names the highest performer and the fastest grower · **no data points and no reasons**.
Example: *"Overall, tourist numbers in all three states rose steadily until 2019 before falling sharply in the final year. Rajasthan remained the most visited state throughout, although Kerala grew the fastest."*

**Q2 — fix the five sentences**
1. ✅ *"The graph shows the number of tourists."* *(delete* about*; plural* tourists*)*
2. ✅ *"Tourism increased **by** 20% in 2015."* ⚠️ *"In the year 2015"* is **already grammatical** — only wordy
3. ⚠️ **Already grammatical.** The Task 1 issue is the outside **reason**: ✅ *"The figure fell sharply in 2020."*
4. ✅ *"**The** number of visitors **was** highest in Rajasthan."* ⚠️ *"It was highest in Rajasthan"* is fine
5. ✅ *"Between 2010 and 2020, the figures rose."* Also *"…there was an upward trend."*

> **⚠️ Marking rule:** `❌` only for genuinely ungrammatical English. If a learner's sentence is well-formed but **regional, dated, or means something else**, say so warmly and give the international version — never call it wrong. 🔗 *(symbol legend in the scripts README)*
