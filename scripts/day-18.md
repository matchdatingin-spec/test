# Day 18 — In, On, or At? ഈ 3 Words ഇനി തെറ്റില്ല

**Target length:** 13–15 minutes
**Level:** A2 / B1
**One-line promise:** *In* = inside a big space. *On* = touching a surface. *At* = one exact point. One picture solves 90% of cases.

---

## 🎬 1. THE HOOK (0:00 – 1:00)

*(Rapid-fire energy.)*

Quick! Fill in the blank:

- I'm ___ the bus.
- The meeting is ___ Monday.
- She lives ___ Kochi.
- Wait for me ___ the bus stop.
- My birthday is ___ June.

*(Pause.)* Did you hesitate? Every single time?

Because in Malayalam, **one ending does all of this**:

- ബസ്സി**ൽ** → in the bus
- തിങ്കളാഴ്ച → on Monday
- കൊച്ചിയി**ൽ** → in Kochi
- ബസ് സ്റ്റോപ്പി**ൽ** → at the bus stop
- ജൂണി**ൽ** → in June

*(Point at camera.)*

**-ൽ. That's it.** One tiny ending for everything. And English splits it into **in, on, at** — three words with completely different rules.

That's why this is the #1 struggle for Malayalam speakers. Not tenses. Not vocabulary. **Prepositions.**

Today, I give you one picture that fixes it. Let's go.

## 🧠 2. THE CORE LOGIC (1:00 – 3:00)

*(Use your hands. This is a visual lesson.)*

Forget the lists. Think about **SIZE and SHAPE**.

### 📦 IN = inside a container (3D space, you're surrounded)

You are **enclosed**. Walls around you. Big space.

- ✅ *in the room, in the car, in Kerala, in a box, in water*
- ✅ *in June, in 2026, in the morning* ← big blocks of time

**IN = surrounded by it.**

### 🛏️ ON = touching a surface (2D, flat, on top)

You are **touching a surface**. Or you're on a line.

- ✅ *on the table, on the wall, on the floor, on the bus*
- ✅ *on Monday, on 15th August* ← a single day sits on the calendar line

**ON = touching the surface of it.**

### 📍 AT = one exact point (no size at all)

Just a **dot on a map**. A specific spot, or a specific moment.

- ✅ *at the bus stop, at the door, at the office, at home*
- ✅ *at 5 o'clock, at midnight, at noon* ← exact time

**AT = a single point.**

### 🎯 The size rule for TIME

*(This part is beautifully logical.)*

| Size of time | Word | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **BIG** — months, years, seasons, parts of the day | **IN** | in June, in 2026, in summer, in the morning |
| **MEDIUM** — days, dates | **ON** | on Monday, on my birthday, on 15th August |
| **SMALL** — exact clock times | **AT** | at 6 o'clock, at 7:30, at night |

*(Say it as a slogan — three times!)*
> **Big time → IN. Day → ON. Clock time → AT.**
> **IN → ON → AT. Big → medium → small.**

*(Note the funny exception.)* **at night** — not "in night"! Even though night is big. Just accept it: *in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening, **at** night.* 🌙 One oddball.

### 🗺️ The size rule for PLACE

| Size of place | Word | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **BIG** — countries, cities, rooms, enclosed spaces | **IN** | in India, in Kochi, in the kitchen, in the car |
| **SURFACE** — flat things, and public transport | **ON** | on the table, on the wall, on the bus, on the train |
| **POINT** — a specific spot or address | **AT** | at the bus stop, at 5th Cross Road, at the gate, at work |

*(Same slogan!)* **Big → IN. Surface → ON. Point → AT.**

## 🔍 3. SENTENCE BREAKDOWN (3:00 – 8:00)

### Sentence 1 — built piece by piece

**Malayalam:** ഞാൻ ഇപ്പോൾ ബസ്സിൽ ആണ്.

Your brain sees **ബസ്സിൽ** → the **-ൽ** feels like "in" → ⚠️ *"I am in the bus."*

*(Surprise!)* But native English says: **"I'm on the bus."**

*(Ask the audience.)* Why *on*?! You're clearly **inside** the bus, not on the roof! 😄

*(Be honest here — this one is convention, not logic.)* ⚠️ There's no clever reason you can reason your way to. **Public transport simply takes *on* in English.** It's a fixed habit of the language, and the fastest thing you can do is **learn the two lists**, not hunt for a rule. ✅

So the pattern is:

| Vehicle | Word | Group |
|---|---|---|
| bus, train, plane, ship, boat | **ON** | public / shared transport 🚌 |
| car, taxi, auto | **IN** | private vehicles you sit inside 🚗 |
| bike, motorcycle, horse | **ON** | you sit on top 🏍️ |

*(Rough memory aid — not a law.)* **Shared transport you travel *on*. A private car you travel *in*. Anything you sit astride, you're *on*.** ⭐

*(Honest caveat.)* ⚠️ It isn't airtight, and it doesn't need to be. Small aircraft and small boats can take **in** *(✅ "in a small boat")*, and speakers vary. ✅ **Learn *on the bus / on the train / on the plane* and *in the car / in the taxi* as fixed chunks** — that covers almost everything you'll ever say. ⭐

Build the sentence:

1. **Who** → I
2. **be verb** → am
3. **Preposition** → on *(public transport)*
4. **What** → the bus

> ✅ **"I'm on the bus."**

*(Practise the whole family.)*
- ✅ *I'm **on** the train.*
- ✅ *I'm **on** the plane.*
- ✅ *I'm **in** the car.*
- ✅ *I'm **in** an auto.*
- ✅ *I'm **on** my bike.*

*(Bonus — get on / get off.)* And the movement verbs follow the same logic!
- ✅ *get **on** the bus* / *get **off** the bus*
- ✅ *get **in** the car* / *get **out of** the car*

*(Emphasise.)* Never ⚠️ *"get down from the bus"* — that's a very Indian phrase. Say **"get off the bus."** ✅

### Sentence 2 — built piece by piece

**Malayalam:** മീറ്റിംഗ് തിങ്കളാഴ്ച രാവിലെ 10 മണിക്ക് ആണ്.

*(This one has all three!)* Watch:
- തിങ്കളാഴ്ച → Monday → a **day** → **ON**
- രാവിലെ → morning → a **big part of the day** → **IN**
- 10 മണിക്ക് → 10 o'clock → an **exact time** → **AT**

Build it — and note the English order: **small time first, then bigger.**

1. **What** → The meeting
2. **be verb** → is
3. **Exact time** → at 10
4. **Part of day** → in the morning
5. **Day** → on Monday

> ✅ **"The meeting is at 10 in the morning on Monday."**

Or more naturally:
> ✅ **"The meeting is on Monday at 10 a.m."**
> ✅ **"The meeting is at 10 a.m. on Monday."**

*(Word order note — very useful.)* English usually goes **small time → big time**:
- ✅ *at 6 o'clock on Sunday in December* ✅
- ⚠️ *in December on Sunday at 6 o'clock* ← backwards

*(Compare with Malayalam.)* Malayalam does the opposite — **big to small**: ഡിസംബറിൽ ഞായറാഴ്ച ആറു മണിക്ക്. So you have to **reverse your instinct**. Practise: **clock → day → month.**

### Five more real-life examples

| Situation | ✅ Correct | Logic |
|---|---|---|
| Telling someone where you are | **I'm at the bus stop.** | exact point 📍 |
| Talking about your city | **I live in Thrissur.** | big space 📦 |
| Your birthday | **My birthday is on 12th March.** | a date 🗓️ |
| Where your phone is | **It's on the table.** | surface 🛏️ |
| Where you work | **I work at Infosys.** / **I work in the IT sector.** | point vs field |

*(Highlight row 5 — very useful for interviews!)*
- **at** + company name → *I work **at** Infosys.* ✅
- **in** + field/department → *I work **in** marketing.* ✅
- **for** + company *(also correct)* → *I work **for** TCS.* ✅

*(Bonus — the "no preposition" cases!)* Some places take **nothing** at all:
- ✅ *I'm going **home**.* ← never "to home"!
- ✅ *He's **at home**.* ← at home ✅ but *going home* ❌ no "to"
- ✅ *She's **at work**.* / *She went **to work**.*
- ✅ *I'm **in bed**.* ← no "the"!

*(Emphasise.)* ❌ *"I am going to home"* is one of the most common Malayali errors. Say **"I'm going home."** Just two words. 🏠

## ⚠️ 4. THE MALAYALI TRAP (8:00 – 10:00)

### Trap 1: "-ൽ" translated as "in" every single time

*(The root of everything.)* Look at how one Malayalam ending spreads across English:

| Malayalam | ⚠️ Direct | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| ബസ്സിൽ | in the bus | **on the bus** | public transport |
| തിങ്കളാഴ്ചയിൽ | in Monday | **on Monday** | a day |
| രാത്രിയിൽ | in night | **at night** | exception 🌙 |
| സ്കൂളിൽ | in school | **at school** | a point/institution |
| ഫോണിൽ | in phone | **on the phone** | surface/connection |
| ചിത്രത്തിൽ | in photo | **in the photo** ✅ | correct! |
| വീട്ടിൽ | in house | **at home** | fixed phrase |
| Facebook-ൽ | in Facebook | **on Facebook** | platforms take *on* |

*(Point out.)* **Platforms and networks usually take ON:**
- ✅ *on WhatsApp, on Instagram, on YouTube, on the internet*
- ✅ *I saw it **on** YouTube.*
- ❌ *I saw it in YouTube.*

But: ✅ ***in** an email* and ✅ ***on** the website*. 😄 Yes, English is not perfect. Some are just fixed.

### Trap 2: The "-ൽ നിന്ന്" and "-ലേക്ക്" endings

Malayalam marks direction with endings; English uses **from** and **to**:

| Malayalam | ✅ English |
|---|---|
| കൊച്ചിയിൽ നിന്ന് | **from** Kochi |
| കൊച്ചിയിലേക്ക് | **to** Kochi |
| വീട്ടിൽ നിന്ന് | **from home** |
| ബാങ്കിലേക്ക് | **to the bank** |

*(Big rule — movement vs position.)*
- **Moving** → **to** *(I'm going **to** the bank)*
- **Already there** → **in / at** *(I'm **at** the bank)*

*(The classic error.)* ⚠️ *"I'm going in the bank"* → ✅ **"I'm going to the bank."**

### Trap 3: Extra prepositions where English wants none

Malayalam loves case endings, so we add prepositions everywhere:

- ❌ *discuss about it* → ✅ **discuss it**
- ❌ *reach to the station* → ✅ **reach the station**
- ❌ *enter into the room* → ✅ **enter the room**
- ❌ *return back* → ✅ **return** / **come back**
- ❌ *marry with her* → ✅ **marry her**
- ❌ *contact to him* → ✅ **contact him**
- ❌ *going to home* → ✅ **going home**

*(Rule.)* **Discuss, reach, enter, marry, contact, answer, request** — these seven verbs take their object **directly**, with no preposition, in this basic meaning. ✅ *(Other senses can take one: ✅ *reach **for** the salt*, *enter **into** an agreement*.)*

*(And the opposite — missing prepositions.)*
- ❌ *listen the song* → ✅ **listen to the song**
- ❌ *wait me* → ✅ **wait for me**
- ❌ *depend the weather* → ✅ **depend on the weather**
- ❌ *laughing him* → ✅ **laughing at him**

### The 12 fixed phrases to memorise today

*(Tell the audience: don't calculate these — swallow them whole.)*

1. **at home** 🏠
2. **at work**
3. **at school / at college**
4. **in bed**
5. **on the bus / on the train**
6. **in the car**
7. **on the phone** 📱
8. **at night** 🌙
9. **in the morning / afternoon / evening**
10. **on time** *(punctual)* vs **in time** *(early enough)*
11. **at the moment** *(= right now)*
12. **in a hurry**

*(Explain #10 — very useful.)*
- ✅ *The bus came **on time**.* = exactly at the scheduled time ⏰
- ✅ *I arrived **in time** to catch the bus.* = early enough, with margin ✅
