## 🔍 3. THE QUIZ — 20 QUESTIONS (3:00 – 13:00)

*(For each: read the question, pause 5 seconds with a countdown timer on screen, then reveal + explain in 20 seconds.)*

---

**Q1.** Fill in: *"I ___ (have / am having) two sisters."*

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **have** — Owning isn't an action, so no *-ing*. *(Day 01)*

---

**Q2.** Correct this: *"Sir, I have a doubt about this lesson."*

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **"Sir, I have a question about this lesson."** — *Doubt* means suspicion. You want information → *question*. *(Day 02)*

---

**Q3.** Which is correct? *"Where you went yesterday?"* / *"Where did you go yesterday?"*

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **"Where did you go yesterday?"** — Questions need the helper, and *did* carries the past. *(Day 03)*

---

**Q4.** Fill in: *"___ you be free tomorrow?"* (Would / Should)

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **Would** — And notice **be**! *Would you **be** free?* Malayalam has no separate "be" verb, so we drop it. *(Day 04)*

---

**Q5.** Fill in: *"He ___ me the truth."* (said / told)

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **told** — A person (*me*) comes right after → **tell**. *Say the word, tell the person.* *(Day 05)*

---

**Q6.** Fill in: *"I ___ a mistake."* (did / made)

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **made** — A mistake is something **new** that appears. Create → *make*. *(Day 06)*

---

**Q7.** Fix this: *"If I had money, I will buy a car."*

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **"If I had money, I would buy a car."** — *had* is the imaginary world, so its partner must be **would**, not *will*. *(Day 07)*

---

**Q8.** Which is more confident in an interview? *"Yes, I can."* / *"Yes, I could."*

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **"Yes, I can."** — *Can* is strong and certain. *Could* sounds unsure. *(Day 08)*

---

**Q9.** Fix this: *"May be I will come tomorrow."*

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **"I might come tomorrow."** or **"Maybe I'll come tomorrow."** — One uncertainty word per sentence, not two. *(Day 09)*

---

**Q10.** Your friend offers to pay. You want to say "no need." Which one?
*"You mustn't pay."* / *"You don't have to pay."*

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **"You don't have to pay."** — *Mustn't* means **forbidden**! *(Day 10)*

---

**Q11.** Your father visited the bank and came home. Which one?
*"He has been to the bank."* / *"He has gone to the bank."*

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **"He has been to the bank."** — *Been* = came back ✋. *Gone* = still there ➡️. *(Day 11)*

---

**Q12.** You're leaving the house. Fill in: *"___ an umbrella, it might rain."* (Bring / Take)

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **Take** — Moving **away** from here ➡️. Remember: കൊണ്ടു**പോകുക** → **take**. *(Day 12)*

---

**Q13.** Fix this: *"Can you borrow me ₹100?"*

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **"Can I borrow ₹100?"** or **"Could you lend me ₹100?"** — *Borrow* = I take. *Lend* = you give. *(Day 13)*

---

**Q14.** Someone says *"I don't like coffee."* You agree. What do you say?

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **"Neither do I."** or **"Me neither."** — Negative agreement needs *neither*, not "me too." *(Day 14)*

---

**Q15.** Fix this: *"He didn't call me still."*

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **"He hasn't called me yet."** or **"He still hasn't called me."** — *Yet* goes at the **end**; *still* goes **before** the negative. *(Day 15)*

---

**Q16.** Which means "I smoked before, but not now"?
*"I am used to smoking."* / *"I used to smoke."*

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **"I used to smoke."** — No *am* → past habit. With *am* → comfortable now. *(Day 16)*

---

**Q17.** Fix this: *"I should came early."*

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **"I should have come early."** — After *have*, use the **third form**: come–came–**come**. *(Day 17)*

---

**Q18.** Fill in: *"I'm ___ the bus, I'll arrive ___ 6 o'clock ___ Monday."*

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **on** the bus, **at** 6 o'clock, **on** Monday — Public transport → *on*. Clock time → *at*. Day → *on*. *(Day 18)*

---

**Q19.** Fix this: *"I am working here since 3 years."* *(two mistakes!)*

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **"I have been working here for 3 years."** — *Since* → *for* (it's a length 📏), and *am working* → *have been working* (it touches now ✋). *(Days 19 + 27)*

---

**Q20.** Fill in: *"I am ___ teacher in ___ government school."*

⏱️ *5 seconds...*

✅ **a** teacher in **a** government school — Jobs always need *a/an*. *(Day 20)*

---

### 🎁 Bonus round — 5 extra marks!

*(For the champions. Say: "these are hard — don't worry if you miss them.")*

**B1.** How many mistakes here? *"Everyone are waiting and I don't have many informations."*
→ **Two.** ✅ *"Everyone **is** waiting and I don't have much **information**."* *(Days 23, 21)*

**B2.** Fix: *"Unless you don't study, you'll fail."*
→ ✅ *"Unless you study, you'll fail."* — *Unless* already means "if not." *(Day 26)*

**B3.** *Between* or *among*? *"The money was divided ___ the four brothers."*
→ ✅ **among** is the safe answer — dividing **spreads** something through a group ☁️. ✅ *Between* is also acceptable if you're stressing each brother's own share. Number doesn't decide it — the **relationship** does. *(Day 25)*

**B4.** Fix: *"Show me the another shirt."*
→ ✅ *"Show me the other shirt."* — *Another* already contains "an." *(Day 24)*

**B5.** What does *"I'll be right back"* translate to in Malayalam?
→ ✅ **ഞാൻ ഇപ്പോൾ വരാം** — Not *"I will come now"*! *(Day 29)*
