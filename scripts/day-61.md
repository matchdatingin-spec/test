# Day 61 — Question Tags Made Easy: Isn't It, Don't You, and Haven't They?

**Target length:** 12–14 minutes
**Level:** B1
**One-line promise:** Malayalam has one universal tag — **അല്ലേ?** English has thirty. Here's the pattern, plus a safe shortcut.

---

## 🎬 1. THE HOOK (0:00 – 1:00)

*(Friendly, conversational.)*

You want to check something with a colleague. And you say:

> ❌ *"You are coming tomorrow, isn't it?"*

*(Point at camera.)*

Every Indian says this. **Every** one of us. And it's understood perfectly in India. ✅

But internationally, it's wrong. The correct tag is:

> ✅ **"You're coming tomorrow, aren't you?"** ✅

*(Explain quickly.)* And here's why we all make the same mistake. Malayalam has **one** tag that fits **every** sentence:

- **നീ വരുന്നുണ്ട്, അല്ലേ?** ✅
- **അവന് അറിയാം, അല്ലേ?** ✅
- **ഇത് ശരിയാണ്, അല്ലേ?** ✅

*(Emphasise.)* **അല്ലേ works everywhere.** One tag, infinite sentences. ✅

*(Lean in.)*

English is **fussy**. The tag has to **match** the sentence — different helper, opposite polarity. About thirty possible tags. 😅

*(Confident.)*

But there's a clean pattern, and there's a **shortcut** for when you don't have time to calculate. Let's go. 🚀

---

## 🧠 2. THE CORE LOGIC (1:00 – 4:00)

*(Slow. Two rules, that's all.)*

### 🔑 THE TWO RULES

> ### 1️⃣ Use the **same helper** as the sentence
> ### 2️⃣ **Flip** the polarity — positive sentence → negative tag ✅

*(Show it.)*

| Sentence | Helper | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| *You **are** coming,* | are | ***aren't you**?* ✅ |
| *He **can** drive,* | can | ***can't he**?* ✅ |
| *She **has** left,* | has | ***hasn't she**?* ✅ |
| *They **will** come,* | will | ***won't they**?* ✅ |
| *You **don't** like it,* | don't | ***do you**?* ✅ *(flip to positive!)* |
| *He **isn't** here,* | isn't | ***is he**?* ✅ |

*(Emphasise.)* **Same helper, opposite sign.** ✅ Two rules, thirty tags. ✅

*(Slogan.)*
> **Copy the helper. Flip the sign.** 🔄
> **Helper അതേ. അടയാളം മാറ്റുക.** ✅

### And if there's no helper? Hire one! 🔧

*(Connect to Day 53.)* 🔗

*(Explain.)* If the sentence has **no** helper, you hire **do/does/did** — exactly as you do for questions ✅

| Sentence | Hidden helper | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| *You **like** tea,* | do | ***don't you**?* ✅ |
| *He **works** here,* | does | ***doesn't he**?* ✅ |
| *She **called** you,* | did | ***didn't she**?* ✅ |
| *They **went** home,* | did | ***didn't they**?* ✅ |

*(Emphasise.)* **Same logic as Day 53.** ✅ Present → *do/does* · Past → *did* ✅

### 🎉 THE SHORTCUT: just say "right?"

*(This is genuinely liberating.)*

*(Explain.)* In everyday spoken English, **"right?"** works as a universal tag — exactly like **അല്ലേ**! ✅

- ✅ *You're coming tomorrow, **right**?* ⭐
- ✅ *He's the manager, **right**?* ⭐
- ✅ *This is the bus to Kochi, **right**?* ⭐
- ✅ *You don't eat meat, **right**?* ⭐

*(Emphasise.)* **"Right?" never changes.** ✅ Use it while you build confidence with the proper tags. ⭐

*(Other universal options.)*
| Tag | Feel |
|---|---|
| **right?** ⭐ | most common, safe everywhere |
| **yeah?** | very casual |
| **correct?** | slightly formal |
| **is that right?** | polite, checking |
| **am I right?** | seeking confirmation |

*(Point at camera.)* So you have a **safety net**. ✅ Learn the proper tags for polish — but you can speak naturally **today** with **"right?"** ⭐

## 🔍 3. SENTENCE BREAKDOWN (4:00 – 11:00)

### Sentence 1 — built piece by piece

**Malayalam:** നീ നാളെ വരുന്നുണ്ട്, അല്ലേ?

Build it:

1. **Main sentence** → You're coming tomorrow
2. **What's the helper?** → **are** *(in "you're" = you are)* ✅
3. **Is the sentence positive?** → Yes → **flip to negative** ✅
4. **Tag** → aren't you?

> ✅ **"You're coming tomorrow, aren't you?"** ✅

*(Note the special one!)* ⚠️ **"I am" → "aren't I?"** 🤯

- ✅ *I'm late, **aren't I**?* ✅
- ❌ *I'm late, amn't I?* ❌ *(doesn't exist!)*

*(Explain.)* English has **no** contraction for *"am not."* ✅ So it borrowed *"aren't"* ✅ Strange but standard. 😄

*(Practise the positive→negative tags — say each.)*
1. *You're ready, **aren't you**?* ✅
2. *He's a doctor, **isn't he**?* ✅
3. *They were late, **weren't they**?* ✅
4. *She can swim, **can't she**?* ✅
5. *You'll help me, **won't you**?* ✅
6. *He's finished, **hasn't he**?* ✅
7. *You like coffee, **don't you**?* ✅
8. *She works there, **doesn't she**?* ✅
9. *They called you, **didn't they**?* ✅
10. *We should leave, **shouldn't we**?* ✅

### Sentence 2 — built piece by piece *(negative sentence)*

**Malayalam:** അവന് ഇത് അറിയില്ല, അല്ലേ?

Build it:

1. **Main sentence** → He doesn't know this
2. **Helper** → **doesn't** ✅
3. **Sentence is negative** → **flip to positive** ✅
4. **Tag** → does he?

> ✅ **"He doesn't know this, does he?"** ✅

*(Practise the negative→positive tags.)*
1. *You don't smoke, **do you**?* ✅
2. *He isn't coming, **is he**?* ✅
3. *She hasn't called, **has she**?* ✅
4. *They weren't there, **were they**?* ✅
5. *You can't drive, **can you**?* ✅
6. *He didn't tell you, **did he**?* ✅

*(Emphasise.)* Notice — the negative tags are **shorter**! ✅ *"do you?"* · *"is he?"* · *"did he?"* ✅ Easier, actually. 😌

---

### 🎵 The tone changes the meaning!

*(This is genuinely important and rarely taught.)*

*(Explain.)* The **same** tag means two different things depending on your **voice**:

**Falling tone ⬇️ = I'm confident, just confirming**
> ✅ *"You're from Kochi, aren't you?"* ⬇️ → *I'm fairly sure. Just checking.* ✅

**Rising tone ⬆️ = I genuinely don't know, it's a real question**
> ✅ *"You're from Kochi, aren't you?"* ⬆️ → *Am I right? I'm not sure.* ✅

*(Demonstrate both out loud — this needs the voice.)* 🔊

*(Emphasise.)* **Malayalam does this too!** ✅ **അല്ലേ?** with a flat tone = confirming · with a rising tone = genuinely asking ✅

*(Point out.)* So you **already** have this skill. ✅ Just apply it to the English tag. ⭐

---

### The special cases 📋

*(These catch people — worth covering.)*

#### 1️⃣ Imperatives → "will you?" ✅
- ✅ *Close the door, **will you**?* ✅
- ✅ *Help me, **would you**?* ✅ *(politer)*
- ✅ *Don't be late, **will you**?* ✅

#### 2️⃣ Let's → "shall we?" ⭐
- ✅ *Let's go, **shall we**?* ⭐
- ✅ *Let's start, **shall we**?* ✅

*(Note.)* This is where **"shall"** genuinely lives in modern English! ✅ 🔗 *(Day 49)*

#### 3️⃣ "There is/are" → keep "there" ✅
- ✅ *There's a problem, **isn't there**?* ✅
- ✅ *There are two options, **aren't there**?* ✅

#### 4️⃣ Nobody / everyone → "they" ✅ 🔗 *(Day 23)*
- ✅ *Nobody called, **did they**?* ✅
- ✅ *Everyone is here, **aren't they**?* ✅

*(Note.)* Singular verb, but **plural** pronoun in the tag! ✅ Same oddity as Day 23. 🔗

#### 5️⃣ Negative words count as negative! ⚠️
- ✅ *He **never** calls, **does he**?* ✅ *(never = negative → positive tag)*
- ✅ *She has **nothing** to do, **does she**?* ✅
- ✅ *Nobody knows, **do they**?* ✅

*(Explain.)* Words like **never, nothing, nobody, hardly, seldom** are already negative ✅ So the tag goes **positive**. ✅

#### 6️⃣ "I think..." → tag the inner sentence ⚠️
- ✅ *I think he's coming, **isn't he**?* ✅ *(not "don't I")*

## ⚠️ 4. THE MALAYALI TRAP (11:00 – 13:00)

### Trap 1: "isn't it?" for everything

*(The signature Indian-English tag.)*

*(Explain warmly.)* **"Isn't it?"** became India's universal tag because it maps perfectly onto **അല്ലേ**. ✅ And within India, it's completely understood — millions of people use it. ✅

*(But.)* Internationally, it only works when the subject is genuinely **"it"**:
- ✅ *It's hot today, **isn't it**?* ✅ *(correct — subject is "it"!)*
- ❌ *You're coming, isn't it?* ❌
- ❌ *He knows, isn't it?* ❌
- ❌ *They finished, isn't it?* ❌

*(The fix — two options.)*

**Option 1: Learn the proper tag** ✅ *(polish)*
**Option 2: Say "right?"** ⭐ *(instant fix)*

*(Emphasise.)* If **"isn't it?"** is deeply automatic for you — replace it with **"right?"** ✅ Same universality, internationally correct. ⭐

*(Practise the swap.)*
- ❌ *You're coming, isn't it?* → ✅ **You're coming, right?** ⭐
- ❌ *He knows, isn't it?* → ✅ **He knows, right?** ⭐

### Trap 2: "no?" and "na?"

*(Direct translations of Malayalam particles.)*

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| You'll come, no? | **You'll come, won't you?** ✅ / **right?** ⭐ |
| Tell me, na? | **Please tell me** ✅ |
| It's good, no? | **It's good, isn't it?** ✅ |

*(Explain.)* **-ഏ, -ല്ലേ, -നോ** are Malayalam's conversational glue ✅ English uses **"right?"** or a proper tag. ✅

### Trap 3: Wrong helper in the tag

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| You like tea, aren't you? | **You like tea, don't you?** ✅ |
| He works here, isn't he? | **He works here, doesn't he?** ✅ |
| She came, isn't she? | **She came, didn't she?** ✅ |

*(The fix.)* 🔗 *(Day 53)*

> **Does the sentence have a helper?** ✅ → copy it
> **No helper?** ✅ → hire *do/does/did* ✅

*(The test.)* Ask: *"How would I make this a question?"* ✅
- *You like tea* → *Do you like tea?* → so the tag uses **do** ✅
- *He works here* → *Does he work here?* → tag uses **does** ✅

### Trap 4: Forgetting to flip

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| You're coming, are you? ⚠️ *(correct, but sounds surprised)* | **You're coming, aren't you?** ✅ |
| He doesn't know, doesn't he? | **He doesn't know, does he?** ✅ |

*(Note.)* ⚠️ *"You're coming, are you?"* **does** exist — but it sounds **surprised** or even **sarcastic**! 😄
- ✅ *"Oh, you're coming, are you?"* → *(so you've decided to show up?)* 😏

*(Emphasise.)* **Same polarity = attitude. Opposite polarity = normal check.** ✅

### Why tags matter *(the real value)* ⭐

*(An honest and useful point.)*

*(Explain.)* Tags aren't decoration. They do a **social** job:

| Purpose | Example |
|---|---|
| **Soften a statement** 🎁 | *That's not correct, **is it**?* ← gentler than "that's wrong" |
| **Invite agreement** 🤝 | *It's a good plan, **isn't it**?* |
| **Start small talk** 💬 | *Lovely weather, **isn't it**?* ⭐ |
| **Check politely** ✅ | *You did send it, **didn't you**?* |

*(Highlight #1.)* ⭐ 🔗 *(Day 42)* A tag is a **softener**! ✅
- ⚠️ *"You forgot to send it."* → blunt 😬
- ✅ *"You forgot to send it, didn't you?"* → gentler ✅

*(Emphasise.)* Tags are one of the easiest ways to sound **less blunt** in English. ✅

### The 3-second self-check 🎯

1. **What's the helper?** *(or hire do/does/did)* ✅
2. **Is the sentence positive or negative?** → **flip it** 🔄
3. **Match the pronoun** *(he, she, they, it, you, I)* ✅
4. **Unsure? Say "right?"** ⭐

*(Drill — I say the sentence, you say the tag.)*
- *You're tired,* → **aren't you?** ✅
- *He can drive,* → **can't he?** ✅
- *She doesn't smoke,* → **does she?** ✅
- *They went home,* → **didn't they?** ✅
- *I'm right,* → **aren't I?** ✅
- *Let's go,* → **shall we?** ✅
- *Close the window,* → **will you?** ✅
- *There's a problem,* → **isn't there?** ✅

*(Emphasise.)* Do that drill every morning for a week. **Twenty seconds.** ✅
