# Day 52 — Is, Am, Are, Was, and Were: Build Correct Sentences

**Target length:** 13–15 minutes
**Level:** A2 / B1
**One-line promise:** Malayalam has no *standalone* "be" word — it's built into the ending. That single difference causes dozens of errors, and today we close it at the root.

---

## 🎬 1. THE HOOK (0:00 – 1:00)

*(Direct, foundational.)*

Listen to these five sentences:

> ❌ *"I working in Kochi."*
> ❌ *"He is know the answer."*
> ❌ *"That would nice."*
> ❌ *"She very tired."*
> ❌ *"I am agree with you."*

*(Point at camera.)*

Five sentences. **Five different errors.** But they all come from **one** source. Just one.

*(Pause.)*

**Malayalam has no *separate, standalone* word for "am / is / are."** ✅

*(Be precise — this matters.)* ⚠️ Malayalam absolutely **has** a linking element: **ആണ്**. ✅ The difference is that it **attaches to the end of the word** instead of standing alone as its own word the way English *is* does. ⭐

*(Explain quickly.)* Look:
- **ഞാൻ ക്ഷീണിതനാണ്** → the **ആണ്** is **glued onto** the word
- **അവൻ നല്ലവനാണ്** → glued again
- **ഞാൻ ജോലി ചെയ്യുന്നു** → here there's **no "be" element at all!**

*(Emphasise.)* So your brain has **no slot** for a standalone *be* verb. Sometimes you **drop** it. Sometimes you **add** it where it doesn't belong. ✅

*(Lean in, confident.)*

We've hit this problem on Days 04, 07, 09, 16, and 28. Today we fix it at the root — permanently. Let's go. 🚀

## 🧠 2. THE CORE LOGIC (1:00 – 4:00)

*(Slow. This is foundational — take the time.)*

### First — the table you must own 🔤

| Subject | Present | Past |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **am** | **was** |
| **he / she / it** | **is** | **was** |
| **you / we / they** | **are** | **were** |

*(Emphasise.)* Six words. Those are **all the present and past forms** you'll ever need. ✅ *(Plus the plain form **be**, and **been** for the perfect — Day 54.)*

*(Memory aid.)*
> **I am · He is · They are.**
> **I was · He was · They were.** ✅

*(Say it as a chant, three times.)* 🗣️

### 🔑 The big question: WHEN do I need "be"?

*(This is the actual lesson.)*

> ### 👉 You need "be" when there is NO other action verb.

*(Explain.)* Every English sentence needs **exactly one** verb doing the main job. If you have a real **action** *(work, eat, go, know)*, that's your verb — no *be* needed. If you have **no action**, *be* fills the gap. ✅

*(Test it.)*

| Sentence | Action verb? | Need be? |
|---|---|---|
| I **work** in Kochi. | yes ✅ | no ❌ |
| I **am** a teacher. | no | **yes** ✅ |
| I **am working** now. | yes, but *-ing* needs help | **yes** ✅ |
| I **know** the answer. | yes ✅ | no ❌ |
| I **am** tired. | no | **yes** ✅ |

*(Point out row 3!)* This is the subtle one. **-ing is not a complete verb** — it always needs a *be* partner. ✅

*(Rule.)* **An -ing verb can't be your main verb on its own.** ✅
- ❌ *I working* ❌
- ✅ *I **am** working* ✅

*(Advanced note — say it once.)* ⭐ *-ing* words **can** appear without *be*, but then they're doing a different job — acting like a noun or a description: ✅ *Swimming helps.* · *I saw her leaving.* ✅ For your **main verb**, always pair *-ing* with *am/is/are/was/were*. ⭐

### The 4 jobs of "be"

*(Comprehensive but simple.)*

**Job 1: Linking a person to a description** 🏷️
- ✅ *I **am** tired.* · *She **is** a doctor.* · *They **are** happy.*

**Job 2: Helping -ing** 🔄
- ✅ *I **am** working.* · *He **was** sleeping.*

**Job 3: Helping the passive** 🔁 *(Day 59!)*
- ✅ *The file **was** sent.* · *English **is** spoken here.*

**Job 4: Location and existence** 📍
- ✅ *He **is** at home.* · *There **is** a problem.*

*(Slogan.)*
> **No action verb? → be. Using -ing? → be. Describing? → be.** ✅

### 🚫 And when you must NOT use "be"

*(Critical — this fixes "I am agree.")*

*(Explain.)* When your word is **already an action verb**, adding *be* breaks the sentence:

| ❌ | ✅ | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I am agree | **I agree** | *agree* is a verb ✅ |
| I am know | **I know** | *know* is a verb ✅ |
| He is understand | **He understands** | verb ✅ |
| I am need help | **I need help** | verb ✅ |
| I am go now | **I'm going now** ✅ | *go* needs *-ing* here |

*(The 15 verbs Malayalis wrongly add "be" to.)* 🔗 *(Day 28)*

**agree · know · understand · remember · forget · need · want · like · love · hate · think · hope · mean · belong · own** ✅

*(Say it.)* These are **verbs**. Don't put am/is/are **directly** in front of them. ✅

*(One clarification, so a native speaker doesn't confuse you later.)* ⭐ Some of these do appear with *be* + **-ing** when the meaning shifts: ✅ *I'm **thinking** about it.* · *I'm **hoping** to finish today.* ✅ What's always wrong is *be* + the **plain** verb: ❌ *I am agree.* ⭐

## 🔍 3. SENTENCE BREAKDOWN (4:00 – 11:00)

### Sentence 1 — built piece by piece

**Malayalam:** ഞാൻ ഒരു അധ്യാപകനാണ്, ഞാൻ കൊച്ചിയിൽ ജോലി ചെയ്യുന്നു.

*(Notice the Malayalam!)* Two halves, and they behave **differently**:
- **അധ്യാപകനാണ്** → the **ആണ്** is present *(glued to the noun)* ✅
- **ജോലി ചെയ്യുന്നു** → **no ആണ്** — there's a real action verb ✅

*(Excited.)* So Malayalam **already** makes this distinction! You just can't see it, because the ആണ് is **inside** the word. ✅

Build **Half 1:**

1. **Who** → I
2. **Is there an action?** → No! Just describing myself → **need be** ✅
3. **be verb for I** → am
4. **What** → a teacher *(Day 20 — jobs need "a"!)* 🔗

> ✅ **"I am a teacher."**

Build **Half 2:**

1. **Who** → I
2. **Is there an action?** → **Yes! work** → **no be** ✅
3. **Verb** → work
4. **Where** → in Kochi

> ✅ **"I work in Kochi."**

**Together:**
> ✅ **"I am a teacher. I work in Kochi."** ⭐

*(Emphasise.)* Look at that — **first sentence has "am," second doesn't.** ✅ And the reason is simply: sentence two has a real action verb. ✅

*(Practise the pair.)*
- ✅ *I **am** an engineer. I **work** at Infosys.* ✅
- ✅ *She **is** a nurse. She **works** at a hospital.* ✅
- ✅ *They **are** students. They **study** in Bangalore.* ✅

*(Point out the -s!)* 🔗 *(Day 47)* For he/she/it → the action verb takes **-s**: *works, studies, knows.* ✅

### Sentence 2 — built piece by piece

**Malayalam:** അവൻ ഇപ്പോൾ ഉറങ്ങുകയാണ്, ഇന്നലെ അവൻ വളരെ ക്ഷീണിതനായിരുന്നു.

*(Notice!)*
- **ഉറങ്ങുകയാണ്** → action + ആണ് → **is sleeping** ✅ *(-ing needs be!)*
- **ക്ഷീണിതനായിരുന്നു** → description + past ആയിരുന്നു → **was tired** ✅

Build **Half 1:**

1. **Who** → He
2. **-ing?** → Yes → **need be** ✅
3. **be verb for he, present** → is
4. **Verb + ing** → sleeping
5. **When** → now

> ✅ **"He is sleeping now."**

Build **Half 2:**

1. **When** → Yesterday
2. **Who** → he
3. **Description, past** → **was**
4. **What** → very tired

> ✅ **"Yesterday he was very tired."**

**Together:**
> ✅ **"He is sleeping now. Yesterday he was very tired."** ✅

*(Note the past forms.)*
| Present | Past |
|---|---|
| I am → | **I was** |
| He is → | **He was** |
| They are → | **They were** |
| You are → | **You were** |

*(Malayali warning!)* ❌ *"They was"* → ✅ **"They were."** ✅ And ❌ *"You was"* → ✅ **"You were."** ✅

### Five more real-life examples

| Situation | ✅ Correct | be or not? |
|---|---|---|
| Introducing yourself | **I'm Anu. I work in HR.** | am ✅, then no be ✅ |
| Explaining you're busy | **I'm in a meeting right now.** | be for location 📍 |
| Agreeing | **I agree with you.** | **no be!** ✅ |
| Describing the weather | **It was raining all night.** | was + -ing ✅ |
| Saying something exists | **There is a problem with the file.** | be for existence ✅ |

*(Highlight row 5 — "There is"!)* ⭐ *(Day 32)* 🔗

**ഉണ്ട് → "There is / There are"** ✅

- ✅ *There **is** a problem.* ← singular ✅
- ✅ *There **are** two options.* ← plural ✅
- ✅ *There **was** a delay.* ← past ✅
- ✅ *There **isn't** any water.* ← negative ✅

*(Malayali warning.)* ❌ *"A problem is there"* → ✅ **"There is a problem."** ✅

---

### The full forms — positive, negative, question 📋

*(Practical reference — practise all three shapes.)*

| Form | Present | Past |
|---|---|---|
| **Positive** | I am / he is / they are | I was / they were |
| **Negative** | I'm not / he isn't / they aren't | I wasn't / they weren't |
| **Question** | Am I? / Is he? / Are they? | Was I? / Were they? |

*(Note the question — the SWAP!)* 🔗 *(Day 35)*
- ✅ *He **is** here.* → ***Is** he here?* ✅
- ✅ *They **were** late.* → ***Were** they late?* ✅

*(Practise the contractions!)* 🔊
| Full | Spoken ⭐ |
|---|---|
| I am | **I'm** |
| he is | **he's** |
| they are | **they're** |
| I am not | **I'm not** |
| he is not | **he isn't** / **he's not** |
| they are not | **they aren't** / **they're not** |
| was not | **wasn't** |
| were not | **weren't** |

*(Emphasise.)* Native speakers contract these **most of the time** in normal speech. Make **"I'm," "he's," "they're"** your default. ✅

*(Note.)* ✅ The full forms aren't wrong — they come back for **emphasis** and careful speech: ✅ *"I **am** serious."* ⭐

## ⚠️ 4. THE MALAYALI TRAP (11:00 – 13:00)

### Trap 1: Dropping "be" completely

*(The most common error — because Malayalam's ആണ് is invisible.)*

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| I working now | **I'm working now** |
| She very tired | **She's very tired** |
| He my brother | **He's my brother** |
| They coming tomorrow | **They're coming tomorrow** |
| That would nice | **That would be nice** ✅ |
| It may difficult | **It may be difficult** ✅ |

*(Highlight the last two!)* 🔗 *(Days 04, 07, 09)*

*(Emphasise.)* After **would / could / should / may / might / will**, when there's no action, you need **be**:
- ✅ *would **be*** · *could **be*** · *should **be*** · *might **be*** · *will **be*** ✅

*(Chant it.)* **Would be. Could be. Should be. Might be. Will be.** ✅ Three times! 🗣️

*(The self-check.)*

> ### 👉 Before you finish a sentence, ask: "Does this sentence have a VERB?"

If the answer is no — insert **be**. ✅

### Trap 2: Adding "be" to action verbs

*(The reverse error.)*

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| I am agree | **I agree** |
| I am know him | **I know him** |
| He is understand | **He understands** |
| I am having a car | **I have a car** 🔗 |
| She is knowing English | **She knows English** |

*(The self-check.)*

> **Is my word already an ACTION?** → Then no *be*. ✅

*(Practise the ten most common.)* Say each:
1. I agree. ✅
2. I know. ✅
3. I understand. ✅
4. I remember. ✅
5. I forget. ✅
6. I need it. ✅
7. I want it. ✅
8. I like it. ✅
9. I think so. ✅
10. I have it. ✅

*(Emphasise.)* **Zero "am" in that list.** ✅

### Trap 3: The wrong be-verb with plurals

*(A subject-verb agreement leak — Day 55's topic!)* 🔗

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| My parents is coming | **My parents are coming** |
| The books is on the table | **The books are on the table** |
| Everyone are here | **Everyone is here** ✅ 🔗 |
| There is many problems | **There are many problems** |
| My family are big | **My family is big** ✅ |

*(Rule.)* **Find the MAIN word of the subject — the thing the sentence is really about.**
- One thing → **is/was** ✅
- More than one → **are/were** ✅
- *everyone/everybody/nobody* → **is** ✅ *(Day 23)* 🔗

*(Warning — don't just grab the nearest word!)* ⚠️ ✅ *The box **of chocolates** **is** on the table.* ✅ The main word is *box*, not *chocolates*. 🔗 *(Day 55 drills this properly.)*

### Trap 4: "Is" for actions in the present

*(A specific and common error.)*

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| I am go to office daily | **I go to the office daily** |
| He is come every day | **He comes every day** |
| She is work in a bank | **She works in a bank** |

*(Explain.)* If you want the **habit** meaning, use the plain verb with **no be** ✅ *(Day 47)* 🔗

*(But if you want "right now," use be + -ing:)*
- ✅ *I **am going** to the office now.* ✅

*(Rule.)* **be + -ing** ✅ or **plain verb alone** ✅ — never **be + plain verb**. ❌

*(The three shapes.)*
| ✅ Correct | Meaning |
|---|---|
| I **work** here | habit 🔁 |
| I **am working** here | now ⏺️ |
| ❌ I **am work** here | ❌ never |

### The 3-second self-check 🎯

*(Give them a practical tool.)*

Before every sentence, run this:

1. **Is there an action word?** *(work, eat, go, know, agree)*
   → **Yes, plain form** → no *be* ✅
   → **Yes, with -ing** → need *be* ✅
   → **No action at all** → need *be* ✅

2. **Which be?** → I=am · he/she/it=is · you/we/they=are ✅

3. **Past?** → I/he/she/it=**was** · you/we/they=**were** ✅

*(Practise fast — I say, you fill in.)*
- *"I ___ tired."* → **am** ✅
- *"I ___ agree."* → **nothing!** ✅
- *"He ___ working."* → **is** ✅
- *"They ___ late yesterday."* → **were** ✅
- *"That would ___ nice."* → **be** ✅
- *"There ___ two problems."* → **are** ✅
