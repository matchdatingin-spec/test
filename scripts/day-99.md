### ✍️ Segment 3 — Live sentence corrections *(20 minutes)*

*(The most engaging segment. High energy.)*

*(Instruct.)* Right — post a **sentence in English** in the chat. ✅ Anything: about your day, your job, your goal. ⭐

*(Firmly, warmly.)* And post it **even if you think it's wrong.** ✅ Especially then. 💙

*(Then work through them live.)*

**Host guidance for each correction:**
1. ✅ **Read it out** with the person's first name — *"Anu says..."* ⭐
2. ✅ **Say one genuine thing that's right** about it first ⭐
3. ✅ Give the correction ✅
4. ✅ Name the **day number** where the rule was taught 🔗
5. ✅ Move on quickly — aim for **15–20** corrections ⚡

*(⚠️ Critical tone note.)* ⭐ Never make a sentence funny at the writer's expense. ✅ These people have just taken a public risk. Protect them. 💙

| Likely submission | Fix | Day |
|---|---|---|
| *"I am learning English since 6 months"* | for six months 🔗 | 19 |

### ❓ Segment 4 — Q&A *(20 minutes)*

*(Have a moderator surface questions. Prepare the top ten in advance.)*

**⭐ The ten questions you WILL be asked — prepare these:**

**1. "How long will it take me to become fluent?"** ⏳
> ✅ *Honest answer: 90 days at 20 min/day moves you from hesitant to comfortable. Genuine fluency is 1–2 years of consistent practice. Anyone promising fluency in 30 days is selling you something.* ⭐

**2. "Which accent should I learn?"** 🇬🇧🇺🇸
> ✅ *Neither. Speak clear Indian English. Fix word stress and chunking. That's Day 73.* ⭐

**3. "I don't have anyone to practise with."** 🚪
> ✅ *Start with self-talk and recording — Day 37. Then voice notes. You need a listener far less than you think.* ⭐

**4. "My mind goes blank in interviews."** 🧊
> ✅ *Day 38. Memorise two emergency phrases until they're automatic. That's the fix — not more grammar.* ⭐

**5. "Is grammar important or not?"** 📚
> ✅ *Important, and you probably already have enough. Days 46–65 covered the whole system. Now you need to use it, not learn more.* ⭐

**6. "How do I stop translating in my head?"** 🧠
> ✅ *Day 37. And be patient — it takes 4–6 months for simple thoughts. It's a growing list, not a switch.* ⭐

**7. "What's the fastest way to improve?"** ⚡
> ✅ *Speak out loud 12 minutes a day. That's genuinely the fastest thing available to you.* ⭐

**8. "Should I take IELTS or OET?"** 🎓
> ✅ *OET if you're a healthcare professional going to a healthcare job — it's more relevant and often easier for nurses. IELTS for everything else.* ⭐

**9. "My English is very poor, where do I start?"** 💙
> ✅ *First — please stop saying that. Day 75. Second — Days 52, 53, 54. The three helper verb videos. Start there.* ⭐

**10. "Will you make more videos?"** 📹
> ✅ *[Your answer — and this is the natural bridge to Day 100.]* ⭐

## ⚠️ 4. HOST GUIDANCE — Handling the room (70:00 – 80:00)

*(Section 4 adapts — practical notes for you, not for the audience.)*

### 😶 If the chat is quiet early

*(Explain.)* Normal for the first ten minutes. ✅ People are shy in a live chat, especially about English. ✅

**Fixes:** ⭐
- ✅ Ask for something **easy and non-linguistic** — city names, how many videos watched ✅
- ✅ Use your **prepared backup scenarios** ⭐
- ✅ Say explicitly: *"You can write in Malayalam if that's easier"* ✅ — removes the barrier ⭐

### 🙏 If someone is self-deprecating

*(This will happen.)* Someone will write *"sorry my English is very poor sir."* ✅

**Response — prepare this:** ⭐
> ✅ *"Please don't apologise. You just wrote a sentence in English in front of hundreds of people. That takes more courage than perfect grammar does."* 💙 ⭐

*(Firmly.)* Say some version of this **every time** it happens. ✅ It's the most important thing you'll say all session. ⭐

### ⚠️ If someone asks about the course early

*(Explain.)* Someone will ask about paid coaching in minute five. ✅

**Response:** ⭐
> ✅ *"I'll mention it at the end — but honestly, most of what you need is already free in the 98 videos. Let's use this hour on your actual questions first."* ⭐

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ That answer builds **more** trust than an immediate pitch, and it converts better. ✅

### 💰 The soft mention *(last 5 minutes only)*

*(Keep this short and honest.)*

*(Explain.)* When you do mention `[COURSE NAME]`, keep it to **90 seconds** and be straight about who it's **not** for: ✅

**Suggested framing:** ⭐
> ✅ *"Before we finish — one thing. Everything in the 100 videos is free and it stays free. If you follow Day 98's plan on your own, it will work.*
>
> *What it doesn't give you is **someone correcting you**. And for some people that's the missing piece — you can't hear your own errors. That's what `[COURSE NAME]` is: structured practice with feedback.*
>
> *If you're self-disciplined, honestly — just use the free videos. If you know you need accountability and correction, tomorrow's video explains what's inside. `[LINK]`"* ⭐

*(Firmly.)* ⭐ **Telling some people not to buy** is the most persuasive thing you can do. ✅ And it's honest. ⭐

## 🔑 PRESENTER ANSWER KEY — *do not read on camera*

*(For the pinned comment, the video description, and replying in the comments.)*

**This is a live Q&A, so there is no graded homework. Three things learners are asked to do:**

**1. Ask a question in the chat**
**What a good answer looks like:** one specific question, not *"how to improve English?"* · ideally tied to their own situation *(a job, an exam, a meeting)*.
**Guidance:** the nine questions already scripted in Section 3 cover the most common ones — reuse those answers rather than improvising.

**2. Comment your profile number (1–4)**
✅ Any number, plus your reply naming their starting day. **Also accept** *"between 2 and 3"* — pick the lower one for them; it's safer to start easier.

**3. Record your baseline tonight**
**What a good answer looks like:** two minutes on *"tell me about yourself"* · recorded **before** they sleep, not "tomorrow" · labelled **Day 1** · deliberately not listened back to.
✅ **Model comment:** *"Profile 2. Baseline recorded ✅"*
**Guidance:** this is the highest-value thing in the video — chase it in the chat more than the link clicks.

> **⚠️ Marking rule:** `❌` only for genuinely ungrammatical English. If a learner's sentence is well-formed but **regional, dated, or means something else**, say so warmly and give the international version — never call it wrong. 🔗 *(symbol legend in the scripts README)*
