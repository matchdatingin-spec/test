## 🧠 2. THE CORE LOGIC (1:00 – 3:00)

*(Before the test — teach them what "level" actually means. This is genuinely valuable.)*

### What do these levels actually mean? 📊

*(Explain honestly, using the international scale.)*

| Level | Name | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **A2** | Beginner+ | simple sentences, present/past, daily topics ✅ |
| **B1** | Intermediate | connect ideas, handle work situations, explain reasons ⭐ |
| **B2** | Upper-intermediate | discuss abstract topics, argue a point, sound natural ⭐ |
| **C1** | Advanced | nuance, idiom, near-native flexibility ✅ |

*(Point out something useful.)* Most Malayali learners who've studied English at school sit at **A2 grammar with B1 vocabulary** ✅ — you **know** more words than your grammar can carry. ✅

*(Emphasise.)* That's why you can **read** English well but **speak** it hesitantly. Your vocabulary is ahead of your structure. ✅

### The three levels of mistakes 🔍

*(This is the framework for today's diagnosis.)*

**Level 1 mistakes — the foundation** 🧱
*(These are the ones people notice immediately)*
- ❌ *He don't like it* · *I am agree* · *Did you went*

**Level 2 mistakes — the connectors** 🔗
*(These limit how much you can express)*
- ❌ *I am working here since 3 years* · *If I had money I will buy*

**Level 3 mistakes — the polish** ✨
*(These are subtle; only careful listeners notice)*
- ❌ *I look forward to hear* · *You'd better to go* · *One of my friends are*

*(The key insight.)*

> ### 👉 Fix Level 1 first. Never skip ahead to Level 3.

*(Explain.)* A single Level 1 error damages your credibility more than ten Level 3 errors ✅ — because Level 1 mistakes are the ones **everybody** hears. ✅

*(Emphasise.)* So today's test will tell you **which level to work on** — and that's more valuable than the score itself. 🎯

### How to take this test ✅

1. **Write your answer before I say it** ✍️
2. **Note the question number for every mistake** 📝
3. **Don't feel bad — feel curious** 😌

*(Ready?)* Let's go. 🚀

### 🧱 LEVEL 1 — Foundation (Questions 1–10)

*(If you get fewer than 8 of these, that's where all your effort should go.)*

**Q1.** *He ___ (don't / doesn't) like spicy food.*
→ ✅ **doesn't** — he/she/it takes *does* *(Day 53)*

**Q2.** *I ___ (am agree / agree) with you.*
→ ✅ **agree** — *agree* is a verb, no *be* *(Days 28, 52)*

**Q3.** *Where ___ you go yesterday? (did / do)*
→ ✅ **did** — past question needs *did*, and *go* stays plain *(Day 03)*

**Q4.** *I ___ (have / am having) two brothers.*
→ ✅ **have** — owning isn't an action *(Days 01, 54)*

**Q5.** *She ___ (work / works) at a bank.*
→ ✅ **works** — he/she/it adds -s *(Days 47, 55)*

**Q6.** *They ___ (was / were) late yesterday.*
→ ✅ **were** — *they* takes *were* *(Day 52)*

**Q7.** *I ___ (didn't went / didn't go) to the office.*
→ ✅ **didn't go** — one past per sentence *(Day 03)*

**Q8.** *I am a teacher ___ a government school. (in / at)*
→ ✅ **at** *(a specific place)* — also *"in a government school"* is acceptable ✅ *(Day 18)*

**Q9.** *There ___ (is / are) two problems with the file.*
→ ✅ **are** — plural noun follows *(Days 52, 55)*

**Q10.** *I ___ (going / am going) home now.*
→ ✅ **am going** — *-ing* always needs *be* *(Day 52)*

---

### 🔗 LEVEL 2 — Connectors (Questions 11–20)

*(This is where most Malayali learners actually sit.)*

**Q11.** *I ___ here for three years. (am working / have been working)*
→ ✅ **have been working** — duration + still true *(Days 19, 27)*

**Q12.** *I have been here ___ 2020. (since / for)*
→ ✅ **since** — a starting point *(Day 19)*

**Q13.** *If I had money, I ___ buy a car. (will / would)*
→ ✅ **would** — past form in the if-half means imaginary *(Days 07, 51)*

**Q14.** *I ___ that movie last week. (have seen / saw)*
→ ✅ **saw** — a finished time was mentioned *(Day 48)*

**Q15.** *He said he ___ come tomorrow. (will / would)*
→ ✅ **would** — reported in the past *(Days 07, 60)*

**Q16.** *When I reached, the train ___ already left. (has / had)*
→ ✅ **had** — the earlier of two past events *(Day 50)*

**Q17.** *I'm supposed ___ finish it today. (to / —)*
→ ✅ **to** — *supposed **to*** ✅ *(Day 63)*

**Q18.** *He made me ___ outside. (wait / to wait)*
→ ✅ **wait** — *make* takes a plain verb *(Day 62)*

**Q19.** *I got my hair ___ yesterday. (cut / cutted)*
→ ✅ **cut** — third form; *cut–cut–cut* *(Day 62)*

**Q20.** *You're coming tomorrow, ___ ? (isn't it / aren't you)*
→ ✅ **aren't you** — copy the helper, flip the sign *(Day 61)*

---

### ✨ LEVEL 3 — Polish (Questions 21–30)

*(These separate B1 from B2/C1. Don't worry if you miss several.)*

**Q21.** *I look forward to ___ from you. (hear / hearing)*
→ ✅ **hearing** — *to* here is a preposition *(Day 57)*

**Q22.** *One of my friends ___ a doctor. (is / are)*
→ ✅ **is** — *one* is the subject *(Day 55)*

**Q23.** *If I ___ you, I'd take the job. (was / were)*
→ ✅ **were** — the imaginary form *(Day 51)*

**Q24.** *I'd rather ___ home tonight. (stay / to stay)*
→ ✅ **stay** — *would rather* takes a plain verb *(Day 64)*

**Q25.** *I prefer tea ___ coffee. (to / than)*
→ ✅ **to** — *prefer X **to** Y* *(Day 64)*

**Q26.** *He asked me where ___ . (was I going / I was going)*
→ ✅ **I was going** — no swap in reported questions *(Days 35, 60)*

**Q27.** *I have three years of ___ . (experience / experiences)*
→ ✅ **experience** — uncountable here *(Day 56)*

**Q28.** *He suggested ___ early. (to go / going)*
→ ✅ **going** — *suggest* takes *-ing* *(Days 57, 60)*

**Q29.** *The phone ___ I bought stopped working. (which / which it)*
→ ✅ **which** — don't repeat the object *(Day 58)*

**Q30.** *Unless you ___ , you'll fail. (study / don't study)*
→ ✅ **study** — *unless* already means "if not" *(Day 26)*
