**Target length:** 13–15 minutes
**Level:** All levels

## 🎬 1. THE HOOK (0:00 – 1:00)

*(Warm, encouraging.)*

Say these four words:

> 🤔 **think** · 👉 **this** · 3️⃣ **three** · 👥 **they**

*(Pause.)*

Did they come out as **"tink," "dis," "tree,"** and **"dey"**? 🤔

*(Warm smile.)* That's the classic substitution. And here's why:

*(Explain.)* English **TH** doesn't exist in Malayalam. So your mouth reaches for the **closest** sound it knows — **ത** *(dental t)* or **ദ** *(dental d)*. ✅

*(Point at camera.)*

And sometimes it changes the word completely:

- 3️⃣ **three** vs 🌳 **tree**
- 🧵 **thin** vs 🥫 **tin**
- 👉 **they** vs 📅 **day**
- 🤔 **think** vs 🥤 **sink** *(some speakers)*

*(Lean in, genuinely encouraging.)*

But here's the good news — and this is real: **Malayalam gets you closer to this sound than almost any other language.** ✅

*(Explain.)* Because your **ത** is already made at the **teeth**. ✅ Hindi speakers, Tamil speakers, Malayalam speakers — you all have a **dental** t. Speakers of many other languages don't!

*(Emphasise.)* You're not starting from zero. You're **one small step** away. ✅

Today, that step. Let's go. 🚀

## 🧠 2. THE CORE LOGIC — Why this happens (1:00 – 5:00)

*(This is the lesson. Be precise and encouraging.)*

### 🪷 What Malayalam gives you 🎁

*(Start with the strength — this framing matters.)*

*(Explain.)* Malayalam has **four** t-type sounds. Four! ✅

| Letter | Where the tongue goes |
|---|---|
| **ത** | tongue at the **teeth** 🦷 ← *dental!* |
| **ട** | tongue **curled back** *(retroflex)* ✅ |
| **ഥ** | dental, with a **puff of air** 💨 |
| **ദ** | dental, **voiced** 🔊 |

*(Emphasise.)* **ത is a DENTAL sound.** ✅ Your tongue is already **at your teeth** — exactly the right neighbourhood for English TH. ⭐

*(Point out.)* English speakers learning Malayalam struggle with ത and ട. ✅ You already control both. 💪

### 🔍 So what's the difference?

*(The precise fix — this is the whole video.)*

*(Explain.)* One thing, and one thing only:

| Sound | Tongue position | Air |
|---|---|---|
| **ത** *(Malayalam)* | tongue **behind** the teeth 🦷 | air **blocked**, then released ✅ |
| **TH** *(English)* | tongue **between** the teeth 🦷 | air **flows continuously** 💨 ⭐ |

*(Emphasise.)*

> ### 👉 **ത STOPS the air. TH LETS IT FLOW.** 💨 ✅

*(That's it.)* Push the tongue **slightly forward** — so the tip actually **touches** or **peeks between** your teeth — and **keep the air moving**. ✅

*(Say it as the slogan.)*
> **നാവ് പുറത്ത്, കാറ്റ് പോകട്ടെ.** ✅
> **Tongue forward. Let the air flow.** 💨

### 🔊 The two TH sounds

*(Important — there are two!)*

#### 1️⃣ Voiceless TH — /θ/ — like **think** 🤔

*(No voice, just air.)*

**Words:** *think, three, thank, thin, thing, both, month, mouth, tooth, path, birthday, healthy, north, south, something, nothing* ✅

*(How to make it.)*
1. Tongue tip **between** your teeth 🦷 *(gently — just touching!)*
2. Blow air **out** 💨
3. **No voice** — just air ✅

*(Test.)* Say **"thhhhh"** ✅ Hold it 3 seconds. It should sound like a soft hiss with your tongue visible. ✅

#### 2️⃣ Voiced TH — /ð/ — like **this** 👉

*(Same position, voice turned on.)*

**Words:** *this, that, these, those, they, them, there, then, the, than, other, mother, father, brother, weather, together, either* ✅

*(How to make it.)*
1. **Same** tongue position 🦷
2. Now turn your **voice on** 🔊
3. Feel the **buzz** in your tongue ✅

*(Test.)* Say **"thhh"** *(air only)* → then **"dhhh"** *(voice on)* ✅ Feel the vibration appear. 🐝

*(Emphasise.)* Same mouth, one has **voice**, one doesn't ✅ Exactly like the **s/z** pair from Day 67! 🔗

*(The pattern!)* ⭐ Notice which words take which:
- **Voiced /ð/** → the **small grammar words**: *the, this, that, they, them, there, then* ✅
- **Voiceless /θ/** → the **content words**: *think, three, thank, month, mouth* ✅

*(Useful.)* So the most **common** TH words *(the, this, that, they)* are all **voiced** 🔊 ✅ Master that one first! ⭐

### 📄 THE PAPER TEST

*(A physical test that proves it.)*

*(Explain.)* Hold a **small strip of paper** in front of your mouth. 📄

**Say "tin"** *(Malayalam ത)* → the paper **jumps once** ✅ *(air released in a burst)*
**Say "thin"** *(English TH)* → the paper **flutters continuously** 💨 ✅ *(air flowing)*

*(Emphasise.)* **A burst is ത. A flow is TH.** ✅

*(Do it on camera.)* Let the viewers see the paper move. 📄

### 🪞 The mirror test

*(A second check.)*

> **Say "think." Look in the mirror. CAN YOU SEE YOUR TONGUE?** 🪞

- ✅ **Yes, tongue visible** → correct TH ✅
- ❌ **No, tongue hidden** → you're making ത ❌

*(Emphasise.)* **TH is the English sound where your tongue tip comes forward to the teeth.** ✅ You can literally see it in a mirror — which makes it easy to check! ⭐ *(A light touch is enough — it doesn't have to stick out far.)*

## 🔍 3. THE PRACTICE (5:00 – 11:30)

*(Drill section. Real gaps for repetition.)*

### 🅐 Step 1 — Isolate the sounds

*(Hold each for 3 seconds, five times.)*

> 💨 **thhhhh** *(air only, tongue out, no voice)* — ×5
> 🔊 **dhhhhh** *(voice on, tongue out)* — ×5

*(Alternate.)*
> **thhh — dhhh — thhh — dhhh** ✅ ×10

*(Check in the mirror each time.)* 🪞 Tongue **visible**? ✅

### 🅑 Step 2 — Single words

#### 💨 Voiceless TH — /θ/ *(air only)*

| Word | Sentence |
|---|---|
| **think** 🤔 | *I **think** so.* ⭐ |
| **thank** 🙏 | ***Thank** you.* ⭐ |
| **three** 3️⃣ | ***Three** days.* ✅ |
| **thing** | *One **thing**.* ✅ |
| **month** 📅 | *Next **month**.* ✅ |
| **birthday** 🎂 | *Happy **birthday**!* ⭐ |
| **something** | ***Something** else.* 🔗 |
| **nothing** | ***Nothing** at all.* ✅ |
| **health** | *Good **health**.* ✅ |
| **both** | ***Both** of them.* ✅ |

*(Note.)* ⚠️ TH at the **end** *(month, both, mouth, tooth)* is often dropped ✅ Make sure the tongue comes out! 🦷

#### 🔊 Voiced TH — /ð/ *(voice on)*

| Word | Sentence |
|---|---|
| **the** | ***The** file.* ⭐ *(the most common word in English!)* |
| **this** 👉 | ***This** one.* ✅ |
| **that** | ***That's** right.* ✅ |
| **they** 👥 | ***They** came.* ✅ |
| **them** | *Tell **them**.* 🔗 |
| **there** 📍 | ***There** is a problem.* 🔗 |
| **than** | *Better **than** that.* ✅ |
| **mother** 👩 | *My **mother**.* ✅ |
| **brother** 👨 | *My **brother**.* ✅ |
| **weather** 🌧️ | *Nice **weather**.* ✅ |

*(Highlight "the"!)* ⭐ **"The"** is the **most frequently used word in English.** ✅ If you fix only one TH, fix **the**. 🎯

*(Note the two pronunciations of "the"!)*
- Before a **consonant sound** → **"thuh"** ✅ *(thuh file, thuh man)*
- Before a **vowel sound** → **"thee"** ✅ *(thee apple, thee office)*

*(Note — go by SOUND, not spelling!)* ⚠️ ✅ *thee **hour*** *(the h is silent → vowel sound)* ✅ but ✅ *thuh **university*** *(it starts with a "yoo" sound)*. ⭐

*(Practise.)* **thuh book · thee apple · thuh man · thee office** ✅

| /ð/ | /d/ |
|---|---|

| **then** | **den** ✅ |
| **there** 📍 | **dare** ✅ |
| **breathe** | **breed** ✅ |

| 4 | 40 sec | One tongue twister ×5 ✅ |

### 📱 The dictation self-test

*(The honest feedback tool from Day 68.)* 🔗

*(Try these five in voice typing.)*
**think · three · they · this · month** ✅

*(And the pairs!)*
**thin** then **tin** ✅ · **three** then **tree** ✅

*(Emphasise.)* If your phone types both correctly — you've got it. ✅ 🎉

### ⚠️ Two things to avoid

**Error 1: Over-doing the tongue** 👅

*(Explain.)* Some learners push the tongue **far** out and it looks and sounds strange 😅

*(Correct.)* The tongue tip **just touches** the bottom of the top teeth ✅ **Barely visible** is enough. ✅ You don't need to stick it out. ⭐

**Error 2: Making TH into F** 💨

*(Explain.)* Some speakers over-correct into **/f/**:
- ❌ *"I fink so"* ❌ → ✅ **"I think so"** ✅
- ❌ *"free" for "three"* ❌ *(different problem, same result)*

*(The check.)* **/f/** uses **teeth on lip** 🦷 *(like /v/!)* ✅ **TH** uses **tongue between teeth** 👅 ✅ Different! ✅

### 💙 And now — the honest reassurance

*(This section matters. Be genuine.)*

*(Warmly.)* Now let me tell you something that might surprise you.

*(Direct.)* **TH is the least important pronunciation fix in this entire block.** ✅

*(Explain.)* Why? Because:

1. ✅ **Millions of native speakers don't use it either.** In London, *"think"* is often *"fink."* In Ireland, *"this"* is often *"dis."* In New York, the same. ✅
2. ✅ **Context almost always saves you.** If you say *"I tink so,"* nobody thinks you said *"I sink so"* — they hear *think*. ✅
3. ✅ **It rarely blocks understanding** the way **word stress** does. 🔗 *(Tomorrow's topic!)* ⚠️ *(It can matter in pairs like* three/tree *and* thin/tin *— so it's worth some practice.)*

*(Emphasise, warmly.)* So — practise it. It's a nice polish. But **do not** lose sleep over it. ✅

*(Firmly.)* And **please** don't let it stop you from speaking. A confident *"I tink so"* is worth far more than a silent, perfect TH. ⭐ 🔗 *(Day 36 — flow beats accuracy!)*

### 🎯 The priority order — where TH really matters

*(Practical and honest.)*

*(Explain.)* If you want to be efficient, focus your TH practice here: ✅

| Priority | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ | **the, this, that, they** | you say these constantly ✅ |
| ⭐⭐ | **think, thank you, three** | very frequent ✅ |
| ⭐ | *month, birthday, health* | occasional ✅ |
| — | *thirty-third, northern* | rare — ignore ✅ |

*(Emphasise.)* **Four words: the, this, that, they.** ✅ Master those and you've covered a huge share of the TH sounds you'll say every day. 🎯

*(Practise those four, ten times each.)* 🗣️ **the · this · that · they** ✅
