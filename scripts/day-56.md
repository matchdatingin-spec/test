# Day 56 — Information or Informations? 30 Uncountable Noun Mistakes

**Target length:** 13–15 minutes
**Level:** A2 / B1
**One-line promise:** English divides nouns into **things you can count** and **stuff you can only measure**. Learn the split, and 30 mistakes disappear.

---

## 🎬 1. THE HOOK (0:00 – 1:00)

*(Energetic, everyday.)*

You want to ask your colleague for help. And you say:

> ❌ *"Can you give me an advice?"*

*(Shake head, smiling.)* Or in an email:

> ❌ *"I need some informations about the project."*
> ❌ *"I have two good news!"*
> ❌ *"We bought new furnitures for the office."*
> ❌ *"He gave me many advices."*

*(Point at camera.)*

Four sentences. **Three unnecessary S's** — *informations, furnitures, advices* — plus one *"two good news"* that needs a completely different fix. ✅

*(Lean in.)*

And why do we all do this? Because in Malayalam, **you can pluralise almost anything**:

- **വിവരങ്ങൾ** → informations 📋
- **ഉപദേശങ്ങൾ** → advices 💬
- **ഫർണിച്ചറുകൾ** → furnitures 🪑

*(Emphasise.)* Malayalam is **generous** with plurals. English is **strangely picky**. ✅

*(Confident.)*

Today: the logic behind the split, **30 nouns** to fix, and a trick for counting things English says you can't count. Let's go. 🚀

## 🧠 2. THE CORE LOGIC (1:00 – 4:00)

*(Slow. The logic is genuinely interesting.)*

### 🔑 The question: can I put a NUMBER in front of it?

> **✅ Yes → COUNTABLE** *(one chair, two chairs)*
> **❌ No → UNCOUNTABLE** *(one water? two waters? ❌)*

*(Test it.)*
- *chair* → one chair, two chairs ✅ **countable**
- *water* → ❌ one water ❌ **uncountable**
- *idea* → one idea, two ideas ✅ **countable**
- *advice* → ❌ one advice ❌ **uncountable**

### Why *advice* but not *idea*? The logic 💡

*(This is the interesting part — spend time here.)*

*(Explain.)* English thinks about nouns in two ways:

**COUNTABLE = separate objects with edges** 📦
You can point at one, then the next. They have **boundaries**.
- chairs, phones, ideas, problems, questions ✅

**UNCOUNTABLE = a mass, a substance, a category** ☁️
It has **no natural edges** — you can only measure it, not count it.
- water 💧, rice 🍚, money 💰, information 📋, advice 💬

*(The insight.)* So English is asking: **"Does this thing come in natural units?"** ✅

- **Chairs** → yes, one chair is obviously one thing ✅
- **Advice** → no! Where does one piece of advice end and the next begin? It's a **flow** of guidance ☁️

*(Point out.)* That's why *information* is uncountable — it's treated as a **substance**, like water. You can have *a lot of* information, or *a piece of* information, but not *"three informations."* ✅

*(Slogan.)*
> **Objects with edges → count them. Substances → measure them.** ✅
> **Countable = എണ്ണാം. Uncountable = അളക്കാം.**

### The 4 rules for uncountable nouns 📋

*(The practical consequences — all four matter.)*

**Rule 1: No -s in the uncountable meaning.** ❌
- ❌ informations · ✅ **information** ✅

**Rule 2: No "a / an."** ❌
- ❌ an advice · ✅ **some advice** ✅

**Rule 3: Use a SINGULAR verb.** ✅ 🔗 *(Day 55)*
- ✅ *The information **is** correct.* ✅
- ✅ *The news **is** good.* ✅

**Rule 4: Use *much*, not *many*.** ✅ 🔗 *(Day 21)*
- ❌ many information · ✅ **much information** / **a lot of information** ✅

*(Emphasise.)* Four rules, one cause. Learn the noun's **type**, and all four follow automatically. ✅

## 🔍 3. SENTENCE BREAKDOWN (4:00 – 11:00)

### Sentence 1 — built piece by piece

**Malayalam:** എനിക്ക് ഒരു ഉപദേശം വേണം.

*(Notice.)* **ഒരു ഉപദേശം** = "one advice" ✅ — Malayalam counts it happily! ✅

Build it:

1. **Is *advice* countable in English?** → **No** ☁️
2. **So no "a," no -s** ✅
3. **What replaces "a"?** → **some** ✅
4. **Polite request** 🔗 *(Day 42)* → Could you give me...

> ✅ **"Could you give me some advice?"** ⭐

*(Other natural versions.)*
- ✅ *Can I ask your advice?* ⭐
- ✅ *I'd like your advice on something.* ✅
- ✅ *Could I get some advice from you?* ✅

*(And if you really need to count it — the container trick!)* 📦

> ✅ **"Could you give me a piece of advice?"** ✅

*(Explain the trick.)* When you need to count uncountable stuff, English adds a **container word** or **unit word**:

| Uncountable | Container word | ✅ Result |
|---|---|---|
| advice 💬 | piece | **a piece of advice** ⭐ |
| water 💧 | glass | **a glass of water** ⭐ |
| information 📋 | piece / bit | **a piece of information** |
| news 📰 | piece | **a piece of news** |
| furniture 🪑 | piece | **two pieces of furniture** |
| bread 🍞 | slice / loaf | **a slice of bread** |
| tea ☕ | cup | **a cup of tea** ⭐ |
| rice 🍚 | plate / kilo | **a plate of rice** |
| luggage 🧳 | piece | **three pieces of luggage** |
| work 💼 | bit / lot | **a bit of work** |

*(Emphasise.)* **"a piece of ___"** ⭐ works for most abstract uncountables. Learn that one pattern. ✅

*(Practise.)* **A piece of advice. A piece of information. A piece of news.** ✅ Say each three times. 🗣️

### Sentence 2 — built piece by piece

**Malayalam:** എനിക്ക് രണ്ട് നല്ല വാർത്തകൾ ഉണ്ട്.

Your brain: ❌ *"I have two good news."*

*(Three problems!)*
1. *news* can't take a **number** ❌
2. *news* can't take **-s** ❌ *(it already looks plural but is singular!)*
3. *news* takes a **singular** verb ✅

Build it:

1. **Uncountable** → no number ✅
2. **Use "some"** → some good news
3. **Who** → I
4. **have** → have

> ✅ **"I have some good news!"** ⭐

*(And if you must count:)*
> ✅ **"I have two pieces of good news."** ✅ *(correct but formal)*
> ✅ **"I have two things to tell you — both good!"** ⭐ *(much more natural!)*

*(Point out.)* Sometimes the **best** fix isn't a container word — it's **restructuring** the sentence. ✅

*(The "news" special notes.)* ⚠️
- ✅ *The news **is** good.* ✅ *(singular verb!)*
- ❌ *The news are good.* ❌
- ✅ *That's great news!* ✅ *(no "a"!)*
- ❌ *That's a great news.* ❌

*(Emphasise.)* ❌ *"a good news"* is one of the most common Malayali errors. ✅ Say **"good news"** or **"some good news."** ✅

### ⚠️ The tricky ones — nouns that can be BOTH

*(Genuinely useful and often missed.)*

*(Explain.)* A few nouns are uncountable as a **substance** but countable as a **type or instance**:

| Noun | ☁️ Uncountable | 📦 Countable |
|---|---|---|
| **experience** | *I have experience in sales.* ✅ | *I had a strange experience.* ✅ |
| **work** | *I have a lot of work.* ✅ | *Shakespeare's works* ✅ *(writings)* |
| **time** | *I don't have time.* ✅ | *I've been there three times.* ✅ |
| **water** | *I need water.* ✅ | *Two waters, please.* ✅ *(= bottles, in a café)* |
| **coffee** | *I like coffee.* ✅ | *Two coffees, please.* ✅ *(= cups)* |
| **hair** | *She has long hair.* ✅ | *There's a hair in my food!* ✅ 🤢 |

*(Highlight "experience" — this matters for interviews!)* ⭐

- ✅ *I have three years of **experience**.* ✅ *(uncountable — your total)*
- ❌ *I have three years of experience**s**.* ❌
- ✅ *I've had some interesting **experiences** at work.* ✅ *(countable — separate events)*

*(Emphasise.)* On your resume, always **"experience"** — no S. ✅

*(And in cafés!)* ✅ *"Two coffees, please"* ✅ is completely correct — because you mean **two cups**. English allows that shortcut. ☕

## ⚠️ 4. THE MALAYALI TRAP (11:00 – 13:00)

### Trap 1: Malayalam pluralises freely

*(The root cause.)*

*(Explain.)* Malayalam has **-കൾ / -ങ്ങൾ** and applies it generously:

| Malayalam | ❌ Direct | ✅ English |
|---|---|---|
| വിവരങ്ങൾ | informations | **information** |
| ഉപദേശങ്ങൾ | advices | **advice** |
| ജോലികൾ | works | **work** / **tasks** ✅ |
| വാർത്തകൾ | newses | **news** |
| ഫർണിച്ചറുകൾ | furnitures | **furniture** |

*(Note "ജോലികൾ"!)* ✅ If you genuinely mean **separate jobs**, English has countable alternatives:
- ✅ *I have a lot of **work**.* ☁️ *(general)*
- ✅ *I have three **tasks** to finish.* 📦 *(specific, countable!)* ⭐
- ✅ *I have two **jobs**.* 📦 *(two employments)* ✅

*(Emphasise.)* **When you need to count, switch to a countable synonym!** ✅ That's often cleaner than "pieces of." ⭐

| ☁️ Uncountable | 📦 Countable alternative |
|---|---|
| work | **task, job, assignment** ⭐ |
| information | **detail, fact, point** ⭐ |
| advice | **tip, suggestion, recommendation** ⭐ |
| news | **story, update, announcement** ⭐ |
| furniture | **chair, table, item** ✅ |
| equipment | **tool, device, machine** ✅ |
| luggage | **bag, suitcase** ✅ |

*(Point out.)* ✅ *"He gave me three **suggestions**"* ✅ is much more natural than *"three pieces of advice."* ⭐

### Trap 2: "a" before uncountable nouns

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| a good news | **good news** / **some good news** |
| an advice | **some advice** / **a piece of advice** |
| a information | **some information** |
| a furniture | **a piece of furniture** / **an item of furniture** |
| a work | **some work** / **a task** |
| a luggage | **a bag** / **a piece of luggage** |

*(The self-check.)*

> **Can I say "two ___"?** ❌ **No → then no "a" either.** ✅

### Trap 3: Plural verbs with uncountable nouns 🔗 *(Day 55)*

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| The information are correct | **The information is correct** |
| The news are good | **The news is good** |
| My luggage are heavy | **My luggage is heavy** |
| The furniture are new | **The furniture is new** |
| The traffic were bad | **The traffic was bad** |

*(Rule.)* **Uncountable = always singular verb.** ✅

### Trap 4: "-s" words that aren't plural ⚠️

*(A confusing subgroup.)*

| Word | Looks | Actually | Verb |
|---|---|---|---|
| **news** | plural | singular | **is** ✅ |
| **mathematics** | plural | singular | **is** ✅ |
| **physics / economics** | plural | singular | **is** ✅ |
| **politics** | plural | singular | **is** ✅ |

*(And the reverse!)*

| Word | Looks | Actually | Verb |
|---|---|---|---|
| **people** | singular-ish | plural | **are** ✅ |
| **police** | singular | plural | **are** ✅ |
| **children** | — | plural | **are** ✅ |
| **staff** | singular | usually singular | **is** ✅ |

*(Emphasise.)* ❌ *"Peoples"* *(for individuals)* ❌ and ❌ *"childrens"* ❌ are **double plurals**. Don't use them. ✅ *(For the record: **peoples** exists only for nations/ethnic groups — 🔗 Day 55.)*

### Bonus: the Indian-English uncountables 🇮🇳

*(Fun and practical.)*

| ❌ Common in India | ✅ International |
|---|---|
| softwares | **software** |
| staffs | **staff / staff members** ⭐ |
| trainings | **training / training sessions** |
| feedbacks | **feedback** |
| researches | **research / research studies** |
| aircrafts | **aircraft** ✅ *(same in plural!)* |
| infrastructures | **infrastructure** |

*(Note "staff.")* ✅ If you mean individual people, say **"staff members"** or **"employees."** ⭐
- ❌ *We have 50 staffs.* ❌
- ✅ **We have 50 staff members.** ✅
- ✅ **We have a staff of 50.** ✅

### The 3-second self-check 🎯

Before any noun, ask:

1. **Can I put a number in front?** → countable 📦 → *-s* is fine ✅
2. **Can't count it?** → uncountable ☁️ → **no -s, no "a," singular verb, use "much/some"** ✅
3. **Need to count it anyway?** → add **"a piece of"** or **switch to a countable synonym** ⭐

*(Drill — I say, you correct.)*
- *"I need some informations."* → **information** ✅
- *"He gave me two advices."* → **two suggestions** / **some advice** ✅
- *"I have a good news."* → **good news** ✅
- *"We bought new furnitures."* → **furniture** ✅
- *"The traffics were bad."* → **traffic was** ✅
