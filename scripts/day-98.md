## 🧠 2. THE CORE LOGIC (1:00 – 5:30)

*(Establish the design principles — so they can adapt it.)*

### 🎯 The three design rules

*(Explain why the plan looks the way it does.)*

#### Rule 1: Twenty minutes, not two hours ⏰

*(Explain.)* 🔗 *(Day 45)* A plan you **abandon** on Day 4 delivers zero. ✅ A plan you **keep** for 90 days delivers everything. ⭐

*(Firmly.)* **Twenty minutes.** ✅ Non-negotiable, and deliberately small. ✅

#### Rule 2: Sixty percent output 📤

*(Explain.)* 🔗 *(Days 45, 97)* Twelve of the twenty minutes must be **your voice or your writing.** ✅

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ This is the rule most plans get wrong, and it's the one that decides whether you improve. ⭐

#### Rule 3: One focus per week 🎯

*(Explain.)* Trying to fix speaking, writing, listening and pronunciation **simultaneously** produces no visible progress in any of them — which is demoralising. ✅

*(Firmly.)* ⭐ **One focus per week.** ✅ The rest stays on maintenance. ⭐

### ⏰ The daily 20 minutes

*(The fixed core. Show as a graphic.)*

| Block | Time | What | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 min | **Shadowing** 🔗 *(Day 44)* | 📤 |
| 2 | 4 min | **Narrate your day** aloud 🔗 *(Day 37)* | 📤 |
| 3 | 4 min | **This week's focus** *(varies)* | 📤/📥 |
| 4 | 3 min | **Listening decode** 🔗 *(Day 43)* | 📥 |
| 5 | 3 min | **3 new words**, used 🔗 *(Day 77)* | 📥/📤 |
| 6 | 3 min | **Write 5 sentences** ✍️ | 📤 |

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ **Output = 13 minutes of 20.** ✅ That's 65%. ✅

*(Firmly.)* Only **Block 3** changes week to week. ✅ Everything else stays identical for 90 days — because **repetition** is the mechanism. ⭐

### 📊 The four things you'll measure

*(Measurement is what sustains motivation.)*

| Metric | How | Target change |
|---|---|---|
| **Freeze count** 🧊 | pauses in your 2-min recording | 8 → **2** ⭐ |
| **Speaking time** ⏱️ | how long without stopping | 45 sec → **3 min** ⭐ |
| **Words used** 📚 | count your notebook | → **250 active words** ✅ |
| **Real uses** 🤝 | English interactions per week | 0 → **7** ⭐ |

*(Firmly.)* ⭐ **Record your baseline today.** ✅ 🔗 *(Day 45)* Two minutes on *"tell me about yourself."* Don't delete it. ⭐

*(Slogan.)*
> **Twenty minutes. Sixty percent output. One focus a week.** ⭐

## 🔍 3. THE 12-WEEK PLAN (5:30 – 15:00)

*(The core deliverable. Hold each phase on screen.)*

---

### 🌱 PHASE 1 — WEEKS 1–4: Build the habit and unfreeze

*(Goal: stop freezing. Nothing else.)*

#### 📅 Week 1 — Just show up

**Focus:** the habit itself ✅
**Block 3:** re-watch **Day 44** and shadow Block 5 *(interview answers)* 🗣️
**Extra:** change your **phone to English** 🔗 *(Day 37)* 📱
**Real use:** none required ✅

*(Warmly.)* ⭐ Week 1's only job is **not missing two days in a row.** ✅ That's the whole target. ⭐

**Expect:** awkwardness. Forgetting. Feeling silly talking to yourself. ✅ All normal. 💙

#### 📅 Week 2 — Add the emergency phrases

**Focus:** never freezing 🧊 🔗 *(Day 38)*
**Block 3:** memorise the **8 emergency phrases** until automatic ⭐
**Real use:** **one** English interaction — a shop, a message ✅

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ *"That's a good question — let me think for a second."* ✅ Say it 20 times. ⭐

**Expect:** the first real interaction will feel enormous. It gets smaller fast. ✅

#### 📅 Week 3 — The 2-minute formula

**Focus:** speaking length 🔗 *(Day 36)*
**Block 3:** **P-E-R-F-O** on one topic daily, recorded 🎙️
**Real use:** **three** interactions ✅

**Aim for:** ⭐ 90 seconds without stopping by the end of the week. ✅

#### 📅 Week 4 — Storytelling + first checkpoint

**Focus:** S-P-A-R-K 🔗 *(Day 40)*
**Block 3:** tell **one story from your day**, out loud, nightly 📖
**Real use:** **five** interactions ✅
**⭐ CHECKPOINT:** re-record the baseline topic. Compare with Day 1. 📊

*(Firmly.)* ⭐ **Do the comparison.** ✅ Hearing four weeks of change is what carries you through weeks 5 to 12. ⭐

**Typical picture after Phase 1:** noticeably fewer freezes. Speaking around 90 seconds. Habit established. ✅ *(Your own numbers will vary — compare with **your** Day 1, not with anyone else.)*

---

### 🏗️ PHASE 2 — WEEKS 5–8: Fix the leaks

*(Goal: accuracy on the errors that get noticed.)*

#### 📅 Week 5 — The helper verbs

**Focus:** am/is/are · do/does/did · have/has/had 🔗 *(Days 52–54)*
**Block 3:** the **question-flip drill** — 10 pairs daily ⭐
**Real use:** 7 interactions + **one voice note** 🎙️

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ This week fixes *"I am agree," "he don't," "did you went."* ✅ The errors people **hear**. ⭐

#### 📅 Week 6 — Small words

**Focus:** in/on/at · since/for 🔗 *(Days 18, 19)*
**Block 3:** learn **phrases**, not rules — *at home, on the bus, for three years* ⭐
**Real use:** 7 interactions ✅

*(Firmly.)* ⭐ 🔗 *(Day 91)* These are among the **most frequent** errors we see in the comments. ✅ Arguably the highest-value week in the plan. ⭐

#### 📅 Week 7 — Writing

**Focus:** professional email 🔗 *(Days 83, 92)*
**Block 3:** rewrite **one real email** daily 📧
**Real use:** send **three** emails in the new style ⭐

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ Fastest-improving skill in the whole plan. ✅ Mostly vocabulary substitution. ⭐

#### 📅 Week 8 — Pronunciation + checkpoint

**Focus:** word stress + chunking 🔗 *(Days 70, 71, 96)*
**Block 3:** **tap-the-stress** on 5 work words + chunk your 3 key sentences 👋
**⭐ CHECKPOINT:** re-record. Re-do **Day 65's** grammar test. 📊

**Expect after Phase 2:** noticeably fewer errors. Emails look professional. Speech is chunked. ✅

## ⚠️ 4. THE FAILURE MODES (15:00 – 18:00)

*(Section 4 adapts — how to survive the plan. This is what makes it stick.)*

### ⚠️ Failure mode 1: The Monday start 📅

*(The classic killer.)* 🔗 *(Day 45)*

*(Explain.)* **"അടുത്ത തിങ്കളാഴ്ച തുടങ്ങാം."** ✅ And then next Monday. And next month. ⚠️

**The fix:** ⭐ Start **today**, with Block 2 only. ✅ Four minutes. Narrate your day. ✅

*(Firmly.)* **Starting** is the hard part, not continuing. ⭐

### ⚠️ Failure mode 2: The perfectionist restart 🔄

*(The most damaging.)*

*(Explain.)* You do 11 days perfectly. You miss Day 12 — a wedding, a fever, work. ✅ And you decide to **restart from Week 1 next month.** ⚠️

*(Firmly.)*

> ### 👉 **Never miss two days in a row.** ✅ That's the only rule. ⭐

*(The emergency version.)* On a terrible day: **2 minutes, narrate your day, done.** ✅ The chain survives. 🔗

### ⚠️ Failure mode 3: Week 5–6 boredom 😴

*(An honest warning about the middle.)*

*(Explain.)* Weeks 1–4 feel exciting. ✅ Weeks 9–12 feel purposeful. ✅

*(Firmly.)* **Weeks 5–8 feel like nothing is happening.** ⚠️ Because progress in the middle is invisible day to day. ✅

*(The fix — three things.)*
1. ⭐ **Trust the Week 4 recording.** ✅ You have evidence it works. 🎙️
2. ✅ Change your **content**, not your plan — new videos to shadow, new topics ✅
3. ⭐ Find **one person** to practise with, even ten minutes weekly 🤝

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ **Everyone** finds weeks 5–8 dull. ✅ It's not a sign the plan is wrong. It's the middle of every plan. ⭐

### ⚠️ Failure mode 4: Collecting instead of doing 📦

*(The input trap, one final time.)* 🔗 *(Days 45, 77, 97)*

*(Firmly.)* ⭐ **For 90 days, watch no other English-learning content.** ✅

*(Explain.)* You have 100 videos. That's more material than 90 days needs. ✅ Adding more channels is **collecting**, and collecting feels like progress while producing none. ⚠️

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ **You don't need more information. You need repetition.** 🔁

### ⚠️ Failure mode 5: Practising your strongest skill 💪

*(From Day 94.)* 🔗

*(Explain.)* If your grammar is 9 and your speaking is 3, more grammar **feels** productive. ✅ It changes nothing. ⚠️

*(Firmly.)* ⭐ **Go where it's uncomfortable.** ✅ That's where the marks are. ⭐
