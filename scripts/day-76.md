## 🧠 2. THE CORE LOGIC (1:00 – 4:00)

*(Explain what they are and why they're hard.)*

### 🔗 What is a phrasal verb?

*(Simple definition.)*

> **A phrasal verb = a verb + a small word, where the meaning CHANGES.** ✅

- *look* 👀 = to see ✅
- *look **into*** 🔍 = to investigate ⭐ *(completely different!)*
- *look **after*** 👶 = to take care of ⭐
- *look **for*** 🔎 = to search ⭐
- *look **up*** 📖 = to find information ⭐
- *look **forward to*** 😊 = to anticipate ⭐ 🔗 *(Day 57!)*

*(Emphasise.)* **One verb. Six meanings.** ✅ The small word does all the work. ✅

### 🪷 Why this is hard for Malayalam speakers

*(The structural reason.)*

*(Explain.)* Malayalam builds meaning by **changing the verb** or adding a **verb ending**: ✅

| Malayalam | Method |
|---|---|
| നോക്കുക | look ✅ |
| അന്വേഷിക്കുക | investigate *(a different verb)* ✅ |
| സൂക്ഷിക്കുക | take care *(a different verb)* ✅ |

*(Emphasise.)* Malayalam uses a **new word** for each meaning. ✅ English uses **the same verb + a different particle**. 🔄 ✅

*(The consequence.)* So your instinct is to look for a **single big word** — and English speakers are using **two small ones**. ✅

*(And the second difficulty.)* The meaning is often **not guessable**:
- *put **off*** 🤔 = postpone ✅ *(nothing to do with "off"!)*
- *give **up*** ✅ = quit
- *turn **down*** ✅ = reject

*(Firmly, honestly.)* **You can't reliably work these out from the parts.** ✅ Learn them as **whole units**, like vocabulary. ⭐ *(Some are transparent — "come in," "sit down" — but the idiomatic ones must be memorised.)*

*(Slogan.)*
> **Learn the whole phrase, not the parts.** 🧱
> **മുഴുവൻ വാക്യം പഠിക്കുക — ഭാഗങ്ങൾ അല്ല.** ✅

### ⚠️ The one grammar rule you need

*(Practical — where does the object go?)*

*(Explain.)* Some phrasal verbs can be **split** by the object. Some can't. ✅

**Separable** ✅ *(object can go in the middle)*
- ✅ *Turn **off** the light.* ✅
- ✅ *Turn the light **off**.* ✅ *(both fine!)*
- ⚠️ *Turn **it** off.* ✅ ← **pronoun MUST go in the middle!**
- ❌ *Turn off it.* ❌

**Inseparable** ✅ *(object always after)*
- ✅ *Look **into** the problem.* ✅
- ❌ *Look the problem into.* ❌

*(The practical rule — this is all you need.)*

> ### 👉 With a **pronoun** *(it, him, them)*, put it in the **middle** if the verb allows splitting. ✅

- ✅ *Turn **it** off.* ✅ · *Pick **her** up.* ✅ · *Give **it** up.* ✅
- ❌ *Turn off it.* ❌ · ❌ *Pick up her.* ❌

*(Emphasise.)* ⭐ **"Turn it off," "pick him up," "sort it out."** ✅ Pronoun in the middle. ✅

## ⚠️ 4. THE MALAYALI TRAP (11:30 – 14:00)

### Trap 1: Reaching for the big word

*(The core habit.)*

*(Explain.)* Kerala's English education rewards **formal vocabulary**. ✅ Exams gave marks for *"utilise"* over *"use."* ✅ So your instinct in a professional moment is to reach **upward**. ✅

*(Emphasise.)* And in **spoken** English, that instinct makes you sound **stiff** — like you're reading from a letter. 📜 ✅

*(The upgrades.)*
| ⚠️ Stiff | ✅ Natural |
|---|---|
| I shall revert to you | **I'll get back to you** ⭐ 🔗 |
| Kindly investigate the matter | **Could you look into it?** ⭐ |
| I will rectify the issue | **I'll sort it out** ⭐ |
| Please intimate me | **Let me know** ⭐ 🔗 |
| We must postpone | **Let's push it back** ⭐ |

*(Emphasise.)* 🔗 *(Day 41's principle — shorter, warmer!)* ✅

### Trap 2: Wrong particle ⚠️

*(A precision issue.)*

*(Explain.)* The **particle** is not optional or interchangeable ✅ Change it, change the meaning: ✅

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *look **into*** 🔍 | investigate ✅ |
| *look **after*** 👶 | take care of ✅ |
| *look **for*** 🔎 | search ✅ |
| *look **up*** 📖 | find information ✅ |
| *look **out*** ⚠️ | be careful! ✅ |
| *look **over*** ✅ | review quickly ✅ |
| *look **down on*** 😞 | consider inferior ✅ |

*(Emphasise.)* ⚠️ ⚠️ *"I'll look after the problem"* ✅ means you'll **care** for the problem 😄 ✅ Say **"look into."** ✅

*(Another important set.)*
| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *take **off*** ✈️ | remove / plane departs ✅ |
| *take **on*** 💼 | accept work ✅ |
| *take **over*** ✅ | assume control |
| *take **up*** ✅ | start a hobby |
| *take **out*** ✅ | remove |
| *take **after*** ✅ | resemble a parent |

*(Firmly.)* **Learn the whole phrase.** ✅ Never guess the particle. ⭐

### Trap 3: Pronoun position ⚠️

*(The grammar error.)*

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| Turn off it | **Turn it off** ✅ |
| Pick up me at 8 | **Pick me up at 8** ✅ |
| Sort out it | **Sort it out** ✅ |
| I'll figure out it | **I'll figure it out** ✅ |
| Give up it | **Give it up** ✅ |

*(Rule.)* ⭐ **Pronoun goes in the MIDDLE.** ✅ Always, with separable verbs. ✅

*(Practise these five.)* **turn it off · pick me up · sort it out · figure it out · give it up** ✅

*(But with inseparable ones, don't split!)*
- ✅ *look **into it*** ✅ *(not "look it into")* ❌
- ✅ *deal **with it*** ✅
- ✅ *get **along with him*** ✅

*(The quick test.)* If the verb has **two** particles *(get along with, run out of, come up with)* → **never split** ✅

### Trap 4: Over-loading a sentence 🔗

*(A useful warning.)*

*(Explain.)* Once you learn phrasal verbs, don't cram them: ✅

- ⚠️ *"I'll look into it, sort it out, follow up, get back to you, and wrap it up."* 😅

*(Emphasise.)* **One or two per sentence.** ✅ Natural, not performed. 🔗 *(Day 34's principle!)* ✅

### Bonus: separable vs inseparable — a quick reference 📋

*(Practical.)*

**Separable** ✅ *(pronoun in the middle)*
*turn on/off · pick up · drop off · put off · sort out · figure out · bring up · point out · give up · turn down · hand over · take on*

**Inseparable** ✅ *(object always after)*
*look into · deal with · get along with · run out of · come up with · look after · carry on with · get back to*

*(Simple pattern!)* ⭐ **Verbs with two particles are almost always inseparable.** ✅

### 📝 How to actually learn these 🔗 *(preview of Day 77)*

*(Practical method.)*

*(Explain.)* Don't make a list of 25. ✅ Instead: ✅

1. **Pick 3** that fit **your** job or life ✅
2. Write **one real sentence** for each — about **your** situation ⭐
3. Use them **this week** ✅
4. Next week, pick 3 more ✅

*(Emphasise.)* **Three a week = 150 a year.** ✅ That's genuinely fluent-level coverage. ⭐
