# Day 62 — Make, Let, Have, or Get? Causative Verbs Made Simple

**Target length:** 12–14 minutes
**Level:** B1 / B2
**One-line promise:** Four verbs for "somebody else did it" — and the amount of **force** in each one decides which you use.

---

## 🎬 1. THE HOOK (0:00 – 1:00)

*(Everyday, relatable.)*

You went to the barber. Now you're telling a friend. And you say:

> ⚠️ *"I cut my hair yesterday."* ✂️

*(Pause. Amused.)* Really? **You** cut it? With your own scissors, looking in the mirror? 😄

Because that's exactly what that sentence means! ✅

The correct sentence:

> ✅ **"I got my hair cut yesterday."** ⭐

*(Point at camera.)*

And the same problem here:

> ⚠️ *"I repaired my bike."* → you fixed it yourself 🔧
> ✅ *"I got my bike repaired."* → the mechanic did it ⭐

*(Lean in.)*

In Malayalam, this is easy — **മുടി വെട്ടിച്ചു**, **ബൈക്ക് നന്നാക്കിച്ചു**. That **-ഇച്ചു** ending means *"I caused it to be done."* ✅ One tiny ending. ✅

*(Emphasise.)* English needs a whole **structure** for that. Four of them, actually. Today: make, let, have, get. Let's go. 🚀

---

## 🧠 2. THE CORE LOGIC (1:00 – 4:00)

*(Slow. The "force" idea organises everything.)*

### What is a causative? 🔧

*(Simple definition.)*

> **A causative = you don't do the action. You CAUSE it to happen.** ✅

- ✅ *I **made** him apologise.* → I forced it 💪
- ✅ *I **let** him go.* → I allowed it 🚪
- ✅ *I **had** the car washed.* → I arranged it 📋
- ✅ *I **got** my hair cut.* → I arranged it ✂️

**Malayalam:** the **-ഇപ്പിക്കുക / -ഇക്കുക** endings ✅ *(ചെയ്യിപ്പിച്ചു, വെട്ടിച്ചു, പറയിപ്പിച്ചു)*

### 🔑 The force meter 💪

*(This organises all four.)*

| Verb | Force | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **make** | 💪💪💪 strong | force / compel |
| **have** | 💪💪 neutral | arrange (with authority) |
| **get** | 💪 gentle | arrange / persuade |
| **let** | 🚪 zero | allow / permit |

*(Emphasise.)* **make = force. let = allow. have/get = arrange.** ✅

### And now — the grammar oddity ⚠️

*(This is what people get wrong.)*

*(Show the pattern.)*

| Verb | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **make** | make + person + **plain verb** ✅ | *He made me **wait**.* |
| **let** | let + person + **plain verb** ✅ | *She let me **go**.* |
| **have** | have + person + **plain verb** ✅ | *I had him **check** it.* |
| **get** | get + person + **TO** + verb ⚠️ | *I got him **to check** it.* |

*(Point out.)* **With a person: make / let / have take a plain verb, and only "get" takes "to."** ✅ *(Two notes for later: with a **thing** — "get it repaired" — there's no *to*; and in the **passive**, *made* does take one: ✅ "I was **made to** wait.")*

*(Say the slogan.)*
> **make · let · have → no "to." get → "to."** ✅

*(The memory trick.)* 🔑 **"get" is the odd one out.** ✅ Three friends and one outsider. 😄

### The second structure: THINGS, not people 🔧

*(This is the more useful one for daily life!)*

*(Explain.)* When you focus on the **object** rather than the person:

> ### 👉 have / get + **THING** + **V3** *(third form)* ✅

- ✅ *I got my hair **cut**.* ✂️
- ✅ *I had my car **repaired**.* 🔧
- ✅ *I need to get this **printed**.* 🖨️
- ✅ *She had her passport **renewed**.* ✅

*(Emphasise.)* **Thing + third form.** ✅ And here **get** takes **no "to"** — because there's no person! ✅

*(Compare the two shapes.)*
| Focus | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **the person** 👤 | get + person + **to** + verb | *I got the mechanic **to fix** it.* |
| **the thing** 🔧 | get + thing + **V3** | *I got my bike **fixed**.* ⭐ |

*(Point out.)* The **second** one is far more common in real speech ✅ — because usually you don't care **who** did it. ⭐

## 🔍 3. SENTENCE BREAKDOWN (4:00 – 11:00)

### Sentence 1 — built piece by piece

**Malayalam:** ഞാൻ ഇന്നലെ മുടി വെട്ടിച്ചു.

*(Notice the Malayalam!)* **വെട്ടിച്ചു** — look at that **-ഇച്ചു** ✅

*(Compare.)*
- **വെട്ടി** → I cut it *(myself)* ✂️
- **വെട്ടിച്ചു** → I had it cut *(someone else did it)* ⭐

*(Excited.)* So Malayalam marks this **beautifully** — with one syllable! ✅ You already know the concept perfectly. ✅

Build it:

1. **Focus on the thing or the person?** → the thing *(my hair)* 🔧
2. **have or get?** → *get* is more natural in speech ✅
3. **Structure** → get + thing + V3
4. **V3 of "cut"** → cut *(cut–cut–cut!)* ✅

> ✅ **"I got my hair cut yesterday."** ⭐

*(Also correct.)*
- ✅ *I **had** my hair cut yesterday.* ✅ *(slightly more formal)*

*(Practise the "get + thing + V3" pattern — say these six.)*
1. *I got my bike **serviced**.* 🔧 ✅
2. *I need to get this **printed**.* 🖨️ ✅
3. *She had her house **painted**.* 🎨 ✅
4. *I'm getting my phone **repaired**.* 📱 ✅
5. *We had the documents **verified**.* 📄 ✅
6. *I must get my visa **renewed**.* ✈️ ✅

*(Emphasise.)* This structure is **everywhere** in daily life ✅ — barber, mechanic, tailor, printing shop, government office. ⭐

*(A Kerala-specific set!)* 🇮🇳
- ✅ *I got my documents **attested**.* ✅
- ✅ *I need to get my certificate **apostilled**.* ✅
- ✅ *I had my measurements **taken** at the tailor.* ✅
- ✅ *I got the AC **serviced**.* ✅

### Sentence 2 — built piece by piece

**Malayalam:** എന്റെ manager എന്നെ overtime ചെയ്യിപ്പിച്ചു.

*(Find the clue.)* **ചെയ്യിപ്പിച്ചു** — the **-ഇപ്പിച്ചു** ending = **forced/caused** ✅ 💪

*(Which verb?)* Strong force → **make** ✅ 💪💪💪

Build it:

1. **Who caused it** → My manager
2. **make, past** → made
3. **Person** → me
4. **PLAIN verb** *(no "to"!)* → work
5. **What** → overtime

> ✅ **"My manager made me work overtime."** ✅

*(The critical error!)* ⚠️
- ❌ *made me **to** work* ❌
- ✅ **made me work** ✅

*(Practise "make" — say these five.)*
1. *He made me **wait** for an hour.* ✅
2. *The film made me **cry**.* 😢 ✅
3. *Don't make me **repeat** it.* ✅
4. *She made him **apologise**.* ✅
5. *What made you **change** your mind?* ⭐

*(Highlight #5!)* ⭐ **"What made you...?"** is a lovely natural question ✅
- ✅ *What made you choose this course?* ⭐ *(great interview question!)*
- ✅ *What made you laugh?* ✅

*(⚠️ The passive exception!)* When *make* becomes passive, **"to" appears**! 🤯
- ✅ *I **was made to** wait.* ✅ *(passive → needs "to")*
- ✅ *We **were made to** stand outside.* ✅

*(Note.)* Odd but real. ✅ Active *make* → no *to* · Passive *made* → **to** ✅

---

### The four verbs in detail 📋

#### 💪💪💪 MAKE = force

**Structure:** make + person + **plain verb** ✅

- ✅ *My parents made me study science.* ✅
- ✅ *The rain made us cancel the trip.* ✅
- ✅ *He makes me laugh.* ⭐

*(Note.)* **make** also works for **feelings**:
- ✅ *This song makes me **happy**.* ✅ *(+ adjective!)*
- ✅ *That makes me **nervous**.* ✅

#### 🚪 LET = allow

**Structure:** let + person + **plain verb** ✅

- ✅ *My boss let me leave early.* ✅
- ✅ *Let me know.* ⭐ *(the most useful phrase in English!)* 🔗
- ✅ *They didn't let us in.* ✅
- ✅ *Let me think for a second.* ⭐ 🔗 *(Day 38)*

*(Note.)* ⚠️ *Let* doesn't form a passive **this** way: ❌ *"I was let to go"* ❌ ✅ Use **"I was allowed to go."** ✅

*(For your ear.)* ⭐ *Let* does appear in a few fixed passive phrases — ✅ *"He was let go"* *(= dismissed)* · *"We were let in."* ✅ But for **permission**, always use **was allowed to**. ⭐

*(And "allow" takes "to"!)*
- ✅ *He **let** me go.* ✅ *(no to)*
- ✅ *He **allowed** me **to** go.* ✅ *(with to)*
- ✅ *I **was allowed to** go.* ✅

#### 💪💪 HAVE = arrange with authority

**Structure A:** have + person + **plain verb** ✅
- ✅ *I'll have someone **call** you.* ⭐
- ✅ *The manager had me **redo** the report.* ✅

**Structure B:** have + thing + **V3** ✅
- ✅ *I had my car **serviced**.* ✅
- ✅ *We had the room **cleaned**.* ✅

*(Note.)* **"I'll have someone call you"** ⭐ is excellent professional English ✅ — used constantly in customer service. 📞

#### 💪 GET = arrange or persuade

**Structure A:** get + person + **TO** + verb ⚠️
- ✅ *I got my friend **to help** me.* ✅
- ✅ *Can you get him **to sign** this?* ✅

**Structure B:** get + thing + **V3** ✅
- ✅ *I got my hair **cut**.* ⭐
- ✅ *I need to get this **fixed**.* ✅

*(Note.)* **get** feels more **informal** and often implies **effort or persuasion**:
- ✅ *It took me an hour to get him **to agree**.* ✅

---

### 🎯 Bonus: "help" — the flexible one

*(A useful extra.)*

**help** takes **either**! ✅

- ✅ *She helped me **carry** the bags.* ✅ *(no to)*
- ✅ *She helped me **to carry** the bags.* ✅ *(with to)*

*(Emphasise.)* **Both correct.** ✅ The version without *to* is more common. ⭐

## ⚠️ 4. THE MALAYALI TRAP (11:00 – 13:00)

### Trap 1: Doing it yourself when someone else did it

*(The core error — because Malayalam marks it with one syllable.)*

| Malayalam | ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|---|
| മുടി വെട്ടിച്ചു | I cut my hair | **I got my hair cut** ⭐ |
| ബൈക്ക് നന്നാക്കിച്ചു | I repaired my bike | **I got my bike repaired** ✅ |
| വീട് പെയിന്റ് ചെയ്യിച്ചു | I painted my house | **I had my house painted** ✅ |
| Photo എടുപ്പിച്ചു | I took a photo ⚠️ | **I had a photo taken** ✅ |
| Dress തയ്പ്പിച്ചു | I stitched a dress | **I got a dress stitched** ✅ |

*(The self-check.)*

> ### 👉 Did I do it with **my own hands**? ✅ → simple past
> ### 👉 Did **someone else** do it for me? ✅ → **got/had + thing + V3**

*(Emphasise.)* Malayalam gives you the answer! ✅ **-ഇച്ചു / -ഇപ്പിച്ചു → got/had + V3** ✅ 🎯

### Trap 2: Adding "to" after make and let

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| He made me to wait | **He made me wait** ✅ |
| She let me to go | **She let me go** ✅ |
| They made us to stand | **They made us stand** ✅ |
| Let me to know | **Let me know** ⭐ |

*(The memory.)* 🔑 **make · let · have → naked verb** ✅ *(no "to")*

*(Practise.)* **made me wait · let me go · had him check** ✅ Say each three times. 🗣️

### Trap 3: "-ഇപ്പിക്കുക" translated as "do"

*(A subtle one.)*

| Malayalam | ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|---|
| അവൻ എന്നെ പറയിപ്പിച്ചു | He did me say | **He made me say it** ✅ |
| ജോലി ചെയ്യിപ്പിച്ചു | He did work me | **He made me work** ✅ |
| അവളെ ചിരിപ്പിച്ചു | He did her laugh | **He made her laugh** ⭐ |

*(Rule.)* **-ഇപ്പിച്ചു with a person → "made [person] + verb"** ✅

### Trap 4: Wrong third form 🔗 *(Day 17)*

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| I got my hair cutted | **I got my hair cut** ✅ *(cut–cut–cut)* |
| I had my bike repair | **I had my bike repaired** ✅ |
| I got the file send | **I got the file sent** ✅ |
| I had it wrote | **I had it written** ✅ |

*(Rule.)* **Use the THIRD form when the action is done TO the thing.** ✅ *(Use *-ing* when it's ongoing: ✅ "I had the engine **running**.")*

*(The V3s you need for this structure.)*
**cut · fixed · repaired · painted · cleaned · printed · washed · serviced · stitched · renewed · delivered · installed · checked · translated · verified** ✅

### Bonus: "get" — the most useful verb in English 🌟

*(A worthwhile digression.)*

*(Explain.)* Malayalis under-use **get**. But it's genuinely everywhere:

| Meaning | Example |
|---|---|
| receive ✅ | *I got your message.* |
| obtain ✅ | *I got a new job.* ⭐ |
| become ✅ | *It's getting late.* ⭐ |
| arrive ✅ | *I got home at 8.* ⭐ |
| understand ✅ | *Got it.* ⭐ 🔗 |
| bring 🔗 | *Can you get me some water?* ⭐ |
| causative ✅ | *I got my hair cut.* ⭐ |

*(Emphasise.)* **"Get" does seven jobs.** ✅ Learning it well is one of the highest-value things an intermediate speaker can do. ⭐

*(And the phrasal verbs!)* 🔗 *(Day 76)*
- ✅ *get **up*** = rise from bed ✅ *(different from **wake up** = stop sleeping — you can wake up at 6 and get up at 6:30!)*
- ✅ *get **on*** = board ✅ 🔗 *(Day 18)*
- ✅ *get **off*** = leave a vehicle ✅
- ✅ *get **back to** you* = reply ⭐ 🔗 *(Day 41)*
- ✅ *get **along with*** = have a good relationship ✅
- ✅ *get **over*** = recover from ✅

### The 3-second self-check 🎯

1. **Did someone else do it?** → causative ✅
2. **Focus on the thing?** → **got/had + thing + V3** ⭐
3. **Focus on the person?** → *make/let/have* + person + **plain verb** ✅ *(or get + to)*
4. **How much force?** → make 💪💪💪 · have 💪💪 · get 💪 · let 🚪

*(Drill — I say Malayalam-style, you fix it.)*
- *"I cut my hair"* → **I got my hair cut** ✅
- *"He made me to wait"* → **He made me wait** ✅
- *"I repaired my phone"* *(mechanic did it)* → **I got my phone repaired** ✅
- *"She let me to go"* → **She let me go** ✅
