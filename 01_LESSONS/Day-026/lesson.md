Question-word contractions common ആണ്. What is becomes what's. Where is becomes where's. Who is becomes who's. How is becomes how's. That is becomes that's. Here is becomes here's. Today full question forms already known; now short sound catch ചെയ്യാം.

When is often becomes when's in casual speech: When's the class? Which is can become which is with very light is, though not every combination needs contraction practice today. Focus on frequent chunks that your ear meets often.

Whose and who's can sound the same. Context meaning protects you. Whose bag is this? asks ownership; whose is one question word. Who's carrying the bag? means who is carrying. In audio, listen to what comes next. A noun directly after whose often signals ownership: whose bag. An ing action after who's often signals who is: who's carrying.

Audio alone sentence decode ചെയ്യാൻ three steps ഉപയോഗിക്കാം. First, catch the content words — person, action, place, time. Second, listen for a small grammar chunk attached to subject or negative. Third, rebuild full meaning, not necessarily full spelling.

Example: “She's meeting Maya tomorrow.” Content words: meeting, Maya, tomorrow. Small chunk: she's, meaning she is. Full recovery: She is meeting Maya tomorrow.

Example: “They don't work on Fridays.” Content words: work, Fridays. Small negative chunk: don't. Full recovery: They do not work on Fridays.

Example: “Where's Arun waiting?” Content words: Arun, waiting. Front chunk where's means where is. Full recovery: Where is Arun waiting?

Common listening mistakes repair ചെയ്യാം. Speaker says, “He's reading.” Learner hears, “He reading,” and copies missing be. Correct internal form: He is reading. Tiny he's already contains is.

Speaker says, “She isn't coming.” Learner catches coming but misses negative. Meaning flips. Train ear to hear the n't chunk before action.

Speaker says, “They don't live here.” Learner hears “They live here.” Again opposite. Don't is not decoration; it controls truth.

Speaker says, “Where's he working?” Learner writes or repeats “Where he working?” The small 's is is the question helper. Full order: Where is he working?

Speaker says, “Who's calling?” Learner interprets whose ownership. Next word calling is ing action, so who's means who is.
