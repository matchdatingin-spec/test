# Day 46 — Present Perfect vs Past Simple — the most confusing pair

നമസ്കാരം. ഇന്ന് പല learners-നും ഏറ്റവും confusing ആയ pair clear ആക്കാം: Present Perfect and Past Simple. രണ്ടും past experience അല്ലെങ്കിൽ past action-നെ touch ചെയ്യാം. പക്ഷേ speaker time-നെ എങ്ങനെ കാണിക്കുന്നു എന്നതാണ് choice. Present Perfect connects the past to now and does not place the action inside a finished past time. Past Simple puts the action in a finished past time or tells the completed past detail.

രണ്ട് sentences കേൾക്കൂ.

I have visited Delhi.

I visited Delhi in 2022.

First sentence life experience. Beginning of my life to now എന്ന open window. Exact finished time പറയുന്നില്ല. Second sentence action-നെ 2022 എന്ന finished past box-ൽ വെക്കുന്നു. അതുകൊണ്ട് Past Simple.

മറ്റൊരു pair.

She has seen that film.

She saw that film last Sunday.

Has seen means the experience exists now. Saw plus last Sunday gives the finished occasion. Form മാത്രം memorize ചെയ്താൽ confusion വരും. Time view മനസ്സിലാക്കിയാൽ choice easy.

Core map aloud പറയൂ. Experience up to now, no finished time: Present Perfect. Finished past time or past detail: Past Simple.

Pause.

Present Perfect form: have or has plus V3. I have seen. She has gone. Past Simple affirmative: V2. I saw. She went. Past Simple question and negative: did or didn't plus V1. Did you see? I didn't see.

ഇപ്പോൾ title-ലെ common learner error എടുത്തോളൂ: I have seen him yesterday. ഇത് തെറ്റാണ്. Yesterday finished past time ആണ്. Have seen past-നെ now-വിനോട് open ആയി connect ചെയ്യുന്നു; yesterday action-നെ closed past box-ൽ വെക്കുന്നു. Correct: I saw him yesterday.

Finished-time signals familiar ആണ്: yesterday, last night, last week, last year, two days ago, in 2020, on Monday, at six. ഇവ വന്നാൽ reported action സാധാരണ Past Simple-ൽ. Marker കണ്ടാൽ mechanical rule മാത്രം follow ചെയ്യരുത്; marker കാണിക്കുന്ന finished time meaning ശ്രദ്ധിക്കൂ.

Present Perfect experience question എങ്ങനെ conversation തുടങ്ങുന്നു എന്ന് നോക്കാം.

Yes, I have.

When did you visit Jaipur?

I visited Jaipur three years ago.

First question asks whether experience exists. Answer yes. Then conversation moves to a finished occasion, so Past Simple: When did you visit? I visited three years ago.

Same verb, different time view. നിങ്ങളുടെ answers truthful ആക്കൂ. Present Perfect answer-ൽ have or haven't. Past Simple answer-ൽ V2, or did/didn't plus V1.

Present connection sometimes ഒരു current situation-ലും കാണാം. “I have lost my keys” means the loss creates a problem now; I do not have the keys now. “I lost my keys yesterday” locates the loss yesterday. It may still be a problem, but the sentence's chosen focus is finished time. Today contrast മനസ്സിലാക്കാൻ ഈ meaning useful ആണ്. New marker systems ഒന്നും തുറക്കുന്നില്ല.

Form danger map. Present Perfect: have plus V3, not V2. I have gone, not I have went. Past Simple affirmative: V2 without have. I went, not I have went. Past Simple question: Did you go? not Did you went? Present Perfect question: Have you gone? not Have you went?
