Conceptual recap. Present Continuous gives subject plus am, is, or are plus verb-ing. It shows happening-now action, and with a concrete future time it can show a fixed near-future arrangement. Present Simple gives habits and common state meanings. WH-questions begin with what, where, when, who, why, how, which, or whose, then use the correct helper order. Contractions compress familiar words but keep grammar and meaning. Direct English speaking begins from a known pattern, not a translated Malayalam word sequence.
