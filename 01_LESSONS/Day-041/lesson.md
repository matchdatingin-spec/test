Like pattern ഇനി. For general likes, like plus verb-ing is a strong safe pattern: I like reading. She likes cooking. We like walking in the evening. Like plus to plus base verb can also be grammatical in many contexts: I like to read before bed. The mistake is mixing both shapes: I like to reading. Choose one complete pattern: I like reading, or I like to read. ഇന്നത്തെ fluent default general activity-ക്ക് like doing practice ചെയ്യാം.

Dislike and don't like can also take ing for activities. I don't like waiting. She dislikes driving in heavy traffic. But today's core anchors remain like doing and enjoy doing.

Difference in meaning strength. I like cooking means cooking is pleasant. I enjoy cooking often sounds a little stronger and focuses on pleasure. Both use ing naturally. Want to cook means desire or intention; it does not tell us whether cooking is enjoyable.

Activities compare ചെയ്യുമ്പോൾ prefer verb-ing to verb-ing. I prefer walking to driving. He prefers cooking at home to eating outside. They prefer travelling by train to flying.

ഇനി would rather. Would rather expresses a choice in a particular situation. After would rather, base verb directly; no to. I would rather stay home. She would rather take the bus. We'd rather leave now. Negative: I would rather not drive tonight.

Compare two options with than. I would rather walk than wait. We would rather cook at home than order food. He would rather call than send a message.

Would rather is same for every subject. I would rather, he would rather, they would rather. No s. After rather, base verb. I would rather to stay wrong. I would rather staying wrong. I would rather stay correct.

Preference nuance. I prefer tea to coffee describes a general preference. Right now someone offers both, and you choose in this situation: I would rather have tea. General: I prefer walking to driving. Today it is raining, so I would rather drive. A person can have one general preference but make a different situational choice.
