Finished? Conceptual recap. Everyday action question needs auxiliary do or does. Do goes with I, you, we, they, and plural subjects. Does goes with he, she, it, names, and singular subjects. Order is auxiliary, subject, main verb in base form, then rest. Does she work, not Does she works. Do they go, not Do they goes. Short answers return the auxiliary: Yes, I do. No, I don't. Yes, she does. No, she doesn't. Be questions use the known be movement instead: Are you ready? Is he at home?
