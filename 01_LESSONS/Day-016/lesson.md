ഇനി sound ശ്രദ്ധിക്കാം. Written ending s or es always same sound അല്ല. ഇന്ന് phonetic symbols memorize ചെയ്യേണ്ട. Three useful listening patterns മാത്രം.

After a quiet ending like k, p, or t, final s often sounds like a soft s. Works. Sleeps. Gets. Say them: works, sleeps, gets.

After hissy endings like sh, ch, s, x, an extra syllable sounds like iz. Washes. Watches. Uses. Fixes. Say them: washes, watches, uses, fixes.

Finished? Conceptual recap. Present Simple positive action sentence-ൽ I, you, we, they take base verb: work, go, study. He, she, it and one-person or one-thing equivalents take third-person singular form: works, goes, studies. Most verbs add s. Hissy or selected endings use es. Consonant plus y changes to ies; vowel plus y keeps y and adds s. Have has the irregular agreement form has. Written endings can sound soft s, z-like, or an extra iz syllable. Meaning decides agreement: one other person or thing versus I, you, we, they or plural subjects.
