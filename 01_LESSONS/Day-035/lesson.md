Quick toolbox recall. Past be: I was tired. We were at home. Negative: She wasn't late. They weren't busy. Questions: Was he ready? Were you outside? Regular past: worked, played, wanted. Irregular past: went, saw, took, made, ate, came. Action questions: Did you work? Where did she go? Action negatives: I didn't work. She didn't go. Central rule പറയൂ. Positive completed action uses V2. Did question uses V1. Didn't negative uses V1. Past be uses was or were without did.

ഇനി model yesterday narration കേൾക്കൂ. Content copy ചെയ്യരുത്; structure observe ചെയ്യൂ.

Yesterday was a full day. I was awake at six thirty, but I wasn't ready to get up. I got out of bed at seven and opened the windows. The morning was cool. I made tea, ate a light breakfast, and checked my messages. At eight thirty, I walked to a nearby office. I worked there for three hours and finished two tasks. At noon, I met a friend and we ate lunch together. The café was busy, but the service was quick. In the afternoon, I returned home and rested for an hour. I didn't go outside in the evening. I called my sister, watched a short programme, and prepared dinner. I was tired at night, so I went to bed at ten thirty.

Model structure notice ചെയ്യൂ. Opening gives overall description. Morning location and feeling use was. Actions use V2: got, opened, made, ate, checked, walked, worked, finished, met, returned, rested, called, watched, prepared, went. One negative uses didn't plus V1: didn't go. Time anchors guide listener: at six thirty, at seven, at eight thirty, for three hours, at noon, in the afternoon, for an hour, in the evening, at night, at ten thirty.

ഇനി last weekend model. Weekend narration usually covers more than one day, so day labels matter.

Last weekend was relaxing. On Saturday morning, I cleaned my room and washed some clothes. I started at nine and worked for about an hour. Then I took a bus to my cousin's house. My cousin was at home, and her children were excited to see me. We talked, played a board game, and ordered lunch. I didn't stay there at night. I left at six and came home before seven.

On Sunday, the weather was pleasant. I walked in a park in the morning and met an old neighbour. We spoke for twenty minutes. In the afternoon, I prepared food for the week and called my parents. I didn't watch any films, and I didn't use my laptop. In the evening, I read a book for an hour. The weekend was simple, but it was enjoyable.

Observe day boundary. On Saturday morning. On Sunday. No “in last weekend.” Say last weekend. Be details: weekend was relaxing, cousin was home, children were excited, weather was pleasant. Action positives mix regular and irregular. Negatives use didn't plus stay, watch, use. Durations use for an hour and for twenty minutes.

Finished? Score yourself honestly in five areas. Meaning and sequence: a listener can follow what happened and when. Past forms: regular and irregular V2 stay accurate. Helper control: did and didn't are always followed by V1. Past be: was and were match subjects and handle their own questions and negatives. Time language and listening: in, on, at, for, ago, last, yesterday are clear, and a short past story is understandable.

A weak area is not failure; it gives the next practice target. If was and were are weak, create ten past place and feeling sentences, half singular and half plural. If regular past is weak, tell five actions and classify each ed ending. If irregular forms are weak, drill ten V1-V2-V3 chains, then use only V2 in yesterday sentences. If did and didn't are weak, repeat triads: I went, did you go, I didn't go. If time expressions are weak, rebuild one day using at, on, in, for, yesterday, and ago. If listening is weak, use three passes: gist, sequence, exact negatives and forms.

Conceptual recap. Past be uses was with I, he, she, it, and singular nouns; were with you, we, they, and plurals. Completed positive actions use V2. Regular V2 usually ends in ed with t, d, or id sound. Irregular V2 three-form map ആയി learn ചെയ്യൂ, while V3 waits for later use. Past action questions use did plus V1. Past action negatives use didn't plus V1. Time anchors make the story followable: at six, on Saturday, in the morning, for an hour, two days ago, last weekend, yesterday.

Closing proof പറയൂ. I can describe where I was and how I felt. I can tell completed regular and irregular actions. I can ask and answer past questions. I can say what I didn't do. I can use clear past time expressions. I can follow and retell a short past story. I can tell what I did yesterday and last weekend without switching back to present forms.
