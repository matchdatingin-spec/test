Quick meaning map recall ചെയ്യാം. Existing plan or intention: am, is, or are going to plus base verb. Immediate decision, prediction, or promise: will plus base verb. Ability or permission: can plus base verb. Advice: should plus base verb. Strong rule: must plus base verb. Situation-based necessity: have to or has to plus base verb. Uncertain possibility: may or might plus base verb. Desire: want to plus base verb. Enjoyment: enjoy plus ing. General comparison: prefer A to B. Current choice: would rather plus base verb.

Checkpoint danger map. Do not stack modal words: not will can, not might can. After will, can, should, must, may, and might, use base verb without to. Have to keeps to. Going to needs am, is, or are before it. Enjoy takes ing. Would rather takes base verb without to. Prefer compares with to, not than.

Structure notice ചെയ്യൂ. Opening gives the main plan. Going to marks activities already decided. Have to and don't have to explain practical requirements. Can and can't create an honest ability profile. Want to names a goal. Enjoy and prefer explain learning style. Would rather gives a choice. May and might keep uncertain details uncertain. Will handles a decision, promise, and prediction. All tools support one coherent topic; they are not random grammar lines.

Independent checkpoint final round. No supplied content after this cue. Deliver three connected parts. Part one: your plans for the next seven days, including preparation and uncertainty. Part two: your abilities, limitations, and one improvement intention. Part three: your likes, enjoyment, general preferences, and current choices. Include at least two questions and answers in each part. Add advice, rules, necessity, or promises only where they naturally support the message. If you make an error, repair that sentence and continue; do not restart.

Start the final checkpoint now.

Finished? Score yourself honestly in five areas. First, meaning choice: plan, decision, ability, advice, necessity, possibility, and preference stay distinct. Second, modal form: will, can, should, must, may, and might take base verbs without to and never stack. Third, plan form: going to always has am, is, or are. Fourth, necessity: have to or has to is correct, and don't have to is not confused with mustn't. Fifth, complement patterns: want to do, enjoy doing, prefer A to B, and would rather do remain stable while speaking.

If meaning choice is weak, take one action such as call and create contrasts: I am going to call, I'll call, I can call, I may call, I have to call, I want to call. If modal form is weak, repeat base-verb chains: can go, should go, must go, may go, might go, will go. If going to is weak, drill subject agreement: I am going to, she is going to, they are going to. If necessity is weak, compare mustn't with don't have to in five real rules. If complement patterns are weak, repeat whole chunks with new verbs: want to learn, enjoy learning, prefer learning at home to learning in a class, would rather learn with a friend.

Conceptual recap. Existing plan uses going to. New decision, prediction, or promise uses will. Ability and permission use can. Advice uses should. Strong rules use must; practical necessity uses have to. Possibility uses may or might. Want points to to plus base verb. Enjoy points to ing. Prefer compares with to. Would rather takes the base verb and can compare with than.

I can make a decision or promise with will. I can keep every following verb in the correct form.
