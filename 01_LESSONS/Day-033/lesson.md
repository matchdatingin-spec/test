Final recap. A past story gives time frame, people, places, completed actions, descriptions, questions, negatives, and an ending. Regular ed has t, d, or id sound. Irregular V2 has no one sound rule, so recognize common forms directly. Did and didn't carry past even when the main verb is V1. Was and were carry past descriptions and locations. Strong listeners build meaning in layers, not word by word.
