Present Simple ഒരു calendar or repeated-pattern camera പോലെയാണ്. I walk to work every day. She cooks dinner on weekdays. They usually play after school. Sentence പറയുന്ന exact second-ൽ action നടക്കണം എന്നില്ല. It is true as a routine, habit, or regular pattern.

Present Continuous ഒരു live camera പോലെയാണ്. I am walking to work now. She is cooking dinner at the moment. They are playing now. Action ഈ സമയത്ത് open ആണ്.

Meaning pair ശ്രദ്ധിക്കൂ. I read before bed. അത് എന്റെ habit. I am reading now. അത് live action. Maya works in a bank. അത് regular situation. Maya is working at home now. അത് ഈ സമയത്തെ live situation. The children play outside in the evening. അത് repeated pattern. The children are playing inside now. അത് current action.

Form recall ചെയ്യാം. Present Simple positive-ൽ I, you, we, they plus base verb: I work. They study. He, she, it, one person plus s, es, or ies: He works. She watches. Arun studies. Negative and question-ൽ do, does, don't, doesn't plus base verb: She doesn't work on Sunday. Does she work on Saturday?

Present Continuous-ൽ subject plus am, is, are plus verb-ing: I am working. She is watching. They are studying. Negative: She is not working. Question: Is she working?

Conceptual recap. Present Simple gives habits, repeated patterns, regular situations, and common state meanings. I work. She works. They don't work. Does he work? Present Continuous gives actions happening now. I am working. She is working. They are not working. Is he working? Do not mix engines. She works or she is working; not she is works. Know, like, want, need, understand, and possession-have usually stay in Present Simple in basic everyday use.
