Now must versus have to in practical speech. Sometimes both express strong necessity and both can be correct. I must call my mother today feels like the speaker personally sees it as important. I have to call the bank before it closes points to a practical situation or deadline. Avoid becoming stuck trying to find a perfect wall between them. First ask: advice or real necessity? If advice, should. If necessity, must or have to according to strength and source. If rule from a sign or authority, must is clear. If schedule or circumstance forces it, have to is natural.
