# Day 72 — Conditionals 2 — if + past, would (imagining and wishing)

നമസ്കാരം. ഇന്നലെ നമ്മൾ real facts-ഉം real future possibilities-ഉം if ഉപയോഗിച്ച് പറഞ്ഞു. If I get leave, I will come എന്ന sentence-ൽ leave കിട്ടാനുള്ള practical chance ഉണ്ട്. ഇന്ന് നമ്മൾ reality-ൽ നിന്ന് ഒരു step പിന്നോട്ട് പോകുന്നു. ഒരു situation ഇപ്പോൾ true അല്ല, unlikely ആണ്, അല്ലെങ്കിൽ വെറും imagination ആണ്. അപ്പോൾ if plus past, would എന്ന pattern ഉപയോഗിക്കാം.

ഒരു simple example കേൾക്കൂ.

If I had more free time, I would learn the guitar.

ഇപ്പോൾ എനിക്ക് വേണ്ടത്ര free time ഇല്ല. പക്ഷേ കൂടുതൽ സമയം ഉണ്ടെന്ന് imagine ചെയ്യുന്നു. ആ imagined condition-ന്റെ result ആണ് I would learn the guitar.

Pattern ശ്രദ്ധിക്കൂ.

If plus past form, would plus base verb.

If I had more free time, I would learn the guitar.

Had കേട്ടാൽ ഇത് yesterday-ലെ past story ആണെന്ന് കരുതരുത്. ഇവിടെ past form distance കാണിക്കുന്നു. Reality-യിൽ നിന്ന് distance. More free time ഇപ്പോൾ ഇല്ല; imagination-ൽ ഉണ്ട്.

ഇന്നലത്തെ real possibility-യുമായി compare ചെയ്യാം.

If I have time this evening, I will call you.

ഇന്ന് വൈകുന്നേരം സമയം കിട്ടാനുള്ള real chance ഉണ്ട്.

If I had more time every evening, I would call my friends more often.

Actually അങ്ങനെ സമയം ഇല്ല. ഒരു different life imagine ചെയ്യുന്നു.

Have plus will real. Had plus would imagined.

വീണ്ടും കേൾക്കൂ.

If I find a cheaper flat, I will move.

Cheaper flat കിട്ടാനുള്ള real search നടക്കുന്നു.

If I found a very cheap flat near my office, I would move immediately.

അത് ഒരു imagined or less likely situation ആണ്.

Malayalam-ൽ രണ്ടും “കിട്ടിയാൽ” പോലെ വരാം. English form തിരഞ്ഞെടുക്കാൻ speaker-ന്റെ view ആണ് പ്രധാനപ്പെട്ടത്. ഇത് practical open possibility ആണോ? Present plus will. ഇത് unreal, unlikely, hypothetical, wishful ആണോ? Past plus would.

ഇനി form carefully നോക്കാം. Past form regular verb-ൽ worked, lived, studied പോലെയാകും.

If I worked closer to home, I would walk to the office.

If she lived nearby, we would meet every week.

If they studied together, they would finish faster.

Irregular verbs-ൽ past form മാറും.

If I knew the answer, I would tell you.

Know becomes knew.

If he had a car, he would drive to work.

Have becomes had.

If we went by train, we would enjoy the journey.

Go becomes went.

Result-ൽ would കഴിഞ്ഞ് base verb ആണ്. Would went അല്ല. Would goes അല്ല.

If we went by train, we would enjoy the journey.

Common error repair ചെയ്യാം.

If I had more money, I would bought a better phone.

Pause.

If I had more money, I would buy a better phone.

Would plus buy. Base form.

If she knew his number, she will call him.

Pause.

If she knew his number, she would call him.

Imagined condition knew ആണെങ്കിൽ matching result would call.

If he would practise more, he would improve.

Pause.

If he practised more, he would improve.

ഇന്നലത്തെ pattern-ൽ if-part-ൽ will ഇടാത്തതുപോലെ, ഇന്നത്തെ pattern-ൽ if-part-ൽ സാധാരണ would ഇടുന്നില്ല. Would result side-ൽ വരുന്നു.

If I would have time, I would travel എന്നല്ല.

If I had time, I would travel.

Good.

Be verb-ന് ഒരു special form കേൾക്കാം.

If I were you, I would speak to the manager.

ഇത് advice നൽകാനുള്ള വളരെ useful expression ആണ്. ഞാൻ നിങ്ങൾ അല്ല. നിങ്ങളുടെ position-ൽ എന്നെ imagine ചെയ്യുന്നു. അതിനാൽ If I were you.

Careful speech-ൽ I, he, she, it എല്ലാം imagined condition-ൽ were ഉപയോഗിക്കുന്നത് വളരെ common ആണ്.

If she were here, she would know what to do.

If the room were larger, we would use it for meetings.

Everyday conversation-ൽ If I was you എന്നും കേൾക്കാം. പക്ഷേ clear standard form ആയി If I were you practise ചെയ്യൂ.

Advice chain ചെയ്യാം.

You have an interview tomorrow and you feel nervous. ഞാൻ advice പറയുന്നു.

If I were you, I would prepare three short examples tonight.

If I were you, I would sleep early.

If I were you, I would not memorise every sentence.

Would not natural speech-ൽ wouldn’t.

If I were you, I wouldn’t speak too fast.

ഇപ്പോൾ നിങ്ങളുടെ turn. Friend ഒരു expensive item ഉടനെ വാങ്ങാൻ പോകുന്നു. Advice പറയൂ.

Pause.

If I were you, I would compare the prices first.

അല്ലെങ്കിൽ, If I were you, I wouldn’t buy it today.

ഇനി wishes എന്ന meaning. ഇവിടെ പുതിയ wish structure പഠിക്കുന്നില്ല; if-sentence കൊണ്ട് better situation imagine ചെയ്യുകയാണ്.

If I had a month off, I would travel across Kerala.

If our house had a garden, I would grow vegetables.

If I could speak five languages, I would work in different countries.

Could ഇവിടെ imagined ability ആണ്. If I could speak means എനിക്ക് ആ ability ഉണ്ടെന്ന് imagine ചെയ്യുന്നു. Result still would plus base verb.

If I could cook well, I would invite everyone for dinner.

If my friend could join me, the trip would be more enjoyable.

ഇവ current facts അല്ല. Hopes, dreams, wishes, imagined advantages ആണ്.

Questions ഉപയോഗിച്ചാൽ conversation lively ആകും.

What would you do if you had a free year?

Where would you live if you could choose any city?

Who would you call if you won a big prize?

Question part-ൽ would subject-ന് മുമ്പ് വരുന്നു.

What would you do?

പിന്നെ condition: if you had a free year.

If you would have a free year എന്നല്ല.

What would you do if you had a free year?

Answer പറയൂ.

Pause.

If I had a free year, I would learn a new skill and travel.

Where would you live if you could choose any city?

Pause.

If I could choose any city, I would live somewhere close to the sea.

Who would you call if you won a big prize?

Pause.

If I won a big prize, I would call my family first.

Won is past form of win. ഇത് prize നേരത്തെ won ചെയ്തു എന്ന past report അല്ല. Winning imagined ആണ്.

Negative condition practise ചെയ്യാം.

If I did not need this job, I would take a long break.

Natural speech: If I didn’t need this job, I’d take a long break.

I’d എന്നത് ഇവിടെ I would.

If the rent weren’t so high, we would choose that flat.

Weren’t means were not.

Result negative:

If I had his email address, I wouldn’t send the message without checking it.

രണ്ട് outcomes contrast ചെയ്യാം.

If I lived in the city, I would use public transport, but I wouldn’t enjoy the noise.

Known connector but ഉപയോഗിച്ച് imagined advantage-ഉം disadvantage-ഉം ചേർത്തു.

ഇനി meaning check. ഞാൻ രണ്ട് sentences പറയും. Difference explain ചെയ്യൂ.

If the company offers me the job, I will accept it.

If the company offered me a job abroad, I would think carefully.

ആദ്യത്തേത് active real possibility. Offer വരാം. വന്നാൽ accept ചെയ്യും. രണ്ടാമത്തേത് hypothetical scenario. Abroad job offer imagine ചെയ്യുന്നു; actual offer ഇല്ലായിരിക്കാം.

മറ്റൊരു pair.

If it rains tomorrow, we will cancel the picnic.

If it rained every day here, I would move somewhere else.

ആദ്യ sentence tomorrow-ന്റെ real weather possibility. രണ്ടാം sentence an imagined repeated situation.

ഇനി correction with meaning. ഒരു learner പറയുന്നു:

ഇവിടെ speaker imaginary manager role ആണ് ഉദ്ദേശിക്കുന്നത്.

Pause.

If I were the manager, I would change the schedule.

മറ്റൊന്ന്:

If I get ten days of unexpected leave, I would visit Delhi.

Speaker leave കിട്ടുന്നത് real possibility ആയി കാണുന്നെങ്കിൽ:

If I get ten days of leave, I will visit Delhi.

Speaker ഒരു dream scenario imagine ചെയ്യുന്നെങ്കിൽ:

If I had ten days of leave, I would visit Delhi.

Grammar correction meaning-നെ ആശ്രയിച്ചിരിക്കുന്നു. Form കണ്ടിട്ട് മാത്രം അല്ല; speaker reality എങ്ങനെ കാണുന്നു എന്ന് കേൾക്കണം.

ഇനി guided imagination. നിങ്ങൾക്ക് ഒരു small café own ചെയ്യാമെന്ന് imagine ചെയ്യൂ. Location, menu, opening time, customers, atmosphere—ഇവയിൽ ideas പറയാം.

If I owned a small café, I would open it near a college. I would serve simple food if students wanted quick meals. If I had enough space, I would create a quiet reading corner. If the café were successful, I would hire two local workers.

ഇവിടെ first if-clause scenario തുറക്കുന്നു. പിന്നാലെയുള്ള details എല്ലാം same imagined world-ൽ ആണ്. ഓരോ sentence-ലും if വേണമെന്നില്ല; പക്ഷേ if ഉപയോഗിക്കുന്നിടത്ത് past form, result-ൽ would form clear ആണ്.

ഇപ്പോൾ നിങ്ങളുടെ café അല്ലെങ്കിൽ dream shop കുറിച്ച് നാല് sentences പറയൂ.

Good. Would result-കൾ clear ആയിരുന്നോ എന്ന് check ചെയ്യൂ. Would opened എന്ന് പറഞ്ഞെങ്കിൽ would open ആക്കൂ. If I would own എന്ന് പറഞ്ഞെങ്കിൽ If I owned ആക്കൂ.

ഇപ്പോൾ ഒരു full imagined situation എടുത്ത് forty-five seconds സംസാരിക്കൂ. നിങ്ങൾക്ക് ഒരു different job choose ചെയ്യാമെന്ന് imagine ചെയ്യാം, ഒരു year off കിട്ടിയെന്ന് imagine ചെയ്യാം, അല്ലെങ്കിൽ മറ്റൊരു city-ൽ ജീവിക്കുന്നതായി imagine ചെയ്യാം. Condition കൊണ്ട് scenario തുറക്കൂ. കുറഞ്ഞത് നാല് would results പറയൂ. ഒരു advantage, ഒരു difficulty, ഒരു choice, ഒരു final decision ഉൾപ്പെടുത്തൂ.

ഇന്നത്തെ proof aloud.

I can use if plus past to open an imagined or unlikely situation. I can use would plus a base verb for the imagined result. I can say If I were you to give advice. I can use could for imagined ability. I do not normally put would in the if-part. I know that the past form here often shows distance from reality, not past time.

അവസാന contrast clear ആയി പറയൂ.

If I have time tonight, I will watch a film.

If I had more time in my life, I would watch more films.

ആദ്യത്തേത് real possibility. രണ്ടാമത്തേത് imagined better situation. ഇതാണ് ഇന്നത്തെ core choice. Situation open and practical ആണെങ്കിൽ if plus present, real result. Situation unreal, unlikely, wishful, hypothetical ആണെങ്കിൽ if plus past, would plus base verb. ഈ difference control ചെയ്താൽ plans മാത്രം അല്ല, dreams, advice, preferences, imagined consequences—ഇവയും natural ആയി discuss ചെയ്യാൻ കഴിയും.
