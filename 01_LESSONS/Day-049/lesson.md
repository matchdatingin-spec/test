Tense map quick recall. Present Simple for routines, habits, and facts: I work from home. Present Continuous for happening now: I am working now. Past Simple for completed past actions and finished past times: I worked yesterday. Past Continuous for an action in progress at a past moment: I was working at eight. Present Perfect for experience or past-to-now connection without a finished past time: I have worked with international clients. Going to for an existing plan: I am going to work from home tomorrow. Will for a decision now, a prediction, or a promise: I'll help you.

Targeted correction round. Choose your weakest tense. If Present Simple weak, say I work, she works, she doesn't work, Does she work? If Present Continuous weak, say I am working, she is working, they are working. If Past Simple weak, say I went, I didn't go, Did you go? If Past Continuous weak, say I was going, they were going, Were they going? If Present Perfect weak, say I have gone, she has gone, Have you gone? If future plan weak, say I am going to go.
