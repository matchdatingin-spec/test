# Day 81 — Workplace English — updates, meetings, instructions and handling problems

നമസ്കാരം. ഇന്ന് workplace-ൽ four common speaking jobs practice ചെയ്യാം: ഒരു clear update നൽകുക, meeting-ൽ participate ചെയ്യുക, instruction നൽകുകയും confirm ചെയ്യുകയും ചെയ്യുക, problem professional ആയി handle ചെയ്യുക. വലിയ formal words അല്ല വേണ്ടത്. Short, calm, specific English ആണ് useful.

Malayalam speakers ചിലപ്പോൾ office English formal ആക്കാൻ sentence heavy ആക്കും.

Regarding the above-mentioned work, the same is under process and will be completed at the earliest possible time.

ഇത് കേൾക്കുമ്പോൾ exact status clear അല്ല. Natural update:

I have finished the first part. I am checking the final figures now, and I will send the file by three.

Who did what, current stage, next step, time—എല്ലാം clear.

Professional English stiff English അല്ല. Polite, accurate, easy-to-act-on English ആണ്.

ആദ്യം updates. ഒരു useful update listener-ന്റെ four questions answer ചെയ്യും. What is done? What is happening now? What comes next? Is anything blocking the work?

ഒരു model കേൾക്കൂ.

The customer list has been updated. I am checking the phone numbers now. After that, I will share the final sheet with the sales team. I am waiting for two missing entries from the north branch, but the rest is on schedule.

Done, now, next, blocker. Update complete.

Update വളരെ long history ആകരുത്. Manager ചോദിച്ചത് current status ആണെങ്കിൽ Monday മുതൽ every action narrate ചെയ്യേണ്ട.

Weak version:

Actually, on Monday I came and then first I checked many things and there were some discussions and yesterday also I tried, so almost it is going on.

Clear version:

I completed the initial check on Monday. Two figures were incorrect, so I asked the accounts team to confirm them. They sent one correction yesterday, and I am waiting for the other. I can finish the report as soon as it arrives.

Relevant history മാത്രം. Delay reason and next action clear.

Useful update chunks aloud പറയൂ.

I have completed the draft.

I am working on the final section.

The file has been sent for approval.

I will share the revised version by noon.

We are on schedule.

We are one day behind schedule.

I am waiting for confirmation from the supplier.

I need help with the last calculation.

Day 78 passive naturally status update-ൽ വരുന്നു: The file has been sent, the request was approved, the meeting was moved. Form analyse ചെയ്യേണ്ട; focus result-ൽ ആണ്.

“Work is going on” understandable ആണ്, പക്ഷേ vague. Specific action പറയൂ.

The design team is revising the home page.

We are testing the payment screen.

The electrician is replacing the damaged switch.

“Do the needful” പല Indian workplaces-ലും കേൾക്കും, പക്ഷേ wider natural English-ൽ exact action പറയുന്നത് better.

Please sign the form and return it by Friday.

Could you check the figures and tell me which one is correct?

Please contact the vendor and confirm the delivery time.

Listener എന്ത് ചെയ്യണം എന്ന് അറിയണം.

Update practice. Facts: draft complete, manager reviewing, two photos missing, photographer sending by four, final brochure tomorrow morning.

Thirty-second update നൽകൂ.

Pause.

ഒരു clear version:

The brochure draft is complete, and the manager is reviewing it now. Two photos are still missing, but the photographer has promised to send them by four. Once they arrive, I will add them and share the final brochure tomorrow morning.

“Has promised” known present perfect use ആണ്. Simpler ആയി The photographer will send them by four എന്നും പറയാം.

ഇനി delayed update. Delay hide ചെയ്യരുത്. Early, factual communication professional ആണ്.

I need to let you know that the delivery will be late. The vehicle developed a problem this morning, and the replacement has not arrived yet. The supplier now expects the materials by five. This may delay packing by about two hours. I will confirm the new packing time at three.

Structure ശ്രദ്ധിക്കൂ: problem, cause, impact, current estimate, next communication. Blame or drama ഇല്ല.

Common correction.

I am having one doubt about the total.

Pause.

I have a question about the total. Or, I am not sure about the total.

My work is pending.

More specific:

Pause.

I still need to check the last two pages.

I will inform you once it will be ready.

Pause.

I will let you know once it is ready.

Once-clause-ൽ unnecessary will ഒഴിവാക്കൂ, real condition pattern പോലെ.

ഇനി meetings. Meeting-ൽ fluency എന്നത് nonstop സംസാരിക്കൽ അല്ല. Enter, clarify, contribute, respond, summarise—ഇവയാണ് practical skills.

Meeting തുടങ്ങുമ്പോൾ objective confirm ചെയ്യാം.

Are we deciding the launch date today?

Just to confirm, is the main goal to choose a supplier?

What do we need to complete by the end of this meeting?

Question direct but polite ആണ്.

Contribution തുടങ്ങാൻ:

I have a quick update on the testing.

I would like to add one point.

From the customer side, the main concern is delivery time.

Based on last month’s figures, Friday may be the better option.

“I want to say something” wrong അല്ല, പക്ഷേ more specific entry listener-നെ prepare ചെയ്യും.

Interrupt politely ചെയ്യേണ്ടി വന്നാൽ:

Sorry to interrupt, but could I clarify one detail?

May I add something before we move on?

Could we return to the budget for a moment?

Over-apology വേണ്ട. One short sorry, then purpose.

Clarification extremely important ആണ്. മനസ്സിലായില്ലെങ്കിലും yes yes എന്ന് പറയുന്നത് later problem create ചെയ്യും.

Could you explain what you mean by the final check?

When you say Friday, do you mean Friday morning or the end of the day?

Could you repeat the last figure, please?

So, are you asking me to contact the client today?

Last sentence confirmation ആണ്. Instruction repeat ചെയ്ത് accuracy protect ചെയ്യുന്നു.

Agreement:

I agree with that approach because it saves time.

That makes sense to me.

I think that will work, provided the data arrives today.

Partial agreement:

I agree with the first part, but I am concerned about the deadline.

Disagreement polite ആക്കാൻ person attack ചെയ്യരുത്; issue explain ചെയ്യൂ.

I see your point, but I think Monday is more realistic.

I am not sure that option will solve the delivery problem.

Could we consider a smaller first order instead?

Day 83-ൽ discussion and debate deeper ആയി reasons support ചെയ്യും. ഇന്ന് meeting goal practical decision and action clarity ആണ്.

Mini meeting കേൾക്കൂ.

Asha says, “The training room is free on Thursday afternoon.”

Ben says, “Thursday is possible, but two new staff members will be travelling.”

Asha says, “Could we hold the main session on Friday and send the recording to anyone who is absent?”

Ben says, “That works for me. I can check the trainer’s availability today.”

നിങ്ങൾ chairperson ആണ്. Decision and actions summarise ചെയ്യൂ.

Pause.

We have agreed to plan the main session for Friday, subject to the trainer’s availability. Ben will contact the trainer today. If Friday is confirmed, Asha will inform the team and arrange a recording for anyone who cannot attend.

Meeting summary-ൽ who, action, when, condition clear ആണ്.

Vague summary:

We discussed training and everyone will do the necessary things.

Action ഇല്ല. Better summary specific.

ഇനി meeting response practice. Proposal: move weekly call from four to three. Your concern: customer support is busiest at three. Polite response പറയൂ.

Pause.

I understand why an earlier call would help, but customer support is busiest at three. Could we try three thirty instead?

Problem plus alternative. “I disagree” മാത്രം അല്ല.

ഇനി instructions. Clear instruction-ന് context, action, order, standard, deadline, check-back എന്നിവയിൽ relevant elements വേണം.

Please update the stock sheet.

ഇത് simple instruction. But task complex ആണെങ്കിൽ:

Please update the stock sheet with today’s delivery figures. Check the quantities against the invoices, mark any difference in yellow, and send me the sheet before four. If an invoice is missing, do not guess the number; call me.

Listener-ന് exact work clear.

Sequence words procedure-ൽ useful ആണ്.

First, save a copy of the original file. Then enter the new figures in the second column. After that, check the total at the bottom. Finally, rename the file with today’s date and upload it to the shared folder.

Day 78 process language ഇവിടെ active instruction ആയി മാറുന്നു. Manual describe ചെയ്താൽ passive possible; colleague-നോട് direct action പറയുമ്പോൾ imperative often natural.

Instruction too indirect ആകരുത്.

If you don’t mind, maybe it would be good if perhaps the report could be sent sometime today.

Deadline actually important ആണെങ്കിൽ:

Could you send the report by two today, please? The client call starts at three.

Polite and clear.

Instruction receive ചെയ്യുമ്പോൾ confirmation:

Let me check that I understood. You want me to update the first two sections and send the file by two. Is that right?

Should I include the cancelled orders?

Where should I save the final version?

Who should I copy on the email?

If the deadline impossible:

I can complete the first two sections by two, but I will need until four for the full report. Which is more urgent?

Silent yes പറഞ്ഞിട്ട് miss ചെയ്യുന്നതിനെക്കാൾ realistic alternative better.

Instruction correction.

Explain me the process.

Pause.

Explain the process to me. Or, show me how the process works.

Please revert back soon.

Natural wider-English options:

Pause.

Please reply soon. Or, please get back to me by noon.

Kindly intimate me after completion.

Pause.

Please let me know when it is complete.

Kindly itself wrong അല്ല, but repeated official wording speech unnatural ആക്കും. Please plus exact action മതി.

Guided instruction task. New colleague must prepare meeting room. Needs projector check, twelve chairs, water, printed agenda, ready by nine thirty, call if cable missing.

Instruction നൽകൂ.

Pause.

Please prepare the meeting room by nine thirty. First, check that the projector is working. Then arrange twelve chairs and place water on the side table. Put a printed agenda on each chair. If the projector cable is missing, call me before you look for a replacement.

ഇനി receiver ആയി confirm ചെയ്യൂ.

Pause.

Let me confirm. You need the room ready by nine thirty, with the projector checked, twelve chairs, water, and printed agendas. If I cannot find the cable, I should call you. Is that correct?

Excellent. Confirmation incompetence അല്ല; reliability ആണ്.

ഇനി handling problems. Professional problem report complaint മാത്രം അല്ല. Facts, impact, action already taken, help or decision needed.

The printer is not working.

Basic fact. Complete report:

The main printer stopped working about twenty minutes ago. It is showing a paper-feed error, although there is no paper inside. I restarted it once, but the error returned. We have thirty forms to print before noon. Could we use the printer on the second floor, or should I call the service team?

Listener decision എടുക്കാൻ enough information ഉണ്ട്.

Avoid blame-first opening.

Ramesh again made a mistake and because of him everything is late.

Better:

The order sheet has the old delivery address, so the driver went to the wrong location. I have sent him the correct address and called the customer. Delivery will be about forty minutes late. After today’s delivery, we should check how the old address remained in the system.

Immediate solution first. Root cause later. Person attack ഒഴിവാക്കി process fix നോക്കുന്നു.

Customer-facing problem-ൽ acknowledge, apologise where appropriate, explain only what is useful, offer next step.

I am sorry; your order has not left the warehouse yet. One item was damaged during packing and had to be replaced. The replacement will arrive this afternoon, and we can deliver the full order tomorrow morning. If that is too late, we can send the available items today and the final item tomorrow. Which option would you prefer?

False promise വേണ്ട. “Definitely in five minutes” ഉറപ്പ് ഇല്ലെങ്കിൽ പറയരുത്.

I am checking it now, and I will update you by eleven.

This is a reliable promise because it promises an update, not an unknown solution.

Problem language correction.

There is one issue happened.

Pause.

An issue has come up. Or, there is a problem.

The server is not opening.

Depending on meaning:

Pause.

I cannot connect to the server. Or, the page is not loading.

Yesterday only we received the file.

Natural emphasis:

Pause.

We received the file only yesterday. Or, we did not receive the file until yesterday.

He is on leave today itself.

Pause.

He is on leave today. “Itself” emphasis ഇവിടെ unnecessary.

ഇനി problem ladder. Facts: client presentation at two, latest chart missing, data team says source file corrupted, old chart available, need decision by one.

Problem report നൽകൂ.

Pause.

We have a problem with the client presentation at two. The latest sales chart is missing because the data team cannot open the source file. We still have last week’s chart, but its figures are not current. The data team is trying to recover the file now. We need to decide by one whether to use the old chart with a clear note or remove that section.

Impact and options clear.

Manager response ആയി clarification ചോദിക്കൂ.

Pause.

How different are the old figures from the latest estimates? And when will the data team know whether the file can be recovered?

Employee response:

The total is about five percent lower in the old chart. The data team will give us an answer by twelve thirty.

Meeting decision summarise ചെയ്യൂ.

Pause.

Let’s wait until twelve thirty. If the file cannot be recovered, we will use the old chart and explain that updated figures will be sent after the meeting.

Known if-language practical decision-ൽ working ആണ്.

ഇനി first independent update. നിങ്ങളുടെ real work reveal ചെയ്യേണ്ട; imaginary task choose ചെയ്യൂ. Completed work, current action, next step and time, one blocker if any. Forty-five seconds.

Pause.

Second task: mini meeting contribution. Proposal choose ചെയ്യൂ: meeting online ആക്കുക, delivery day മാറ്റുക, smaller event venue use ചെയ്യുക. Objective clarify ചെയ്യൂ. One useful point add ചെയ്യൂ. Another view acknowledge ചെയ്യൂ. Concern പറയൂ. Alternative suggest ചെയ്യൂ. Final action summary നൽകൂ.

Pause.

Third task: instruction chain. A colleague must complete a five-step task. Exact order, deadline, quality check, what to do if something is missing. Then receiver confirmation സ്വയം role-play ചെയ്യൂ.

Pause.

Fourth task: problem report. One equipment, schedule, customer, or information problem. Fact. Impact. Action taken. Current status. Help or decision needed. Blame ഒഴിവാക്കി thirty to sixty seconds.

Pause.

Self-evaluation. Update vague ആയിരുന്നോ, exact status ഉണ്ടോ? Meeting-ൽ contribution goal-നോട് connected ആയിരുന്നോ? Disagreement-ന് reason and alternative ഉണ്ടോ? Instruction-ൽ who, what, when clear ആണോ? Receiver confirmation ഉണ്ടോ? Problem report-ൽ emotion കൂടുതലോ facts കൂടുതലോ? Next step and next update time പറഞ്ഞോ?

ഇനി combined workplace scene. Morning meeting-ൽ നിങ്ങൾ report ചെയ്യണം. Website text ready. Product photos two missing. Designer can finish by four if photos arrive by noon. Supplier has not replied. You called once and sent a message. Need manager to decide whether temporary images may be used.

One-minute contribution നൽകൂ: update, problem, impact, action, request for decision.

Pause.

ഒരു possible version:

The website text is ready, and the designer has completed the main layout. We are still waiting for two product photos from the supplier. I called this morning and sent a follow-up message, but I have not received a reply. If the photos arrive by noon, the designer can finish by four. If they do not arrive, we need either to move the deadline or use temporary images. Could we agree now on which option you prefer?

ഇപ്പോൾ manager response imagine ചെയ്യൂ: use temporary images, replace tomorrow, label folder clearly, tell sales team. Instruction ആയി പറയൂ.

Pause.

Use the temporary images for today and replace them when the final photos arrive tomorrow. Please keep both versions in clearly labelled folders and tell the sales team which link to use. Send me a quick update when the replacement is complete.

Employee confirmation:

Pause.

Understood. I will use the temporary images today, keep the two versions in separate labelled folders, and inform the sales team. I will replace the images tomorrow and update you when it is done.

Final clean chain aloud.

I have completed the first draft, and I am checking the figures now.

Two entries are still missing, so I have contacted the branch office.

Could you confirm whether the deadline is three or four?

I see your point, but four would be more realistic because the final check takes an hour.

Please save the approved file in the shared folder and let me know when it is uploaded.

We may be delayed by thirty minutes, but I will give you another update at two.

Excellent. ഇന്ന് workplace English-നെ formal decoration-ൽ നിന്ന് practical action-ലേക്ക് കൊണ്ടുവന്നു. Updates-ൽ done, now, next, blocker clear ആക്കി. Meetings-ൽ clarify, contribute, agree or disagree, and summarise ചെയ്തു. Instructions-ൽ exact action, order, standard, deadline നൽകി; receiver ആയി confirm ചെയ്തു. Problems-ൽ facts, impact, action, and needed decision പറഞ്ഞു.

Workplace-ൽ trust build ചെയ്യുന്നത് perfect accent അല്ല. മറ്റൊരാൾക്ക് നിങ്ങളുടെ status മനസ്സിലാക്കാനും, ശരിയായ action എടുക്കാനും, problem surprise ആകുന്നതിന് മുമ്പ് respond ചെയ്യാനും കഴിയണം. Clear, calm, specific English അതാണ് ചെയ്യുന്നത്.
