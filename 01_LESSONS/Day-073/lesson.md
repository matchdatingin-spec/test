# Day 73 — Past Perfect — had + V3, the earlier past

നമസ്കാരം. ഇന്ന് നമ്മൾ past-ലെ രണ്ട് സംഭവങ്ങൾ പറയുമ്പോൾ അതിൽ ഏതാണ് ആദ്യം നടന്നത് എന്ന് വളരെ clear ആക്കാൻ പഠിക്കുകയാണ്. Yesterday ഒരു event നടന്നു. അതിന് മുമ്പ് മറ്റൊരു event already നടന്നിരുന്നു. ആ earlier past കാണിക്കാൻ had plus V3 ഉപയോഗിക്കാം. ഇതാണ് past perfect.

ഒരു situation കേൾക്കൂ.

I reached the station at eight fifteen. The train left at eight.

രണ്ടും past events ആണ്. Train ആദ്യം പോയി. ഞാൻ പിന്നെ station-ൽ എത്തി. ഈ order ഒറ്റ sentence-ൽ clear ആക്കാം.

When I reached the station, the train had left.

I reached എന്നത് later past. Had left എന്നത് earlier past. ഞാൻ എത്തുന്നതിന് മുമ്പ് train പോയിക്കഴിഞ്ഞിരുന്നു.

Pattern വളരെ stable ആണ്.

Subject plus had plus V3.

I had finished.

She had called.

They had gone.

The train had left.

Subject മാറിയാലും had മാറുന്നില്ല. I had, he had, she had, we had, they had. അതിന് ശേഷം verb-ന്റെ third form, V3.

Regular verbs-ൽ V2, V3 പലപ്പോഴും same ആണ്.

Finish, finished, finished.

Call, called, called.

Start, started, started.

Irregular verbs പ്രത്യേകം ശ്രദ്ധിക്കണം.

Go, went, gone.

See, saw, seen.

Eat, ate, eaten.

Write, wrote, written.

Take, took, taken.

Leave, left, left.

Past perfect-ൽ had went അല്ല; had gone. Had saw അല്ല; had seen. Had ate അല്ല; had eaten.

Repeat ചെയ്യൂ.

She had gone.

We had seen it.

He had eaten.

I had written the email.

Good.

Past perfect എന്തിന് വേണം എന്ന് timeline കൊണ്ട് മനസ്സിലാക്കാം. രാവിലെ ഏഴിന് ഞാൻ breakfast finish ചെയ്തു. എട്ടിന് friend എത്തി.

I had finished breakfast before my friend arrived.

Earlier event: had finished. Later event: arrived.

Order sentence-ൽ മറിച്ചും പറയാം.

Before my friend arrived, I had finished breakfast.

Meaning same. Grammar form timeline ആണ് കാണിക്കുന്നത്.

After ഉപയോഗിച്ചും പറയാം.

After I had finished breakfast, my friend arrived.

ഇവിടെ after-clause-ൽ earlier action ആണ്, അതിനാൽ had finished. Later action simple past: arrived.

ഒരു important practical point. Before, after പോലുള്ള time words order ഇതിനകം clear ആക്കുമ്പോൾ everyday English-ൽ രണ്ടിടത്തും simple past കേൾക്കാം.

I finished breakfast before my friend arrived.

ഇത് correct and clear ആണ്. പക്ഷേ past perfect earlier event-നെ പ്രത്യേകം highlight ചെയ്യുന്നു.

I had finished breakfast before my friend arrived.

ഇന്നത്തെ skill എല്ലായിടത്തും had നിറയ്ക്കുക അല്ല. Past timeline confusing ആകാവുന്നിടത്ത് earlier past mark ചെയ്യുകയാണ്.

കാരണം-result story-ലും ഇത് useful ആണ്.

Anu was tired because she had not slept well.

Tired ആയത് later state. അതിന് മുമ്പുള്ള രാത്രി sleep നന്നായില്ല. Had not slept earlier reason കാണിക്കുന്നു.

The room looked different because they had moved the furniture.

Room later looked different. Furniture earlier move ചെയ്തിരുന്നു.

I could not open the door because I had lost the key.

Door തുറക്കാൻ കഴിഞ്ഞില്ല എന്ന later problem-ന്റെ earlier cause: had lost the key.

Negative form easy ആണ്.

Had not plus V3.

I had not checked the date.

She had not received the message.

Natural speech-ൽ had not, hadn’t ആകാം.

I hadn’t checked the date.

She hadn’t received the message.

Hadn’t went എന്നല്ല. Hadn’t gone.

He hadn’t went home when I called.

Pause.

He hadn’t gone home when I called.

ഇനി questions. Had subject-ന് മുമ്പിലേക്ക് വരും.

Had you met him before the meeting?

Had she finished the work when you arrived?

What had they decided before the discussion began?

Short answers:

Yes, I had.

No, I hadn’t.

Question-ൽ did ഉപയോഗിക്കരുത്.

Did you had met him എന്നല്ല.

Had you met him?

Because had തന്നെയാണ് helping verb.

ഒരു mini-story കേൾക്കൂ.

When Maya arrived at the office, everyone was waiting outside. Someone had locked the main door, and the guard had gone for tea. Maya called the guard because she had saved his number. He returned in five minutes and opened the door.

Timeline കണ്ടെത്താം. Maya arrived ഒരു past point. അതിന് മുമ്പ് door lock ചെയ്തിരുന്നു: had locked. Guard പോയിരുന്നു: had gone. Maya അതിലും മുമ്പ് number save ചെയ്തിരുന്നു: had saved. He returned പിന്നീട്. Opened പിന്നീട്.

Story-ൽ ഓരോ past verb-നും had ഉപയോഗിച്ചിട്ടില്ല. Main sequence simple past-ൽ move ചെയ്യുന്നു: arrived, called, returned, opened. അതിന് മുമ്പ് already complete ആയ background actions മാത്രം past perfect-ൽ.

ഇത് വളരെ പ്രധാനമാണ്. ഒരു learner ഇങ്ങനെ പറയാം:

Maya had arrived at the office, had seen everyone, had called the guard, and had waited.

എല്ലാ action-ഉം same sequence-ൽ ആണെങ്കിൽ ഇത്ര had ആവശ്യമില്ല. Natural story:

Maya arrived at the office, saw everyone outside, called the guard, and waited.

പിന്നിൽ നടന്ന earlier information ചേർക്കുമ്പോൾ മാത്രം:

Someone had locked the door.

അതായത് past perfect ഒരു rewind signal പോലെ ആണ്. Main past story-യിൽ നിന്ന് listener-നെ കുറച്ച് പിന്നിലേക്ക് കൊണ്ടുപോകുന്നു.

ഇനി correction chain.

When I reached the cinema, the film already started.

Meaning മനസ്സിലാകും, പക്ഷേ earlier action clear ആക്കാം.

Pause.

When I reached the cinema, the film had already started.

Already സാധാരണ had-നും V3-ക്കും ഇടയിൽ വരാം: had already started.

She had went home before I called.

Pause.

She had gone home before I called.

We had ate dinner when they arrived.

Pause.

We had eaten dinner when they arrived.

He did not had finished the work.

Pause.

He had not finished the work.

Had they saw the message before the meeting?

Pause.

Had they seen the message before the meeting?

Good.

Just, already, never പോലുള്ള words past perfect-നൊപ്പം earlier experience കൂടുതൽ precise ആക്കാം.

The bus had just left when we reached the stop.

ഞങ്ങൾ എത്തിയതിന് തൊട്ടുമുമ്പ് bus പോയിരുന്നു.

I had already sent the file before my manager asked for it.

Manager ചോദിക്കുന്നതിന് മുമ്പേ file അയച്ചിരുന്നു.

She had never travelled alone before that trip.

ആ trip-ന് മുമ്പ് അവൾ ഒരിക്കലും ഒറ്റയ്ക്ക് travel ചെയ്തിട്ടില്ലായിരുന്നു.

Until then, we had never seen such a large crowd.

ആ point വരെ അത്തരം crowd കണ്ടിട്ടില്ലായിരുന്നു.

ഇനി sentence building. രണ്ടു facts നൽകാം. Earlier event-ന് had plus V3 കൊടുക്കൂ.

The guests arrived at seven. We cleaned the room at six.

Pause.

We had cleaned the room before the guests arrived.

അല്ലെങ്കിൽ, When the guests arrived, we had cleaned the room.

The power went off. I saved the document first.

Pause.

I had saved the document before the power went off.

Ravi felt sick. He ate some old food earlier.

Pause.

Ravi felt sick because he had eaten some old food.

The teacher asked for the homework. Nila did not complete it.

Pause.

Nila had not completed the homework when the teacher asked for it.

ഇപ്പോൾ order change. Sentence കേട്ട് first event പറയൂ.

When we entered the hall, the programme had begun.

First event ഏത്?

Pause.

The programme began first. We entered later.

I had spoken to the receptionist before I met the doctor.

First event?

Pause.

I spoke to the receptionist first. I met the doctor later.

After the children had gone to bed, the parents watched a film.

First event?

Pause.

The children went to bed first. The parents watched a film later.

Timeline കേൾക്കാൻ കഴിയുന്നത് form പറയുന്നതിനെക്കാൾ equally important ആണ്.

ഇനി real-life explanation. നിങ്ങൾ late ആയതിന്റെ reason പറയുകയാണ്.

I arrived late because I had missed the first bus. I had set an alarm, but my phone battery had died during the night. By the time I woke up, the bus had already left. I took the next bus and called the office.

ഇവിടെ arrived late main result. Missed bus അതിന് മുമ്പ്. Alarm set ചെയ്തത്, battery died, bus left—ഇവ relevant earlier actions. അവസാന main action took and called simple past-ൽ story മുന്നോട്ട് കൊണ്ടുപോകുന്നു.

By the time എന്ന phrase later point എത്തുമ്പോഴേക്കും earlier event complete ആയെന്ന് വ്യക്തമാക്കുന്നു.

By the time we reached the restaurant, the kitchen had closed.

By the time the meeting started, everyone had read the report.

ഇത് useful ആണ്, പക്ഷേ pattern overload വേണ്ട. Later point simple past. അതിന് മുമ്പ് complete action had plus V3.

ഇനി നിങ്ങളുടെ turn. Yesterday ഒരു appointment-ന് late ആയെന്ന് imagine ചെയ്യൂ. Earlier reasons choose ചെയ്യാം: alarm did not ring, bus left, phone battery died, wrong address saved. മൂന്ന് past perfect sentences, പിന്നെ ഒരു simple past ending പറയൂ.

Pause.

ഒരു model കേൾക്കൂ.

I was late for the appointment because my alarm had not rung. I had saved the wrong address, so I went to another building first. By the time I found the correct place, the receptionist had called me twice. I apologised and explained what had happened.

What had happened എന്ന question-like clause ഇവിടെ whole earlier situation-നെ സൂചിപ്പിക്കുന്നു. പക്ഷേ ഇന്നത്തെ focus timeline തന്നെയാണ്.

Past perfect വേണ്ടാത്തിടവും തിരിച്ചറിയാം.

Yesterday I had woken up, had brushed my teeth, had eaten breakfast, and had left home.

ഇത് simple chronological list മാത്രം. Better:

Yesterday I woke up, brushed my teeth, ate breakfast, and left home.

But:

When I left home, I realised that I had forgotten my wallet.

Left, realised main sequence. Wallet forget ചെയ്തത് അതിന് മുമ്പ്. അതിനാൽ had forgotten useful ആണ്.

മറ്റൊരു contrast.

I lost my key and called my brother.

First lost, then called; and order clear.

I called my brother because I had lost my key.

ഇവിടെ call-ന്റെ earlier reason highlight ചെയ്യുന്നു.

ഇനി guided memory practice. നിങ്ങളുടെ കഴിഞ്ഞ ആഴ്ചയിലെ ഒരു small problem ഓർക്കൂ. Problem കണ്ടെത്തിയ moment simple past-ൽ പറയൂ. അതിന് മുമ്പ് നടന്ന രണ്ട് കാര്യങ്ങൾ past perfect-ൽ പറയൂ. പിന്നെ നിങ്ങൾ എടുത്ത action simple past-ൽ പറയൂ.

For example:

On Friday, I noticed that my bag was missing. I had left it in a café, and I had not written my phone number inside it. I called the café, and an employee found it near my table.

ഇപ്പോൾ നിങ്ങളുടെ story പറയൂ.

Pause.

Self-check ചെയ്യൂ. Had കഴിഞ്ഞ് V3 വന്നോ? Had left, had written, had seen, had taken. Main story മുഴുവൻ had കൊണ്ട് heavy ആക്കിയോ? അങ്ങനെ ആണെങ്കിൽ chronological actions simple past ആക്കൂ; earlier background മാത്രം past perfect ആക്കൂ.

I was hungry because I had not ate anything.

Pause.

I was hungry because I had not eaten anything.

ഇത് already correct ആണ്. Repeat ചെയ്യൂ.

Yes, I had. Or, No, I hadn’t.

ഇപ്പോൾ independent speaking. ഒരു missed train, forgotten item, unexpected closed shop, അല്ലെങ്കിൽ delayed meeting എന്ന topic choose ചെയ്യൂ. Story-യുടെ main past moment ആദ്യം പറയൂ. പിന്നെ listener-ന് ആവശ്യമായ earlier causes past perfect ഉപയോഗിച്ച് explain ചെയ്യൂ. Finally എന്ത് ചെയ്തു എന്ന് simple past-ൽ finish ചെയ്യൂ. ഏകദേശം ഒരു minute സംസാരിക്കൂ.

ഇന്നത്തെ proof aloud.

I can use had plus V3 for an action completed before another past event. I use the same had with every subject. I know important third forms such as gone, seen, eaten, written, and taken. I can make negatives with had not and questions by putting had before the subject. I use simple past for the main story and past perfect only when I need to show the earlier past clearly.

അവസാന timeline കേൾക്കൂ.

When I opened my bag, I realised that I had left my notebook at home.

Opened and realised ആണ് later past scene. Had left ആണ് rewind. Listener ഉടനെ order മനസ്സിലാക്കുന്നു. അതാണ് past perfect-ന്റെ ജോലി. Had plus V3 വെറും longer grammar form അല്ല; past-ലെ രണ്ട് moments കുഴയാതെ, ആദ്യം നടന്ന കാര്യം clear ആയി കാണിക്കുന്ന timeline tool ആണ്.
