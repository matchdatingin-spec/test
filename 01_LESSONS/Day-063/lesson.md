Now shadowing. Shadowing means listen to a short model phrase and follow it closely, copying timing, strong words, and intonation while preserving clarity. You are not memorising new content. You are not competing with audio speed. First echo after model. Then overlap or follow with only a tiny delay. If exact overlap is difficult, half-second delay is acceptable.

Shadowing rule one: meaning first. If you do not understand line, shadow becomes empty sound copying. We will check meaning before full run.

Rule two: phrase, not individual word. Copy I need to PICK it UP at SIX as one thought, not seven separate blocks.

Rule three: key details stay clear. Names, numbers, negatives, times, and places cannot disappear.

Rule four: if you lose one phrase, do not chase it at double speed. Skip, breathe, join next phrase.
