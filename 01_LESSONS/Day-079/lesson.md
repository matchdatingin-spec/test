# Day 79 — Storytelling — narrating an incident from beginning to end

നമസ്കാരം. ഇന്ന് ഒരു incident beginning മുതൽ end വരെ listener-ന് clear ആയി narrate ചെയ്യാം. Grammar rules ഒറ്റയ്ക്ക് പറയുന്നത് അല്ല ലക്ഷ്യം. നിങ്ങൾ എവിടെയായിരുന്നു, normal situation എന്തായിരുന്നു, എന്ത് unexpected event വന്നു, നിങ്ങൾ എന്ത് ചെയ്തു, ഒടുവിൽ എന്ത് സംഭവിച്ചു—ഈ complete journey ആണ് storytelling.

ഒരു story interesting ആകാൻ വലിയ accident അല്ലെങ്കിൽ dramatic adventure വേണ്ട. Wrong bus എടുത്തത്, key മറന്നത്, മഴയിൽ കുടുങ്ങിയത്, stranger സഹായിച്ചത്, ഒരു appointment almost miss ചെയ്തത്—ഇതെല്ലാം good incidents ആണ്. പ്രധാനമായത് event sequence listener-ന് confuse ആകരുത്.

ഒരു weak version കേൾക്കൂ.

Yesterday bus, I was late, one man, phone, then office, problem solved.

Words ഉണ്ടെങ്കിലും story ഇല്ല. Who, where, what happened, why it mattered, how it ended—ഒന്നും connected അല്ല.

ഇനി clearer version കേൾക്കൂ.

Yesterday morning, I was travelling to an appointment when I realised that I was on the wrong bus. I got off at the next stop and checked the route on my phone, but the battery died. A shopkeeper showed me a shorter way to the main road. I found another bus, reached the office ten minutes late, and explained what had happened. Fortunately, the appointment had not started, so everything ended well.

ഇവിടെ beginning ഉണ്ട്, unexpected turn ഉണ്ട്, response ഉണ്ട്, ending ഉണ്ട്. Listener mentally follow ചെയ്യാം.

Story പറയുന്നതിന് മുമ്പ് five anchors mentally hold ചെയ്യൂ. ഇത് list recite ചെയ്യാനുള്ള exercise അല്ല; mind-ലുള്ള route ആണ്. Setting. Trigger. Response. Result. Final feeling or lesson. എവിടെ, എപ്പോൾ? എന്താണ് സംഭവം തുടങ്ങിച്ചത്? നിങ്ങൾ പിന്നെ എന്ത് ചെയ്തു? outcome എന്ത്? ഇപ്പോൾ അതിനെക്കുറിച്ച് എന്താണ് തോന്നുന്നത്?

ആദ്യ anchor setting ആണ്. Story തുടങ്ങുന്ന സമയം listener-നെ നിലത്ത് ഇറക്കണം.

Last Saturday evening, I was waiting outside a small clinic.

A few months ago, my family and I went to a village festival.

One morning, while I was getting ready for work, I heard a strange sound from the kitchen.

Setting വളരെ നീളരുത്. Weather, dress, family history എല്ലാം പറഞ്ഞ് main event delay ചെയ്യേണ്ട. Incident മനസ്സിലാക്കാൻ ആവശ്യമായ time, place, people മാത്രം.

Quick opening practice. “Market, Sunday morning, with my cousin” എന്ന details ഉപയോഗിച്ച് opening പറയൂ.

Pause.

“Home, late evening, alone” ഉപയോഗിക്കൂ.

Pause.

One evening, I was at home alone when the lights suddenly went out.

Past continuous known tool ആണ്. Background action चलിക്കുമ്പോൾ short event interrupt ചെയ്താൽ useful ആണ്.

Was walking gives background. Saw gives event.

We were having dinner when someone knocked on the door.

Were having background. Knocked event.

Repair ചെയ്യൂ.

Pause.

While we talked, the phone was ringing.

Pause.

ഇനി trigger. Story move ചെയ്യിക്കുന്ന moment signal ചെയ്യണം.

Suddenly, I noticed that my bag was missing.

Just then, the driver stopped the bus.

A few minutes later, I received an unexpected call.

When I opened the box, I found someone else’s order inside.

Suddenly എല്ലാ sentence-ലും വേണ്ട. One key turning point-ന് ഉപയോഗിച്ചാൽ effect ഉണ്ട്. Then, then, then എന്ന് mechanical chain ആക്കാതെ time links vary ചെയ്യാം: at first, a few minutes later, while, when, after that, eventually, in the end.

Sequence practice. ഈ fragments full story chain ആക്കൂ: waiting at station; train delayed; called friend; took bus; arrived on time.

Pause.

I was waiting at the station when I heard that the train was delayed. I called my friend and explained the problem. Then I took a bus from the next street. After a slow journey, I finally arrived on time.

Clear ആണ്, പക്ഷേ richer incident-ന് cause and response ചേർക്കാം.

Because the train was delayed, I looked for another way to travel. Since my phone had little battery left, I called my friend before checking the bus route. The bus was crowded, but it reached the town just in time.

ഇവിടെ because and so overuse ഇല്ലാതെ logic clear ആണ്.

Story timeline-ൽ simple past main road ആണ്.

I left the house, walked to the stop, and boarded the bus.

Past continuous background lane ആണ്.

People were hurrying home, and rain was falling heavily.

Past perfect earlier-past signpost ആണ്.

I realised that I had left my wallet at home.

Had left main incident-നേക്കാൾ മുമ്പ് സംഭവിച്ചു. എല്ലാ past sentence-ലും had ഇടേണ്ട. Once earlier relationship clear ആയാൽ simple past-ലേക്ക് return ചെയ്യാം.

Wrong version കേൾക്കൂ.

ഇത് unnecessary past perfect chain ആണ്. Main sequence simple past മതി.

I reached the stop, waited for a few minutes, and got on the bus when it arrived.

Past perfect genuinely useful ആകുന്ന point:

When the conductor asked for my ticket, I discovered that I had boarded the wrong bus.

Boarding earlier. Discovery later.

ഇപ്പോൾ choose ചെയ്യൂ. Simple past ആണോ past perfect ആണോ?

By the time I returned to the café, someone blank my umbrella to the counter.

Take എന്ന verb.

Pause.

By the time I returned to the café, someone had taken my umbrella to the counter.

Earlier action, so had taken.

I blank the assistant and collected it.

Thank എന്ന verb.

Pause.

I thanked the assistant and collected it.

Main sequence, so simple past.

Day 78-ൽ പഠിച്ച passive story-ൽ occasionally result focus ചെയ്യാൻ സഹായിക്കും.

പക്ഷേ full story passive ആക്കരുത്. I was gone to the shop തെറ്റാണ്. I went to the shop. I was helped by a guard possible ആണ്, എന്നാൽ A guard helped me everyday story-ൽ കൂടുതൽ direct. Focus natural ആയി choose ചെയ്യൂ.

ഇനി people and speech. Incident-ൽ someone said something important ആണെങ്കിൽ exact dialogue മുഴുവൻ act ചെയ്യേണ്ട. Short direct line അല്ലെങ്കിൽ known reported speech ഉപയോഗിക്കാം.

Or:

The driver told me that the bus did not go to Central Road.

Then I asked him where I should get off.

Story-ൽ speaker clear ആയിരിക്കണം. He said, then he told him, then he went എന്ന chain listener-നെ confuse ചെയ്യും. Names or roles repeat ചെയ്യൂ.

The driver spoke to the conductor. He told him to stop near the bank.

Who told whom? Context may help, but safer version:

The driver told the conductor to stop near the bank.

Clarity elegance-നെക്കാൾ important ആണ്.

ഇനി ഒരു complete incident കേൾക്കൂ. കേൾക്കുമ്പോൾ setting, trigger, response, result, final thought identify ചെയ്യൂ.

Last month, I went to a bookshop to buy a gift for my niece. The shop was crowded because a sale was going on. While I was looking at the children’s books, I put my phone on a shelf for a moment. A few minutes later, I chose a book and walked to the payment counter. Then I reached for my phone and realised that it was missing. I went back to the shelf, but it was not there. I told a staff member what had happened, and she asked me to call my number from the shop phone. We heard it ringing near a stack of notebooks. Another customer had found it and had given it to the cashier. I thanked both of them, paid for the book, and left with a huge sense of relief. The incident reminded me not to put my phone down carelessly in a busy place.

Setting: last month, bookshop, gift. Trigger: phone missing. Response: returned, told staff member, called number. Result: customer had found and returned it. Final thought: relief and lesson.

Story-ൽ details selective ആണ്. Niece-ന്റെ age, shop wall colour, book price എന്നിവ incident outcome-ന് വേണ്ടതല്ല, അതുകൊണ്ട് omit ചെയ്തു. നാളത്തെ lesson vivid description-ന് പ്രത്യേകമായി focus ചെയ്യും. ഇന്ന് description story movement stop ചെയ്യാത്തത്ര മാത്രം.
