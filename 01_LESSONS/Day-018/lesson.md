# Day 18 — Don't / Doesn't: Making Negative Sentences

**Phase 1 — Foundation | Week 3**

## Learning Objective

The learner can make negative Present Simple sentences with **don't** and **doesn't**, without adding **-s** to the main verb.

## Why This Matters

Day 4-il *"I am not a teacher"* പഠിച്ചു — be-verb-inte negative.

ഇന്ന് **action verbs-inte negative**:
> ഞാൻ ചായ കുടിക്കില്ല. · അവൻ ഇവിടെ ജോലി ചെയ്യുന്നില്ല.

ഇന്നലെ ചോദ്യത്തിന് *do/does* ഉപയോഗിച്ചു. ഇന്ന് അതേ സഹായി **negative-inum** വരും. ഒരേ ഉപകരണം, രണ്ട് ജോലി.

## Lesson

### The rule

| Subject | Negative |
|---|---|
| I, You, We, They | **don't** (do not) |
| He, She, It | **doesn't** (does not) |

**Subject + don't / doesn't + main verb (base form)**

> I **don't** drink coffee.
> You **don't** need a ticket.
> We **don't** live here.
> They **don't** eat meat.
> He **doesn't** work on Sunday.
> She **doesn't** like tea.
> It **doesn't** rain in December.

### ⚠️ വീണ്ടും: -s ഒരിക്കൽ മാത്രം

*doesn't* ഇൽ "-s" ഉണ്ട്. Main verb-inu വേണ്ട:

> ❌ She doesn't **likes** tea. → ✅ She doesn't **like** tea.
> ❌ He doesn't **goes** there. → ✅ He doesn't **go** there.

### Positive → Negative

> I like coffee. → I **don't like** coffee.
> She works here. → She **doesn't work** here. *(works → work!)*
> They watch TV. → They **don't watch** TV.
> He has a car. → He **doesn't have** a car. *(has → have!)*

**Verb എപ്പോഴും base form-il തിരിച്ചുപോകും.** ഇത് ശ്രദ്ധിക്കുക.

### be-verb vs action verb — രണ്ട് വേറെ വഴി

| Type | Negative |
|---|---|
| I **am** a teacher. | I **am not** a teacher. |
| I **work** here. | I **don't work** here. |
| He **is** busy. | He **isn't** busy. |
| He **works** here. | He **doesn't work** here. |

> ❌ I don't am a teacher. → ✅ I **am not** a teacher.
> ❌ I am not work here. → ✅ I **don't work** here.

### Full form vs short form

| Full | Short |
|---|---|
| do not | **don't** |
| does not | **doesn't** |

സംസാരത്തിൽ എപ്പോഴും **don't / doesn't**. Full form ഉപയോഗിക്കുന്നത് ഊന്നൽ കൊടുക്കാൻ മാത്രം:
> I **do not** want it. *(ദൃഢമായി)*

### Useful everyday negatives

> I don't know. *(ഏറ്റവും കൂടുതൽ ഉപയോഗിക്കുന്ന negative!)*
> I don't understand.
> I don't think so.
> I don't have time.
> It doesn't matter.
> She doesn't speak English.

ഇവ **ചേർത്തുപറയേണ്ട ready-made phrases** ആയി ഓർക്കുക.

## Common Malayalam-Speaker Mistakes

1. **doesn't-inte കൂടെ -s**
❌ He doesn't works. → ✅ He doesn't **work**.

2. **don't he**
❌ He don't like tea. → ✅ He **doesn't** like tea.
*(ഇത് വളരെ സാധാരണം. He/She/It → **doesn't**.)*

3. **be-verb-um don't-um ഒരുമിച്ച്**
❌ I don't am ready. → ✅ I **am not** ready.

4. **not മാത്രം ഉപയോഗിക്കുന്നത്**
❌ I not like coffee. → ✅ I **don't** like coffee.
Malayalam-il "കുടിക്കില്ല" — verb-il തന്നെ negative. English-il **വേറെ ഒരു വാക്ക് വേണം.**

5. **doesn't have → doesn't has**
❌ She doesn't has a car. → ✅ She doesn't **have** a car.

6. **Double negative**
❌ I don't know nothing. → ✅ I don't know **anything**. / I know **nothing**.

## Guided Practice

don't / doesn't ഇടുക:

1. I ___ eat fish.
2. He ___ work here.
3. They ___ live in Kochi.
4. She ___ like coffee.
5. It ___ rain much here.
6. We ___ have a car.

*Answers: 1. don't 2. doesn't 3. don't 4. doesn't 5. doesn't 6. don't*

Negative ആക്കുക:
7. She goes to college. →
8. They watch TV. →
9. He has two cars. →
10. I understand this. →

*Answers: 7. She doesn't go to college. 8. They don't watch TV. 9. He doesn't have two cars. 10. I don't understand this.*

തെറ്റ് തിരുത്തുക:
11. He don't likes tea. →
12. I not have time. →
13. She doesn't has a phone. →

*Answers: 11. He doesn't like tea. 12. I don't have time. 13. She doesn't have a phone.*

## Speaking Practice

**Ten things you don't do.** ഉറക്കെ പറയുക:

> I don't smoke. · I don't drink coffee. · I don't eat meat. · I don't watch TV in the morning. · I don't work on Sunday…

ഇനി മറ്റൊരാളെക്കുറിച്ച് **അഞ്ച് doesn't sentences**:
> My father doesn't ___. · My sister doesn't ___.

**Mixed drill** — നാല് രൂപവും മാറി മാറി:
> I am not tired. · I don't work here. · He isn't busy. · He doesn't work here.

## Quick Review

- **don't** = I/You/We/They · **doesn't** = He/She/It
- **doesn't-inte കൂടെ main verb-inu -s ഇല്ല**
- Verb എപ്പോഴും **base form**
- be-verb-inu **am not / isn't / aren't** — don't അല്ല
- **I don't know** — ഏറ്റവും ഉപകാരമുള്ള negative

4. ഞാൻ തിരക്കിലല്ല →
