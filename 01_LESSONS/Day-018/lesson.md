Don't is the spoken short form of do not. Doesn't is the spoken short form of does not. Everyday natural speech-ൽ don't and doesn't very common. Meaning simply action or fact is not true for that subject.

Subject map already familiar. I, you, we, they and plural subjects use don't. I don't eat meat. You don't live here. We don't start at eight. They don't watch television. My parents don't work on Sunday.

He, she, it, names, and singular subjects use doesn't. He doesn't eat meat. She doesn't live here. It doesn't work. Ravi doesn't watch television. My mother doesn't work on Sunday.

Repeat the contrast. I don't work. You don't work. We don't work. They don't work. He doesn't work. She doesn't work. It doesn't work.

Malayalam speaker often places not directly before a verb: I not understand. English Present Simple action negative needs don't: I don't understand. Another common mix comes from be: I am not understand. Understand is action or mental main verb in this pattern; say I don't understand.

ഇനി frequency meaning. I never drink coffee and I don't drink coffee can both suggest zero coffee in normal context. Never explicitly means at no time and sits before the main verb. Choose one clear route: I never drink coffee, or I don't drink coffee.

Finished? Conceptual recap. Present Simple action negative uses subject plus don't or doesn't plus base-form main verb. I, you, we, they, and plural subjects use don't. He, she, it, names, and singular subjects use doesn't. Main verb never keeps third-person s after doesn't: doesn't work, doesn't go, doesn't study, doesn't watch, doesn't have. Malayalam negative transfer may give I not work; English needs I don't work. Be meanings follow their own learned route: I am not tired, she isn't at home, they aren't ready.

One final switch പറയൂ. She works here. She doesn't work here. Does she work here? No, she doesn't. He studies at night. He doesn't study at night. Does he study at night? No, he doesn't.

Positive statement carries s on the main verb. Question carries does before subject. Negative statement carries doesn't after subject. Question and negative both release main verb back to base form.
