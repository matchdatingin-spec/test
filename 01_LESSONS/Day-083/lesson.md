# Day 83 — Discussion and Debate — supporting your opinion with reasons

നമസ്കാരം. ഇന്ന് opinion പറയുന്നതിൽ നിന്ന് ഒരു step മുന്നോട്ട് പോകാം. “I agree,” “I don’t like it,” “This is best” എന്ന one-line reaction discussion complete ആക്കില്ല. നിങ്ങളുടെ position എന്ത്, എന്തുകൊണ്ട്, example or evidence എന്ത്, opposite view-ൽ valid point എന്ത്, conclusion എന്ത്—ഇവ support ചെയ്യണം.

Debate എന്ന് കേൾക്കുമ്പോൾ shouting competition imagine ചെയ്യേണ്ട. Good discussion-ൽ goal മറ്റൊരാളെ defeat ചെയ്യുക അല്ല. Idea test ചെയ്യുക, reasons compare ചെയ്യുക, practical conclusion കണ്ടെത്തുക.

ഒരു unsupported opinion കേൾക്കൂ.

Online classes are bad. I don’t like them.

Speaker feeling അറിയാം. Reason അറിയില്ല. Stronger version:

I prefer face-to-face classes because learners can ask questions more naturally and teachers can notice confusion more quickly. Online classes are convenient, especially for people who live far away, but they can be difficult when the internet connection is weak. For that reason, I think important practical lessons should be held in person when possible.

Position, two reasons, opposite advantage, condition, conclusion.

ഇന്നത്തെ basic speaking chain simple ആണ്. State your view. Give a reason. Explain the reason. Add an example. Acknowledge another view. Respond. Conclude. എല്ലായ്പ്പോഴും every step വേണമെന്നില്ല, പക്ഷേ one reason and support almost always വേണം.

Opinion signal natural ആക്കാം.

I think public parks should stay open later.

In my view, the bus service needs more evening routes.

From a parent’s point of view, the main concern is safety.

For me, reliability is more important than low cost.

“As per my opinion” എന്നത് avoid ചെയ്യൂ. Natural: In my opinion. “According to me” widely heard ആണ്, പക്ഷേ natural personal view-ന് I think or In my view better. According to usually source report ചെയ്യുന്നു: According to the timetable, the last bus leaves at nine.

ഇനി reason signal.

I support the plan because it gives more people access.

The main reason is that the current room is too small.

One advantage is that families will save travel time.

This matters because many workers finish after six.

Reason same idea repeat ചെയ്യരുത്.

I think it is useful because it is very useful.

No support. Better:

I think the shared calendar is useful because everyone can see deadline changes immediately.

Explanation practical consequence കാണിക്കുന്നു.

Opinion plus reason practice.

Topic: local library should open on Sunday. Reason: students and workers free.

Pause.

I think the local library should open on Sunday because many students and full-time workers are free only at the weekend.

Explain one step more.

Pause.

A Sunday service would give them time to borrow books, study quietly, and use the computers without missing work or class.

Example add ചെയ്യൂ.

Pause.

For example, several students in my area travel to another town on Sundays because our library is closed.

Specific example argument concrete ആക്കുന്നു. Example invented fact ആയി present ചെയ്യരുത്. Real data ഇല്ലെങ്കിൽ hypothetical signal കൊടുക്കൂ.

For example, a student who works during the week could use the library on Sunday.

This is an illustration, not a claimed statistic.

Facts and opinions distinguish ചെയ്യണം.

The centre closes at five.

Check ചെയ്യാവുന്ന fact.

The centre closes too early.

Opinion, because “too early” depends on need.

Fact support opinion:

The centre closes at five, but many commuters return after six. In my view, it should remain open for at least one more hour.

Numbers invent ചെയ്യരുത്. “Ninety percent of people agree” evidence ഇല്ലെങ്കിൽ harmful. Honest language:

Many people I have spoken to prefer the earlier time.

In my own experience, the morning bus is usually crowded.

I do not have exact figures, but the repeated delays suggest that the schedule needs review.

Credibility protect ചെയ്യുക.

ഇനി supporting reason types. Practical effect: saves time, reduces cost, improves access. Human effect: reduces stress, supports families, improves safety. Quality effect: gives better learning, fewer errors. Fairness: equal opportunity. Long-term effect: maintenance, environment, skill growth.

Topic: school students should have a short daily reading period. Two different reasons പറയൂ.

Pause.

A daily reading period would help students build vocabulary over time. It would also give every child regular access to books, including children who may not have many books at home.

Two reasons different ആണ്: learning and access.

Weak reason pile:

It is good, useful, helpful, and beneficial.

Same idea four words. Better two developed reasons.

ഇനി example and explanation connect ചെയ്യാൻ:

For example, ...

This means that ...

As a result, ...

That is important because ...

In practice, ...

A smaller first event would cost less. In practice, the organisers could test the plan without risking the full budget.

Reason consequence clear.

ഇനി disagreement. Person-നെ reject ചെയ്യാതെ idea challenge ചെയ്യൂ.

You are wrong.

Too personal. Better:

I see the situation differently.

I understand that concern, but I do not think closing the gate is the best solution.

That may solve the noise problem, but it could create an access problem for older residents.

I agree with the goal, but not with the proposed method.

Strong disagreement പോലും calm ആയി:

I cannot support that proposal because it would exclude people who do not have internet access.

Clear, reasoned, not insulting.

Agreement-നും support വേണം.

I agree with Mira’s point because the evening option gives working parents more time.

I agree in principle, but we still need a plan for transport.

Partial agreement intelligent discussion-ൽ common ആണ്. Everything yes or no അല്ല.

ഇനി another view acknowledge ചെയ്യൂ. Opposite side weak version deliberately create ചെയ്ത് attack ചെയ്യരുത്. Fair version പറയൂ.

Some people prefer the online option because it is cheaper and easier to attend from different locations.

Then response:

That is a real advantage. However, practical demonstrations are harder to follow on a small screen, so I would use online sessions for theory and in-person sessions for practice.

Acknowledgement position surrender അല്ല. Discussion fair ആക്കുന്നു.

Useful balance words: however, on the other hand, at the same time, although, even though. ഇവ one sentence-ൽ all pile ചെയ്യരുത്.

Although the new venue is cheaper, it is difficult to reach by bus.

The hall is expensive. However, it already has the equipment we need.

On the other hand comparison-ൽ use ചെയ്യാം.

The town centre is convenient for bus users. On the other hand, parking is limited.

Common correction.

Even though it is expensive, but it is reliable.

Pause.

Even though it is expensive, it is reliable. Or, It is expensive, but it is reliable.

Because the road is narrow, so large buses cannot enter.

Pause.

Because the road is narrow, large buses cannot enter. Or, The road is narrow, so large buses cannot enter.

One connector pair enough.

ഇനി opinion strength control ചെയ്യാം.

This is definitely the only solution.

Strong claim evidence ആവശ്യപ്പെടും. Uncertainty ഉണ്ടെങ്കിൽ qualify ചെയ്യൂ.

This seems to be the most practical option at the moment.

In many cases, a shorter meeting is more effective.

The plan may work for small teams, but it could be difficult for larger offices.

All, always, never, everyone എന്നിവ easy but risky. “People always waste time in meetings” exaggeration. Better: Meetings often waste time when there is no clear agenda.

Condition known language use ചെയ്ത് position precise ആക്കാം.

I would support the plan if evening transport were available.

If the trial succeeds, we can extend the service.

If the cost rises further, the cheaper option may become necessary.

Condition tells listener what could change your view. ഇത് intellectual honesty ആണ്.

ഇനി one-minute argument build ചെയ്യാം. Topic: town should create a car-free street on Sunday mornings. Facts: local shops want more visitors; families want safe walking space; some drivers need access; nearby parking available but limited.

Position choose ചെയ്യൂ.

Pause.

ഒരു model pro position:

I support a car-free street on Sunday mornings because it would give families a safer place to walk and could bring more people to local shops. For example, cafés and small stores could serve visitors who spend the morning in the area. I understand that some drivers need direct access, especially for deliveries and older passengers. However, deliveries could be allowed before the road closes, and a small drop-off area could remain open. I would begin with a one-month trial and review the effect before making the change permanent.

Claim, reasons, example, concern, response, condition-like trial conclusion.

ഇനി opposite position fairly പറയൂ.

Pause.

ഒരു possible version:

I like the idea of a safer walking area, but I do not support closing the whole street yet. Nearby parking is already limited, and some residents may have difficulty reaching their homes. A trial could also affect shops that depend on quick vehicle access. I would first close only half the street and create clear delivery hours. If that works without causing serious access problems, a wider closure could be considered later.

Opposite argument also reasonable. Debate skill means only your favourite side സംസാരിക്കാൻ കഴിയുന്നത് അല്ല.

ഇനി ask and respond. Discussion two-way ആണ്.

What is the main reason you support that option?

How would your plan deal with the extra cost?

Could you give an example?

Would your view change if public transport improved?

What do you think is the biggest disadvantage?

Question idea test ചെയ്യണം, person trap ചെയ്യരുത്.

Response before disagreement:

That’s a fair question. My main concern is not the initial cost but the yearly maintenance cost.

I had not considered that point. It makes the plan less simple, although I still support a short trial.

Admitting a useful point weakness അല്ല. It shows listening.

ഇനി mini dialogue role-play. Speaker A says:

I think all staff should work from home three days a week because it would reduce travel time.

Speaker B ആയി one clarification and one concern പറയൂ.

Pause.

Do you mean every team, including reception and maintenance? My concern is that some services require staff to be physically present.

Speaker A response:

That’s true. I would limit the policy to roles that can be done remotely, and each team could choose suitable days.

Speaker B partial agreement:

Pause.

In that case, I support trying it with eligible teams, provided customer service remains available throughout the week.

Discussion progressed because terms became precise.

ഇനി common Malayalam-influenced discussion corrections.

I am agree with this.

Pause.

I agree with this.

I don’t agree to your opinion.

Pause.

I don’t agree with that view. Or, I see it differently.

What you are telling is correct.

Pause.

What you are saying is correct. More natural: I agree with your point.

I have a different opinion than you.

Natural version:

Pause.

I have a different view. Or, I see this differently from you.

My point is, why because the cost is high.

Pause.

My main point is that the cost is high. Or, I oppose it because the cost is high.

There have many advantages.

Pause.

There are many advantages. Better: One major advantage is easier access.

“Only” placement ശ്രദ്ധിക്കൂ.

We discussed the budget only.

Depending on meaning, ഇത് budget and nothing else. “Yesterday only we discussed” intended recent emphasis ആണെങ്കിൽ: We discussed it only yesterday, or We did not discuss it until yesterday.

ഇനി reason ladder. Topic: phones should be limited during meetings. First claim.

Pause.

Phones should be limited during meetings.

Reason.

Pause.

They can distract both the user and other participants.

Example.

Pause.

For example, a person who keeps reading messages may miss an important decision or repeat a question that has already been answered.

Counterpoint.

Pause.

However, some staff need phones for urgent work or family calls.

Balanced proposal.

Pause.

For that reason, phones should remain on silent mode, but urgent calls should be allowed outside the room.

Conclusion.

Pause.

A flexible rule would protect attention without creating unnecessary problems.

Excellent. One-line opinion became complete argument.

ഇനി evaluate sources in ordinary speech. ആരെങ്കിലും says, “They said it is true.” Who are they? Clarify.

The local office announced that the road would reopen on Monday.

A neighbour told me that the evening bus is often late.

I read the updated schedule on the transport department’s website.

Source strength differs. Personal report useful but not official proof. Debate-ൽ source accurately label ചെയ്യൂ. Fake authority avoid ചെയ്യുക.

ഇനി emotional topic-ൽ respectful boundary. “Anyone who disagrees is selfish” person attack. Better:

I think this policy would have an unfair effect on low-income families because they would pay a larger share of their income.

Impact critique, character judgment അല്ല.

If discussion becomes unclear, summarise.

So far, we agree that the building needs repair. The remaining question is whether the work should happen all at once or in stages.

Summary shared ground and disagreement separate ചെയ്യുന്നു.

Conclusion new reason introduce ചെയ്യേണ്ട സ്ഥലം അല്ല. Main position and strongest reason restate ചെയ്യൂ.

Overall, I support a short trial because it gives us real information without committing the full budget.

For these reasons, I would keep the current system but improve the evening schedule.

On balance, the second option seems more practical, although its transport problem must be solved first.

ഇനി first independent discussion turn. Topic choose ചെയ്യൂ: shops should stop giving plastic bags; students should do more group projects; public offices should offer one late evening each week; local events should charge a small entry fee.

One minute. Position. Two different reasons. One example. Opposite concern. Response. Conclusion.

Pause.

Self-check. Opinion first thirty seconds-ൽ clear ആണോ? Because ശേഷം real reason ഉണ്ടോ? Example reason support ചെയ്യുന്നുണ്ടോ? Opposite side fair ആയി stated ആണോ? Response concern address ചെയ്തോ? All or never പോലുള്ള exaggeration ഉണ്ടോ? Conclusion complete ആണോ?

ഇനി switch sides. Same topic-ൽ opposite position forty-five seconds argue ചെയ്യൂ. നിങ്ങൾ personally agree ചെയ്യേണ്ട. Strongest reasonable reasons find ചെയ്യൂ.

Pause.

ഇത് debate flexibility ആണ്. Opposite side understand ചെയ്താൽ സ്വന്തം argument refine ചെയ്യാം.

ഇനി quick response rounds.

Claim: Free public transport would reduce traffic.

Ask one clarification.

Pause.

Would it be free for everyone or only during busy hours?

Give one supporting reason.

Pause.

Lower fares could encourage some commuters to leave their cars at home.

Give one concern.

Pause.

However, the town would need a reliable way to pay for more buses and maintenance.

Offer balanced proposal.

Pause.

A cheaper monthly pass may be more realistic than making every journey completely free.

Next claim: Every meeting should be online.

Acknowledge advantage and challenge claim.

Pause.

Online meetings save travel time, but practical demonstrations and sensitive discussions may work better in person.

Condition.

Pause.

I would support online meetings when all participants can join easily and no physical demonstration is needed.

ഇനി two-minute independent debate response. Topic: community centre has money for either a computer room or a larger sports area, not both. Consider children, adults, cost, access, maintenance, long-term use. Choose one. Support with reasons. Acknowledge the other option’s value. Explain why your priority is stronger. State what evidence could change your view. Conclude.

Pause.

Repair take. Reason repeated ആണെങ്കിൽ second category add ചെയ്യൂ. Counterpoint missing ആണെങ്കിൽ one fair sentence add ചെയ്യൂ. Conclusion weak ആണെങ്കിൽ “On balance” sentence പറയൂ. Personal attack വന്നെങ്കിൽ policy impact ആയി reframe ചെയ്യൂ.

Pause.

Final dialogue simulation. Imaginary partner says:

The sports area should be chosen because physical activity benefits everyone.

Respond with acknowledgement plus clarification.

Pause.

I agree that physical activity is important. When you say everyone, are you including older residents and people with limited mobility?

Partner says:

The design could include a walking track and simple exercise equipment for different age groups.

Now give your computer-room argument and one condition for compromise.

Pause.

That would make the sports area more inclusive. I still think a computer room may give greater access to education and job services, especially for families without devices at home. However, if the centre can show that most residents already have reliable computer access, I would support the inclusive sports plan.

Partner asks for conclusion.

Pause.

On balance, I would choose the computer room because it addresses an access gap that is difficult for individual families to solve. The sports proposal has real value, but outdoor exercise options already exist nearby.

Final clean chain aloud.

In my view, the evening service should continue because many workers depend on it.

The main reason is that the current closing time excludes people who finish work after six.

For example, a parent may need to collect a document without taking a full day off.

I understand that longer hours would increase staffing costs.

However, the office could stay open late on one day and close earlier on another.

I would support a three-month trial and review the number of visitors before making a permanent decision.

Overall, limited extended hours seem both useful and realistic.
