After each strong first beat, second syllable light ആക്കൂ. Doctor-ൽ doc clear; tor quick. Office-ൽ off clear; ice quick. You are not deleting the second syllable; you are reducing its force.

But do not invent a rule that every two-syllable noun stresses first. Hotel is a familiar exception with second-syllable stress: ho-TEL. Police also has second stress: po-LICE. Machine: ma-CHINE. Exact word stress belongs to the word; spelling alone always predict ചെയ്യാൻ പറ്റില്ല.

ഇനി word family ending clues. Many words ending in tion or sion place main stress on the syllable immediately before that ending. Information: in-for-MA-tion. Education: ed-u-CA-tion. Decision: de-CI-sion. Discussion: dis-CUS-sion. Instruction: in-STRUC-tion.

This is a useful tendency, not permission to guess every unfamiliar word forever. For a new word, trusted audio pronunciation is best. Listen once, identify peak, imitate whole word, then use it.

Longer everyday words. University: u-ni-VER-si-ty. Electricity: e-lec-TRI-ci-ty. Opportunity: op-por-TU-ni-ty. These have weak syllables around one clear main stress. Do not make every piece equally heavy.

Word stress can sometimes distinguish two related words. A PRE-sent can mean a gift; to pre-SENT can mean to show or introduce formally. A RE-cord is a stored document; to re-CORD is the action. ഇനി stress production method. First, say all syllables slowly. Second, choose the known stressed syllable. Third, stretch and clarify that syllable slightly. Fourth, make the other syllables shorter. Fifth, say the whole word at natural speed.

Use hospital. Slow chunks: hos, pi, tal. Stress first: HOS. Light pi and tal. Whole word: hospital.

Use computer. Chunks: com, pu, ter. Stress middle: PU. Light com and ter. Whole word: computer.

Use information. Chunks: in, for, ma, tion. Stress ma. Light the others. Whole word: information.

Finished? Conceptual recap. A syllable is a spoken beat inside a word. A multisyllable English word usually has one main stress peak. That syllable tends to be longer, clearer, and stronger; unstressed syllables become shorter and softer. Malayalam-influenced speech may give every beat equal weight, so English words sound flat or the peak lands in the wrong place. Common first-stress words include doctor, college, hospital, family, morning. Common later-stress words include hotel, return, computer, important, information. Patterns help, but each word's heard pronunciation is the final guide.
