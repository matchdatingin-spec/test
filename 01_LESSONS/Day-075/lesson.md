# Day 75 — Reporting Real Conversations — reported questions, requests and instructions

നമസ്കാരം. ഇന്നലെ ഒരാൾ പറഞ്ഞ statements report ചെയ്തു. Real conversation-ൽ statements മാത്രം ഇല്ല. ആളുകൾ questions ചോദിക്കും, സഹായം request ചെയ്യും, instructions നൽകും. ഇന്ന് അവയെ മറ്റൊരാളോട് clear ആയി report ചെയ്യാം. Original conversation-ന്റെ purpose preserve ചെയ്യുകയാണ് goal: ചോദ്യം statement ആക്കരുത്, request harsh command ആക്കരുത്, instruction vague ആക്കരുത്.

ആദ്യം reported questions നോക്കാം. Direct question:

Where do you live?

Later report:

She asked me where I lived.

വലിയ change ശ്രദ്ധിച്ചോ? Direct question-ൽ do you live എന്ന question order ആണ്. Report-ൽ where I lived എന്ന statement order ആണ്. Subject ആദ്യം, verb പിന്നെ. Do അപ്രത്യക്ഷമായി.

She asked me where did I live എന്നല്ല.

She asked me where I lived.

ഇതാണ് Malayalam speakers-ന് വരുന്ന പ്രധാന correction.

Direct: What time does the shop open?

Reported: He asked what time the shop opened.

Does ഇല്ല. The shop subject ആദ്യം. Opened verb പിന്നെ.

Direct: Why did you leave early?

Reported: She asked me why I had left early.

Did ഇല്ല. Earlier action clear ചെയ്യാൻ had left വരുന്നു.

Direct wh-word report-ൽ stay ചെയ്യുന്നു: where, what, why, when, who, how.

Where are you going?

He asked me where I was going.

When will you return?

She asked me when I would return.

Why are they waiting?

He asked why they were waiting.

How can I contact you?

She asked how she could contact me.

Question mark voice report-ൽ ഇല്ല. He asked me where I was going എന്നത് whole sentence ആയി statement rhythm-ൽ വരുന്നു. നിങ്ങൾ information report ചെയ്യുകയാണ്; ആ question വീണ്ടും listener-നോട് ചോദിക്കുന്നില്ല.

ഇനി yes-or-no questions. Wh-word ഇല്ലെങ്കിൽ if അല്ലെങ്കിൽ whether ഉപയോഗിക്കാം.

Are you free?

She asked me if I was free.

Do you need help?

He asked whether I needed help.

Did they receive the message?

She asked if they had received the message.

Will you attend the meeting?

He asked me whether I would attend the meeting.

ഇവിടെ if എന്നത് conditional result ഉണ്ടാക്കുന്ന if അല്ല. “ആണോ എന്ന്” എന്ന reported-question link ആണ്.

She asked if I was free.

അവൾ ഞാൻ free ആണോ എന്ന് ചോദിച്ചു.

Whether അല്പം more formal ആയി കേൾക്കാം, പക്ഷേ രണ്ടും useful ആണ്.

ഇനി reported question pattern കേൾക്കൂ.

Asked plus person, optional wh-word or if or whether, then subject plus verb.

She asked me where I worked.

He asked us why we were late.

They asked if the room was available.

The manager asked whether I had finished the file.

Ask കഴിഞ്ഞ് person വരാം: asked me, asked her, asked the receptionist. Question content that കൊണ്ട് തുടങ്ങരുത്.

He asked me that where I lived എന്നല്ല.

He asked me where I lived.

She asked that was I ready എന്നല്ല.

She asked if I was ready.

Correction chain ചെയ്യാം.

He asked me where do I work.

Pause.

He asked me where I worked.

She asked why did we cancel the trip.

Pause.

She asked why we had cancelled the trip.

They asked me am I busy.

Pause.

They asked me if I was busy.

My friend asked that whether I could come.

Pause.

My friend asked whether I could come.

Good.

ഇപ്പോൾ requests. ഒരാൾ politely എന്തെങ്കിലും ചെയ്യാൻ ചോദിക്കുന്നു.

Please send me the address.

Report:

She asked me to send her the address.

Pattern:

Asked plus person plus to plus base verb.

He asked me to wait outside.

They asked us to arrive early.

My sister asked me to buy some milk.

Original sentence-ൽ please ഉണ്ടാകാം. Report-ൽ asked എന്ന verb തന്നെ request meaning നൽകുന്നു.

Could you open the window, please?

He asked me to open the window.

Would you call me after lunch?

She asked me to call her after lunch.

ഇവയെ reported questions ആയി treat ചെയ്യരുത്, കാരണം form question പോലെയാണെങ്കിലും function request ആണ്. Could you open the window? എന്നതിൽ speaker നിങ്ങളുടെ ability അറിയാൻ അല്ല ചോദിക്കുന്നത്; window തുറക്കാൻ request ചെയ്യുന്നു. അതിനാൽ:

He asked me to open the window.

He asked me if I could open the window എന്നും literal ability question context-ൽ possible ആണ്. പക്ഷേ polite request ആയിരുന്നെങ്കിൽ asked me to open ആണ് clear report.

Negative request:

Please do not share this number.

She asked me not to share that number.

Not സ്ഥാനം ശ്രദ്ധിക്കൂ.

Asked me not to share.

Asked me to not share എന്നും കേൾക്കാം, പക്ഷേ clean learning pattern not to ആണ്.

Please don’t wait for me.

He asked us not to wait for him.

ഇനി instructions. Teacher, manager, doctor, parent—ആർക്കെങ്കിലും ചെയ്യേണ്ട action clear ആയി പറയുന്നു.

Submit the form before Friday.

The manager told us to submit the form before Friday.

Turn off your phone.

The teacher told the students to turn off their phones.

Take this medicine after food.

The doctor told me to take that medicine after food.

Pattern:

Told plus person plus to plus base verb.

Request-ൽ asked someone to. Clear instruction-ൽ told someone to.

Tone preserve ചെയ്യണം.

My friend said, Please help me carry this box.

My friend asked me to help carry the box.

Manager says, Send the final file by five.

The manager told me to send the final file by five.

രണ്ടും to-form ആണ്, പക്ഷേ reporting verb relationship കാണിക്കുന്നു: asked for a request, told for an instruction.

Negative instruction:

Do not enter this room.

The guard told us not to enter that room.

Do not switch off the computer.

The technician told me not to switch off the computer.

Did not to enter എന്നല്ല. Not to enter.

Common correction:

The manager told to finish the work.

ആരോടാണ് പറഞ്ഞത് missing.

Pause.

The manager told us to finish the work.

She asked that me to wait.

Pause.

She asked me to wait.

He told me that open the door.

Pause.

He told me to open the door.

The doctor told me don’t eat before the test.

Pause.

The doctor told me not to eat before the test.

Excellent.

ഇപ്പോൾ ഒരേ original words context അനുസരിച്ച് എങ്ങനെ മാറുന്നു എന്ന് നോക്കാം.

Can you drive?

ഒരു job interview-ൽ ഇത് genuine ability question ആണ്.

The interviewer asked me if I could drive.

ഒരു friend injured ആയി car വീട്ടിലേക്ക് കൊണ്ടുപോകാൻ പറയുന്നു: Can you drive me home?

ഇത് request ആണ്.

My friend asked me to drive him home.

Words മാത്രം കേൾക്കാതെ function മനസ്സിലാക്കണം.

മറ്റൊരു pair.

Will you be at home tonight?

Information question:

She asked me whether I would be at home that night.

Will you bring the documents tonight?

Context-ൽ request ആണെങ്കിൽ:

She asked me to bring the documents that night.

Reported conversation natural ആക്കാൻ sequence വേണം. ഒരു reception conversation കേൾക്കൂ.

Receptionist: What is your name?

Visitor: My name is Ajay Nair.

Receptionist: Do you have an appointment?

Visitor: Yes, I have an appointment at ten thirty.

Receptionist: Please wait near the second door.

Later visitor reports:

The receptionist asked me what my name was, and I told her that my name was Ajay Nair. She asked whether I had an appointment. I said that I had an appointment at ten thirty. Then she asked me to wait near the second door.

ഇവിടെ question reports, statement reports, request report—all linked. Asked me what my name was; not what was my name. Asked whether I had; not whether did I have.

Pronouns വീണ്ടും check ചെയ്യണം. Mother says to Ravi:

Please call me when you arrive.

Ravi reports:

My mother asked me to call her when I arrived.

Original me refers to mother, so her. Original you refers to Ravi, so me or I depending position.

Manager says to Latha:

Can you send me your notes?

Latha reports:

My manager asked me to send him my notes.

Manager woman ആണെങ്കിൽ her. Real person-നനുസരിച്ച് pronoun choose ചെയ്യണം.

ഇനി mixed real-conversation practice. ഒരു colleague പറയുന്നു:

Are you attending the training tomorrow? Please bring your laptop. Do not share the access code with anyone.

Report next day.

Pause.

My colleague asked me whether I was attending the training the next day. She asked me to bring my laptop and told me not to share the access code with anyone.

Question first. Request second. Serious instruction third. Reporting verbs purpose reflect ചെയ്യുന്നു.

ഒരു clinic conversation:

Doctor: When did the pain begin?

Patient: It began last night.

Doctor: Take these tablets after food. Do not lift anything heavy for two days.

Patient later reports:

The doctor asked me when the pain had begun. I said that it had begun the night before. The doctor told me to take the tablets after food and not to lift anything heavy for two days.

Second negative instruction-ൽ told me repeat ചെയ്യാതെ and not to കൊണ്ട് join ചെയ്തു. Meaning clear ആണ്.

ഇപ്പോൾ independent reporting task. Imagine a short conversation at a hotel. Receptionist asks your name, asks whether you have a booking, requests you to show identification, and tells you to wait for five minutes. ആ conversation report ചെയ്യൂ.

Pause.

ഒരു model കേൾക്കൂ.

The receptionist asked me what my name was and whether I had a booking. Then she asked me to show my identification. After checking it, she told me to wait for five minutes.

ഇപ്പോൾ model ഇല്ലാതെ മറ്റൊരു situation. A supervisor asked where you had kept a file, asked whether the client had called, requested you to check your email, and instructed you not to leave before five. Complete report പറയൂ.

ഇപ്പോൾ one-minute challenge. നിങ്ങൾ ഇന്നലെ ഒരു office, clinic, shop, school, അല്ലെങ്കിൽ station-ൽ നടന്ന short conversation report ചെയ്യുകയാണ്. കുറഞ്ഞത് ഒരു wh-question, ഒരു yes-or-no question, ഒരു request, ഒരു instruction ഉൾപ്പെടുത്തൂ. Original dialogue act ചെയ്യേണ്ട; smooth report നൽകൂ. Person and time words adjust ചെയ്യൂ.

Pause.

ഇന്നത്തെ proof aloud.

I can report wh-questions with the wh-word followed by subject and verb. I can report yes-or-no questions with if or whether. I do not repeat do, does, or did inside a reported question. I can report requests with asked someone to plus a base verb. I can report instructions with told someone to plus a base verb. I can use not to for negative requests and instructions. I choose the reporting form from the speaker’s purpose, not only from the shape of the original sentence.
