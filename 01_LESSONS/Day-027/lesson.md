Another direct technique is substitution. One accurate sentence creates many new sentences. Base pattern: I am reading a message now. Change subject: She is reading a message now. Scene mapping is another method. Look at a room and assign English slots. Who or what? What action? Where? Now? Start with subject visible in your mind: A man. Choose live engine: is carrying. Add object: a box. Add place: near the door. Final: A man is carrying a box near the door.

For routine mapping: who? repeated action? time? My neighbour, walks, every morning. My neighbour walks every morning.

For fixed plan: who? arranged action? future anchor? We, meet the doctor, tomorrow at ten. We are meeting the doctor tomorrow at ten.

For existence: number? thing? location? Two, buses, near the station. There are two buses near the station.

Create six scene sentences by mapping, not translating. Two live actions, one routine, one fixed plan, one possession fact, and one there sentence.
