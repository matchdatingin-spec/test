Now intonation changes tag function. Falling tag often sounds like speaker expects agreement or invites friendly confirmation. Beautiful day, isn't it? Voice settles because speaker thinks yes.

Politeness does not require an extremely long sentence. Excuse me. Where is the washroom? is polite in many contexts. Could you tell me where the washroom is? gives a softer option. Choose for situation.

Also avoid double politeness overload. Kindly please could you tell me where the office is sounds heavy. One opening plus please if natural is enough. Excuse me. Could you tell me where the office is?
