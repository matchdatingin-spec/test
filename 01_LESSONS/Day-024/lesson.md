ആദ്യം meaning. ഒരു vague wish: someday visit Chennai. അത് fixed arrangement അല്ല. എന്നാൽ Friday morning train ticket booked, friend knows, departure time decided: I am travelling to Chennai on Friday morning. Sentence form same familiar engine ആണ്: subject plus am, is, or are plus verb-ing. Future meaning listener-ന് clear ആക്കാൻ time expression വളരെ important ആണ്. I am meeting my manager tomorrow. ഇവ പറയുന്ന നിമിഷം actions happening now അല്ല. Tomorrow, this evening, at seven on Saturday, next Monday എന്ന future time words camera-യെ മുന്നോട്ട് മാറ്റുന്നു. Grammar form same; time context future arrangement meaning നൽകുന്നു.

Compare only to locate today’s use. I am meeting my manager now means live action. I am meeting my manager tomorrow at eleven means fixed near-future plan. Same structure, different time anchor. Today we stay with the second meaning.

Fixed ആണോ എന്ന് test ചെയ്യാൻ നാല് practical checks ഉണ്ട്. Has a decision been made? Is there a clear near-future time? Is something arranged with a person, place, ticket, class, visit, or appointment? Would changing it require informing someone or changing a real arrangement? എല്ലാ sentence-നും four checks pass ചെയ്യണമെന്നില്ല, പക്ഷേ vague thought-നെക്കാൾ arrangement stronger ആയിരിക്കണം.

Meaning error next. “I am visiting London one day, maybe.” The phrase one day, maybe shows no fixed arrangement. Do not pretend it is scheduled. If you truly have a booked date, give that date: I am visiting London next month on the tenth. Today's form should carry a real arrangement meaning.

Fixed does not mean impossible to change. Plans can be cancelled. It means arrangement currently exists. We are meeting at five can later become We are not meeting today. Our meeting is not happening today. The negative tells listener current schedule status.

Conceptual recap. Present Continuous can describe a fixed near-future plan when an arrangement already exists. Form remains subject plus am, is, or are plus verb-ing. Future day or time makes meaning clear: I am meeting her tomorrow at ten. She is travelling on Friday. We are having dinner this weekend. Negative uses not after be. Confirmation question moves be to the front. A vague hope or someday wish is not today's target. A decision with a concrete arrangement is.
