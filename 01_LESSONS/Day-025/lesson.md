ഇന്ന് eight question words own ചെയ്യാം: what, where, when, who, why, how, which, whose. Word meaning മാത്രം പഠിച്ചാൽ മതി എന്നില്ല. Malayalam statement order 그대로 വെച്ച് Where he is going? എന്ന് ചോദിക്കരുത്. Correct order build ചെയ്യണം: Where is he going?

Question word ആദ്യം meaning gap കാണിക്കുന്നു. What asks for a thing, action, or information. Where asks for a place. When asks for a time. Who asks for a person. Why asks for a reason. How asks for manner or condition. Which asks for a choice from a limited set. Whose asks for an owner.

Say the meaning connection aloud. What — thing or information. Where — place. When — time. Who — person. Why — reason. How — manner or condition. Which — choice. Whose — owner.

എന്നാൽ question word കഴിഞ്ഞുള്ള engine sentence type അനുസരിച്ച് മാറും. നിങ്ങൾക്കറിയാവുന്ന മൂന്ന് routes മതി. Be main word ആണെങ്കിൽ be മുന്നിലേക്ക്. Action habit ആണെങ്കിൽ do or does. Present Continuous ആണെങ്കിൽ am, is, or are already helper ആണ്; അത് subject-ന് മുമ്പിൽ വരും.

Be route: She is at home. Place missing ആക്കുക. Where is she? They are busy. Reason missing ആക്കുക. Why are they busy? This is my bag. Owner missing ആക്കുക. Whose bag is this?

Present Simple action route: You work in Kochi. Place missing. Where do you work? She starts at nine. Time missing. When does she start? They walk because the shop is near. Reason missing. Why do they walk?

Present Continuous route: He is reading a newspaper. Thing missing. What is he reading? They are waiting near the gate. Place missing. Where are they waiting? She is leaving at six. Time missing. When is she leaving?

Core order voice-ൽ പറയൂ. Question word, helper or be, subject, main part. Where do you work? What is she reading? Why are they waiting? For many questions this frame will protect you from Malayalam word order.

Which choice-limited question ആണ്. Two or more visible or known options ഉണ്ടെങ്കിൽ which useful. Which shirt do you want, the blue one or the white one? Which bus goes to the hospital, number ten or number twelve? Which bag is yours? Which book is she reading?

What and which difference. What do you want? Open answer; many possibilities. Which drink do you want, tea or coffee? Limited choice. What book are you reading? asks information about the book. Which book are you reading, this one or that one? asks selection from known options.

Selection process slow ആയി പറയാം. First, what information is missing: thing, place, time, person, reason, method, choice, or owner? Choose the WH word. Second, inspect the remaining statement. Be sentence ആണോ? Present Simple action ആണോ? Present Continuous ആണോ? Third, put helper before subject. Fourth, after do or does use base verb. Fifth, keep ing after am, is, are in Continuous.

Question transformation practice. Statement: Leena works in a hospital. Place missing. Choose where. Present Simple one-person action, so does plus subject plus base work. Final: Where does Leena work?

Statement: The boys are playing in the garden. Place missing. Choose where. Continuous plural helper are. Final: Where are the boys playing?

Statement: This is Joseph's pen. Owner missing. Choose whose plus noun. Be question order. Final: Whose pen is this?

Statement: Sara is tired because she is working late. Reason missing. Choose why. Be sentence. Final: Why is Sara tired?
