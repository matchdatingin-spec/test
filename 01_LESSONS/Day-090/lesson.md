Challenge തുടങ്ങുന്നതിന് മുമ്പ് body and mind ready ആക്കാം. One normal breath. Voice comfortable volume. English meaning direct. Response തുടങ്ങാൻ “perfect first sentence” wait ചെയ്യരുത്. Simple line first, detail next.

ഈ three rescue lines ഒരിക്കൽ മാത്രം പറയൂ.

Listening rule. Final conversation ഉടൻ തുടങ്ങും. സാധ്യമെങ്കിൽ ഒരു five-minute timer start ചെയ്യൂ, പക്ഷേ timer set ചെയ്യാൻ speech plan ചെയ്യരുത്. Audio ഓരോ “Pause”ൽ stop ചെയ്ത് natural answer പറയാം. ഓരോ main answer twenty to thirty seconds. Short follow-up ten to twenty seconds. ഞാൻ model answers നൽകില്ല. ഇത് നിങ്ങളുടെ performance ആണ്.

Ready. Conversation begins now.

Hello. It’s good to speak with you today. How has your day been so far?

Pause.

Choose one detail from your answer. What made that part of the day easy, difficult, useful, or memorable?

Pause.

I’d like to know a little about your everyday life. What is one routine that helps you manage your time?

Pause.

Has that routine always worked well, or have you changed it recently? Tell me about one change and its result.

Pause.

Now think of a place in your area that you know well. What is the place like, and who usually uses it?

Pause.

If you could make one practical improvement to that place, what would you change, and why?

Pause.

Imagine someone says, “That change may cost too much and only a few people may use it.” How would you respond to that concern?

Pause.

Let’s move to a past experience. Tell me about a recent occasion when a plan did not go as expected. What happened?

Pause.

How did you respond, and what was the final result?

Pause.

Looking back, would you handle anything differently next time?

Pause.

Now listen to this short situation. A workshop you planned to attend tomorrow has been moved from ten in the morning to three in the afternoon. The venue is the same, but participants must confirm before six today. Three o’clock is difficult for you because you already have an appointment. What would you say when you call the organiser?

Pause.

The organiser replies, “We also have a place in Friday’s session at eleven. Would that work for you?” Respond, confirm the important details, and ask one useful question.

Pause.

Now I’ll share an opinion. I think adults often wait too long before speaking a new language because they want every sentence to be correct. I believe regular imperfect conversation is more useful than silent preparation. Do you agree, partly agree, or disagree? Explain your view.

Pause.

Give me one example from your own learning experience that supports your answer.

Pause.

Now ask me one question about learning or using English.

Pause.

Here is my answer, whatever exact question you chose: regular practice becomes useful when it has a clear purpose. Instead of trying to practise everything every day, I would choose one real task, such as explaining an experience, understanding a message, or holding a short conversation. What kind of real English task would be most valuable for you during the next month?

Pause.

Why is that task important in your life now?

Pause.

What is one obstacle that might stop you from practising, and what practical step can help you continue?

Pause.

We’re almost at the end. Please summarise the main thing you can do in English now that was difficult for you at the beginning of this course.

Pause.

And finally, complete the conversation with one clear commitment for your next ninety days.

Pause.

Thank you. That completes the five-minute unprepared conversation.
