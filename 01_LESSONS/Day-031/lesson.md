Final recap. Irregular verbs do not use the normal ed rule. Learn them as three-form chunks. V1 is base, V2 is past, V3 is the third form. Completed past positive uses V2: went, saw, took, gave, wrote, ate. V3 forms gone, seen, taken, given, written, eaten are learned now because they matter later, but their later sentence system is not today's topic. Some verbs have three different forms, some share V2 and V3, and some never change.
