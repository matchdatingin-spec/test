# Day 82 — Interviews and Professional Self-Introduction

നമസ്കാരം. ഇന്ന് professional situation-ൽ നിങ്ങളെ clear, relevant, believable ആയി introduce ചെയ്യാനും interview questions answer ചെയ്യാനും practice ചെയ്യാം. Interview English എന്നത് memorised big words അല്ല. Listener ചോദിച്ച question മനസ്സിലാക്കി, useful evidence select ചെയ്ത്, complete but concise answer നൽകുകയാണ്.

ഒരു weak self-introduction കേൾക്കൂ.

Myself Arun. I am coming from Kerala. I have completed my degree. My hobbies are reading books and listening music. That’s all about me.

ഇത് വളരെ common pattern ആണ്. പക്ഷേ natural professional introduction അല്ല. Correct and useful version:

My name is Arun, and I am based in Kerala. I recently completed a degree in commerce. During my final year, I worked on a small project that involved organising survey data and presenting the results. I enjoy work that requires accuracy and clear communication, and I am now looking for an entry-level role where I can develop those skills further.

“Myself Arun” എന്നല്ല. “My name is Arun” അല്ലെങ്കിൽ “I’m Arun.” “I am coming from” current movement പോലെ sound ചെയ്യും. Home location-ന് I’m from, current base-ന് I’m based in. Listening music എന്നല്ല, listening to music. പക്ഷേ professional intro-ൽ hobbies relevant അല്ലെങ്കിൽ first priority അല്ല.

Self-introduction autobiography അല്ല. Interviewer-ന് ഇപ്പോൾ relevant ആയ professional picture വേണം. Present position, useful past experience, key strength with evidence, and current direction. ഈ flow beginner-നും experienced person-നും work ചെയ്യും.

Present position examples:

I am a customer support assistant with two years of experience in retail services.

I recently completed a diploma in electrical engineering.

I currently manage orders and customer records for a small distribution company.

I am returning to work after a career break and have recently updated my computer and accounting skills.

Truthful context choose ചെയ്യൂ. Job title ഇല്ലെങ്കിൽ skill and activity പറയാം.

I help with my family’s business, where I handle customer calls and daily accounts.

I have been volunteering at a local reading programme, mainly organising sessions and communicating with parents.

Unpaid experience also experience ആണ്, if relevant and honestly framed.

Past evidence sentence:

In my current role, I introduced a simple tracking sheet that reduced missed follow-ups.

During my course, I coordinated a four-person project and completed it before the deadline.

Over the last year, I have become comfortable speaking with customers and resolving basic service problems.

Claim plus evidence. “I am hardworking, sincere, punctual, dedicated” എന്ന adjective chain interviewer പലരിൽ നിന്നും കേൾക്കും. Example differentiates you.

Current direction:

I am now looking for a role where I can combine customer service with stronger administrative responsibilities.

I would like to begin in a practical technical role and learn from an experienced team.

This position interests me because it involves both careful data work and communication with clients.

Direction company-നെ praise ചെയ്യുന്ന empty sentence ആവരുത്. Fit explain ചെയ്യണം.

ഇനി “Tell me about yourself.” ഇതിന് self-introduction answer തന്നെയാണ്, പക്ഷേ job context match ചെയ്യണം. One minute around enough for most initial answers. Very short answer interviewer-ന് material കൊടുക്കില്ല. Five-minute life story focus നഷ്ടപ്പെടുത്തും.

ഒരു experienced candidate model:

I am a store supervisor with five years of experience in grocery retail. I began as a sales assistant and gradually took responsibility for stock checks, shift schedules, and customer complaints. In my current role, I supervise eight staff members and prepare the daily closing report. One of my strengths is noticing small process problems early; for example, I changed the shelf-check routine after we had repeated pricing errors. I am interested in this role because it offers a larger operation and more responsibility for staff training.

Chronology selective ആണ്. Every employer name വേണ്ട. Growth, current responsibilities, evidence, motivation.

ഇനി question answering-ന്റെ simple principle: answer first, then support. Malayalam-ൽ context നീളത്തിൽ പറഞ്ഞിട്ട് answer അവസാനം വരാം. Interview-ൽ direct answer first helpful ആണ്.

Question: What is one of your strengths?

Weak answer:

There are many situations in life, and from childhood I have always believed that time is valuable.

Direct answer:

One of my strengths is organising work clearly. In my current role, I prepare a daily priority list and confirm urgent orders before noon. This has helped me avoid missed deadlines during busy weeks.

Strength, evidence, result.

Strength choose ചെയ്യുമ്പോൾ role-relevant and true ആയിരിക്കണം: clear communication, patience, accuracy, planning, learning quickly, solving practical problems, staying calm, teamwork. “My strength is my family” personal truth ആകാം, പക്ഷേ interviewer usually asks professional strength.

Practice. Strength: patient communication. Evidence: explained app to older customers. Result: fewer repeat calls.

Answer പറയൂ.

Pause.

One of my strengths is patient communication. In my previous role, I often helped older customers use our mobile app. I explained one step at a time and asked them to try each step themselves. As a result, many customers could use the basic features without calling again.

“I am a quick learner” evidence ഇല്ലാതെ cliché. Better:

I learn new systems quickly. When our office introduced new billing software, I practised with sample entries after the initial training and was able to handle regular bills independently within a week.

ഇനി “What is a weakness?” Trap question പോലെ പേടിക്കേണ്ട. Fake strength പറയരുത്: I am a perfectionist, I work too hard. Serious role failure choose ചെയ്യേണ്ട. Real manageable area, effect, action, progress.

Earlier, I found it difficult to speak in large meetings, so I sometimes waited too long before sharing an idea. Over the last six months, I have started preparing one or two points before each meeting and contributing early. I am still improving, but I am much more comfortable now.

Honest, but helpless അല്ല. Improvement evidence ഉണ്ട്.

Another version:

I used to spend too much time checking routine emails. I now use a short checklist and set a time limit for the final review. That has helped me work faster without reducing accuracy.

“Used to” ഇവിടെ known past habit expression as course scope allows; form lesson അല്ല.

Your weakness answer choose ചെയ്യൂ. Area, previous effect, action now, progress. Thirty seconds.

Pause.

ഇനി experience or challenge question. “Tell me about a time when you handled a difficult situation.” Day 79 storytelling skill professional answer-ൽ compress ചെയ്യാം. Situation, responsibility, action, result. Long background വേണ്ട.

ഒരു model:

During a busy evening at the clinic, our appointment system stopped working. I was responsible for the front desk, and about fifteen patients were waiting. I wrote down each arrival time, checked urgent cases with the nurse, and explained the delay calmly to the waiting patients. I also called technical support and kept the doctor updated. The system returned after forty minutes, and we entered the handwritten records later. No appointment was lost, and the waiting area remained calm.

Situation clear. Responsibility clear. Actions specific. Result measurable enough.

“I handled everything successfully” evidence ഇല്ല. എന്ത് actions ചെയ്തു എന്ന് പറയണം.

Pause.

ഒരു possible version:

I was coordinating the group, so I divided that person’s section among the remaining three members and updated the slides. We practised the transitions twice that evening. The next day, we completed the presentation on time, and the teacher specifically praised the clear flow between speakers.

Professional story one to two minutes മതി. Day 79 full incident പോലെ sensory detail ആവശ്യമില്ല. Job evidence ആണ് focus.

ഇനി “Why do you want this role?” Weak answer:

Because your company is a very reputed company and I need a job.

Need honest ആയാലും interviewer fit ചോദിക്കുന്നു. Better answer role feature, your matching evidence, growth direction connect ചെയ്യും.

Company research facts ഉണ്ടെങ്കിൽ accurate മാത്രം പറയൂ. Invent ചെയ്യരുത്.

ഇനി “Why should we hire you?” Boast അല്ല; match summary.

“Where do you see yourself?” Wild promise വേണ്ട. Learning plus contribution realistic ആയി.
