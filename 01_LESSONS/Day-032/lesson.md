Positive: V2. Question after did: V1. Negative after didn't: V1.

Yes-or-no question form: Did first, then subject, then V1, then the rest. Did you call me? Did he arrive early? Did they buy the tickets? Did Maya take the bus?

Notice all subjects use did. Did I, did you, did he, did she, did it, did we, did they. Present questions-ൽ do and does subject അനുസരിച്ച് മാറിയിരുന്നു. Past question-ൽ എല്ലാവർക്കും did.
