# Production Warnings

Concise notes on issues encountered during generation. Not an audit. The Master Syllabus was not modified.

| Day | Type | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 012 | Ambiguous day boundary | V2 D12 owns the ഉണ്ട് contrast (`there is` vs `have`), but `have/has` is owned by D19. Resolved in production by teaching only the *recognition* side on D12 ("this Malayalam word becomes two different English structures") and leaving all `have/has` form work — forms, negatives, questions — to D19. D12 uses `have` in three example sentences only. Flagging because a future regeneration of D12 could easily over-teach `have`. |
| 002 | Prerequisite (by design) | D2 teaches action sentences with I/You/We/They. Learners will immediately want negatives and questions for action verbs, which require do/don't (D17–D18) — a 15-day wait. Handled in production by scoping D2 to positive statements and telling the learner explicitly that questions and negatives for action verbs are coming. No syllabus change needed; noting it because the gap is visible to learners. |
| 003 | Absorbed content | V2 folds adjectives into D3 (per the V2 change log, item 2). D3 therefore carries be + noun *and* be + adjective. Load is acceptable but D3 is the densest day in Week 1. |
